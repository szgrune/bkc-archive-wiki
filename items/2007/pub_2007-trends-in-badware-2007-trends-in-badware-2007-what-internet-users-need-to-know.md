# Trends in Badware 2007: What Internet Users Need to Know

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2007/Trends_In_Badware_2007) · **Published:** 2007-10-01 · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> "Trends in Badware 2007: What Internet Users Need to Know" is StopBadware's 2007 update on the state of badware on the web. The short report is a plain-English
> explanation of badware threats to user privacy and security, based on our research in 2007.
> It explains online security issues such as legitimate websites that have been hacked to distribute badware,
> social networking scams, and other badware trends that pose significant risk to the average internet user.
