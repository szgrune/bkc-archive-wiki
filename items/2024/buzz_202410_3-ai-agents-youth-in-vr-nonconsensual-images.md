** Controlling AI agents (https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/lawfare-daily--jonathan-zittrain-on-controlling-ai-agents)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jonathan Zittrain joins the Lawfare Daily (https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/lawfare-daily--jonathan-zittrain-on-controlling-ai-agents) podast to discuss his recent essay on the safety risks of "AIs that act independently on behalf of humans." "This routinization of AI that doesn’t simply talk with us, but also acts out in the world," he wrote (https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2024/07/ai-agents-safety-risks/678864/) , "is a crossing of the blood-brain barrier between digital and analog, bits and atoms. That should give us pause."

** Youth risk in virtual reality (https://cyberbullying.org/metaverse-risks-and-harms-among-us-youth-experiences-gender-differences-and-prevention-and-response-measures)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sameer Hinduja and Justin Patchin explore teenagers' use of virtual reality and the particular harms that they encounter, especially along gender lines. "There remains so much promise with these new technologies, but vigilance is required when it comes to the unique challenges they present, as well as the unique vulnerabilities that certain youth users may have."

https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12138v1

** Reporting abuse on X (https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12138v1)
------------------------------------------------------------

With a team of coauthors, Eric Gilbert uses AI-generated nude images to test X's takedown removal rates for nonconsensual images. "All 25 reports submitted under the DMCA resulted in the successful removal of the [non-consensual intimate media] content. In contrast, none of the 25 reports made under X’s non-consensual nudity policy led to the removal of the images within the three-week observation period."

https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/a-financial-primer-on-a-new-international-ai-organization

** Fragmented funding (https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/a-financial-primer-on-a-new-international-ai-organization)
------------------------------------------------------------

Cyberlaw Clinic student Justin Curl estimates the startup cost of creating an AI International Organization (IO) and compares different funding structures in a competitive marketplace. "It’s clear that without a solid funding plan that can support its success, a new AI IO will merely fragment the international ecosystem and dilute funding for AI-related organizations."
