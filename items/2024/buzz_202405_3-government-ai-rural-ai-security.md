** Public sector AI (https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14660)
------------------------------------------------------------

Tom Zick, Mason Kortz, David Eaves, and Finale Doshi-Velez look to jurisdictions with mature AI regulations to help inform how the US should approach the adoption of AI in the public sector. "While simple to articulate, promoting ethical and effective roll outs of AI systems in government is a notoriously elusive task."

https://www.techpolicy.press/what-were-talking-about-when-we-talk-about-rural-ai/

** Rural AI (https://www.techpolicy.press/what-were-talking-about-when-we-talk-about-rural-ai/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jasmine McNealy explains what is meant by "rural communities" when discussing AI and remote areas. "When thinking about the artificial intelligence conversations that are being had, especially with regards to what policy needs to do ... many of those conversations leave out geography as a perhaps exacerbating or amplifying factor in outcomes."

https://www.theregreview.org/2024/05/06/de-silva-de-alwis-vialle-is-ai-facilitated-gender-based-violence-the-next-pandemic/

** Gender-based violence (https://www.theregreview.org/2024/05/06/de-silva-de-alwis-vialle-is-ai-facilitated-gender-based-violence-the-next-pandemic/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Rangita de Silva de Alwis and Elodie Vialle warn that the rise of deepfakes and other AI-generated misinformation threatens women's freedom. "This rise of online gender-based violence is deeply intertwined with the rise of digital authoritarianism. Authoritarian anti-democratic states can employ anti-feminist narratives and policy measures to justify the oppression of marginalized groups."

https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/llms-data-control-path-insecurity/

** Vulnerable AI (https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/llms-data-control-path-insecurity/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Bruce Schneier shows that a key feature of generative AI—self-modification based on input data—introduces vulnerabilities, and as a result, we need to think carefully about whether such tools should be implemented in a given context. “We need to take a more nuanced view of AI systems, their uses, their own particular risks, and their costs vs. benefits.”
