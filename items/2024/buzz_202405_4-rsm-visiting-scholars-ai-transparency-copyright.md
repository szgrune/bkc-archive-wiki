** CLeAR Documentation (https://shorensteincenter.org/clear-documentation-framework-ai-transparency-recommendations-practitioners-context-policymakers/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a new report co-authored by experts across academia, industry, and civil society, Kasia Chmielinski, Sarah Newman, Chris Kranzinger, and their co-authors introduce the CLeAR (Comparable, Legible, Actionable, and Robust) Documentation Framework to offer guiding principles for AI documentation. Such documentation “provides information to users and potentially affected communities that can be used to determine the appropriateness of an AI system or its underlying data or models, thus helping inform consumer choice, advocacy work, regulation development, and regulation enforcement.”

------------------------------------------------------------

Ana Andrijevic explains the interaction between copyright law and transparency around the training sets for generative AI tools.
