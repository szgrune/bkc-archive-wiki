** Radio waves revealed (https://invisiblewaves.org)
------------------------------------------------------------

metaLAB’s mesmerizing WAVES (https://invisiblewaves.org) installation reveals the invisible architecture of our wireless world: WiFi, cellular, satellite, and more. Abstract radio waves are transformed into tangible art, letting visitors see how data flows through our spaces. "We're literally swimming in a sea of information," says Sarah Newman who, with a team of BKC summer interns, brought the project to life.

https://therokoreport.beehiiv.com/p/seo-for-ai

** SEO for AI (https://therokoreport.beehiiv.com/p/seo-for-ai)
------------------------------------------------------------

SEO wizards are quietly reshaping AI's digital playground, warns Sean Harvey. "When SEO professionals found out about AI chatbots, their first response was euphoria. Now they could do their job with less effort, and at 100x the scale! Then someone figured out that AI is about to replace search. The pie is going to shrink, and their standard bag of tricks will soon be useless. The risk for big brand advertisers is made even bigger by an impending shrinkage in social media use. And so the stage is set."

https://fortune.com/2024/11/14/x-elon-musk-leaving-election-trump-threads-bluesky-social-media-fragmentation/

** X-it strategies (https://fortune.com/2024/11/14/x-elon-musk-leaving-election-trump-threads-bluesky-social-media-fragmentation/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In Fortune, Jonathan Bellack comments on social media fragmentation and the recent exodus from X. “And yes, we’re seeing more people doing it more loudly at the moment, but people have been leaving for months and months. Now, I wonder if people are choosing to leave because of their beliefs, or because the experience on the platform has gotten worse.”

** BKC happenings
------------------------------------------------------------

In October, an M-Lab Community Call (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sq-v5GfYInQ) brought together Fellows Jim Cowie (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jim-cowie) and Maria Xynou (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/maria-xynou) and others to discuss the complexities of large-scale internet performance datasets. The panel explored challenges in measuring the internet, the value of historical datasets, blind spots, and sustaining the ecosystem.
