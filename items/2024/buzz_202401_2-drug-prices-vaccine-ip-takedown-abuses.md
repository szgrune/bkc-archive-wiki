** Toward affordability (https://hls.harvard.edu/today/ip-expert-ruth-okediji-discusses-biden-administrations-march-in-proposal-to-target-high-drug-prices/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Ruth Okediji discusses the Biden administration's recent proposal to use federal "march-in" rights to lower drug costs. “This nod by the Biden administration to the serious challenge of access to medicines may contribute to the eventual resolution of a long-standing national problem.”

Plus, listen (https://www.npr.org/2024/01/01/1221606624/mickey-mouse-public-domain-disney) to Okediji (https://www.npr.org/2024/01/01/1221606624/mickey-mouse-public-domain-disney) discuss "Steamboat Willy" and protection of the public domain.

https://journals.law.harvard.edu/ilj/2023/11/intellectual-property-and-the-lost-year-of-covid-19-deaths/

** IP in a health crisis (https://journals.law.harvard.edu/ilj/2023/11/intellectual-property-and-the-lost-year-of-covid-19-deaths/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Current BKC fellow Madhavi Sunder and Haochen Sun explain how Intellectual Property protections impacted the COVID-19 pandemic and the development, availability, and distribution of the COVID vaccines. "The tradeoff — breakthrough innovation in exchange for monopoly rights that raise prices and keep critical know-how under lock and key — does not work in pandemic times."

https://www.lumendatabase.org/media_mentions/search

** Investigating with Lumen (https://www.lumendatabase.org/media_mentions/search)
------------------------------------------------------------

Discussing bogus DMCA notices (https://torrentfreak.com/all-dmca-notices-filed-against-torrentfreak-in-2023-were-bogus-240101/) filed against them in 2023, TorrentFreak highlights Lumen Database as a key resource. And in a writeup of a wild Project Brazen investigation, Lumen also recently received a shoutout apropos the all-too-common "backdated article" technique (https://www.samaa.tv/208737270-conman-gaurav-srivastava-caught-lying-to-google-over-corruption-expose) whereby fraudsters request that actually original articles be taken down for infringement.

https://www.wired.com/story/ai-needs-to-be-both-trusted-and-trustworthy/

** Trusting advanced AI (https://www.wired.com/story/ai-needs-to-be-both-trusted-and-trustworthy/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Bruce Schneier explains the need for a rethinking of trust and governance to prepare for the future of AI. "If massive, decentralized AIs run everything, then who controls those AIs matters a lot. ... An AI that is both trusted and trustworthy will become a critical requirement."

Bulelani Jili and Matreya Shaw.
