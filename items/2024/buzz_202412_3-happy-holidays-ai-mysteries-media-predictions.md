** Build-A-Lawyer (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jels.12396)
------------------------------------------------------------

Faculty Associate Aileen Nielsen and coauthors explore human-machine cooperation in legal practice, running an experiment "on the causal impact of machine assistance on the efficiency of legal task completion in a private law setting." "Our results show that AI support can dramatically increase the efficiency of legal task completion." Read here » (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jels.12396)

------------------------------------------------------------

With a group of coauthors, RSM Visiting Scholar Marshall Van Alstyne asks when AI is the right tool for the job. "Data is the most crucial input to any AI model, but too often the size of the data receives much more attention than its quality." The group explores approaches to assessing data quality and other challenges that organizations face when leveraging AI. Read here » (https://hbr.org/2024/12/is-ai-the-right-tool-to-solve-that-problem)
