** Adjudicating social media (https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/free-speech-vs-free-ride-navigating-the-supreme-courts-social-media-paradox/)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Supreme Court must begin treating social media platforms consistently, argues Marshall Van Alstyne. "Social media firms want the best of both [worlds]. They want publishers’ freedom to exclude users and control over editorial decisions but common carrier protection from user content liability."

Plus, next week, Marshall Van Alstyne joins the Rebooting Social Media Speaker Series for a talk about Combating Misinformation Through Digital Markets (https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/events/combating-misinformation-through-digital-markets-rsm-speaker-series/) . Join us!

** Youth fight back (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0160791X24002896?via%3Dihub)
------------------------------------------------------------

Gregory Gondwe explores the sophisticated digital activism of Zambian youth as manifested in the nation's 2020 protests. "Through coordinated efforts, peer-to-peer file sharing, and the involvement of influencers and the diaspora," he writes, "Zambian youth have managed to amplify their voices and sustain their movements despite government crackdowns."

https://english.elpais.com/international/women-leaders-of-latin-america/2024-10-19/paola-ricaurte-researcher-large-technology-companies-are-allies-of-authoritarian-governments.html

** Equalizing AI (https://english.elpais.com/international/women-leaders-of-latin-america/2024-10-19/paola-ricaurte-researcher-large-technology-companies-are-allies-of-authoritarian-governments.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

"There are very few leaders who are controlling the [AI] debate and they do it in their favor, so that these technologies are quickly appropriated and governments and citizens continue to be clients." AI development tracks and reinforces gaps in social capital, contends Paola Ricaurte, advocating for the defense of rights in the Global South.

https://prospect.org/power/2024-10-18-sec-whistleblower-payouts-ai/

** Whistleblower payouts (https://prospect.org/power/2024-10-18-sec-whistleblower-payouts-ai/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Bruce Schneier and Nathan Sanders warn that AI will complicate the Securities and Exchange Commission's payouts to whistleblowers. "While AI automation will also help SEC staff process tips more efficiently, it could exponentially increase the number of tips the agency has to deal with, further decreasing the efficiency of the [whistleblower] program."
