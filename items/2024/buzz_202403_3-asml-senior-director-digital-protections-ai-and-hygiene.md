** AI iatrogenesis (https://time.com/6957890/next-tech-backlash-hygiene/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jonnie Penn argues that the increased incorporation of AI into our lives may harm our basic hygiene, that is “the conditions or practices conducive to maintaining health and preventing disease.” "Digital tools have become iatrogenic: they harm us as we 'use' them to try and improve our lives."
