** AI in political ads (https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2024/09/ai-election-ads-regulation/680010/)
------------------------------------------------------------

“There has been some movement toward constraining the use of AI [in political advertising], yet progress has been painstakingly slow in the area where it may count most: the 2024 election,” write Nathan Sanders and Bruce Schneier. “The reasons for concern are obvious—the technology makes it easier for bad actors to construct highly persuasive and misleading content.”

https://tsjournal.org/index.php/jots/article/view/206

** Algorithmic Impact Assessments (https://tsjournal.org/index.php/jots/article/view/206)
------------------------------------------------------------

What challenges do practitioners face when implementing Algorithmic Impact Assessments? Amar Ashar and coauthors identify concerns and offer suggestions for mitigation. "Our research underscores the importance of framing AIAs not as checklists...but as dynamic tools that evolve in response to the unique challenges and needs of the practitioners who interact with them."

https://connectedbydata.org/resources/global-deliberation-ai

** G (https://connectedbydata.org/resources/global-deliberation-ai) lobal citizen assemblies (https://connectedbydata.org/resources/global-deliberation-ai)
------------------------------------------------------------

Tim Davies and coauthors ask how global citizen assemblies might shape the future of AI. "Although numerous sets of principles and ethical codes for AI exist, few have been developed through truly global and inclusive dialogue, taking into account perspectives from a full range of geographic, cultural, religious, political and socio-economic backgrounds."

https://www.citationneeded.news/hachette-v-internet-archive/

** C (https://ooni.org/post/2024-kazakhstan-report/) ensoring speech online (https://ooni.org/post/2024-kazakhstan-report/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Maria Xynou and colleagues examine internet interference and censorship in Kazakhstan, seeking to reveal which news media, human rights, political, and circumvention tool sites are blocked in the country, and how such blocks are implemented. "Answering such questions can help with evaluating whether and to what extent internet users in Kazakhstan experience restrictions."
