# Post-Truth, Fake News and Democracy: Mapping the Politics of Falsehood

**Source:** [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z1IVDPlZYsM) · **Published:** 2024-12-13 · **By:** Berkman Klein Center · **Feed tags:** bkc-video

> How can we effectively talk about truth? How are misinformation campaigns being addressed across the globe?
> 
> The Institute for Rebooting Social Media welcomes Johan Farkas to discuss contemporary political struggles over how we define what’s true and what’s false, based on the 2nd edition of Post-Truth, Fake News and Democracy: Mapping the Politics of Falsehood. The explores how fake news has impacted recent historic events, including the COVID-19 pandemic, and presents both a critique of prominent current solutions to the spread of disinformation as well as potential democratic alternatives.
