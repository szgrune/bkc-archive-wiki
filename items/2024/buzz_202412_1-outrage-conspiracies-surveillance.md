** Anger goes algorithmic (https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adl2829)
------------------------------------------------------------

Kate Klonick and coauthors reveal how outrage fuels the spread of misinformation online. "Outrage acts as a catalyst, propelling misinformation across platforms by tapping into users' emotional responses, which algorithms then amplify," they write, highlighting how anger and algorithms drive viral falsehoods. ------------------------------------------------------------

AI shaped the 2024 elections, for better or worse. Campaigns used it for "language translation" and AI chatbots, while "AI-created emails, texts, audio and video" played key roles. Yet, as Bruce Schneier and Nathan Sanders warn, "there was AI-created misinformation and propaganda, even though it was not as catastrophic as feared."
