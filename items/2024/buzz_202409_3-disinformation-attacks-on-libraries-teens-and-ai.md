** Freedom to read (https://www.citationneeded.news/hachette-v-internet-archive/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Molly White links the recent Second Circuit decision in Hachette v. Internet Archive to a larger pattern of publishers interfering with libraries and, more generally, with reading practices. "Readers should be able to read without any third parties spying over their shoulders, or preventing them from accessing the materials they have legally obtained."

** AI in homes and schools (https://www.commonsensemedia.org/research/the-dawn-of-the-ai-era-teens-parents-and-the-adoption-of-generative-ai-at-home-and-school)
------------------------------------------------------------

Mary Madden, Angela Calvin, Alexa Hasse, and Amanda Lenhart illustrate the roles of rapidly-evolving generative AI software in teens' and caregivers' lives. "Given that youth seek support from trusted adults in navigating the pitfalls and promises of gen AI, it is critical to support conversations at home and school about the role these systems could play in teens' future."
