** Matters of size (https://www.wired.com/story/social-media-is-getting-smaller-and-more-treacherous/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Ethan Zuckerman asserts that there are important downsides to the increasing move towards smaller online communities. "Small rooms have a big downside: They’re as useful for Nazis as they are for knitters. These conversations, insulated from outside scrutiny, can normalize extreme points of view and lead people deeper into dark topics they expressed a passing interest in."

https://cyberscoop.com/cfpbs-data-rules-security-privacy-competition/

** Bye-bye, data brokers (https://cyberscoop.com/cfpbs-data-rules-security-privacy-competition/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Bruce Schneier and Barath Raghavan argue that, if adopted, the CFPB's proposed data rules would promote greater competition and increase consumer privacy and security. "By giving the public greater control over their banking data, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's proposal would deal a blow to data brokers."

https://cdt.org/insights/from-automation-to-agency-the-future-of-ai-ethics-education/

** AI ethics education (https://cdt.org/insights/from-automation-to-agency-the-future-of-ai-ethics-education/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Ashley Lee and Victoria Hsieh discuss the future of ethics as a component of education on AI. "AI ethics education can play a significant role in empowering students to collectively reimagine AI practices and processes, and contribute to a cultural transformation that prioritizes ethical and responsible AI."

https://open.spotify.com/episode/4L2jrmJO1dVEJ02KzKSjln

** Culture, community, creatives (https://open.spotify.com/episode/4L2jrmJO1dVEJ02KzKSjln)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jabari Evans welcomes David Craig to his podcast to discuss the implications of race, class, and gender in the burgeoning field of creator studies within the discipline of communication studies. “Hip hop culture is much broader than rap music, one of the things [it was originally about] was community.”
