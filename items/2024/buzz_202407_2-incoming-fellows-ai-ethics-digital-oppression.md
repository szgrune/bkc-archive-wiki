** Ethical future (https://www.chathamhouse.org/2024/06/artificial-intelligence-and-challenge-global-governance/08-ethics-framework-ai-generated)
------------------------------------------------------------

Micaela Mantegna contributes to a new Chatham House essay collection on how to create an ethical AI ecosystem. "Public institutions are particularly suggestible to the promises of modernization, security, and efficiency. AI systems are already being deployed in areas like justice and surveillance without proper safeguards, assessments, or possibilities for recourse in the event of error."

https://www.article19.org/queer-resistance-to-digital-oppression/

** Resisting digital oppression (https://www.article19.org/queer-resistance-to-digital-oppression/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In collaboration with The De|Center and local experts, ARTICLE 19 released a series of three reports that show how queer communities in the Middle East and North Africa are fighting for better tech futures. "If we protect the most criminalised and marginalised communities, we will protect everyone," writes principal researcher Afsaneh Rigot.
