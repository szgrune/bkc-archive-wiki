** Moscow's media muzzle (https://ooni.org/post/2024-russia-report/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Maria Xynoo and the Open Observatory of Network Interference (OONI) team, with RKS Global, analyze the suppression of independent news in Russia. “Nearly three years ago, when Russia launched its military operation in Ukraine, it also initiated a parallel campaign: a censorship war targeting independent news media within the country.” Read here » (https://ooni.org/post/2024-russia-report/)

** Elite imaginings (https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/medialse/2024/12/06/the-elite-contradictions-of-generative-ai/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Asher Kessler and Nick Couldry wonder “why, in spite of AI’s extraordinary powers, does it give us a future in which governing through democracy...seem less possible than us living until the age of 160?” They explore how Silicon Valley leaders imagine a future in which the advantages of AI are available to a biologically advantaged few. ** Not set in silicon (https://www.bostonreview.net/forum_response/trust-issues/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Reflecting on the past and future of AI, Bruce Schneier and Nathan Sanders take the position that “AI is better understood as a creative, global field of human endeavor that has been largely captured by U.S. venture capitalists, private equity, and Big Tech. But that was never the inevitable outcome, and it doesn’t need to stay that way.” Read here » (https://www.bostonreview.net/forum_response/trust-issues/)
