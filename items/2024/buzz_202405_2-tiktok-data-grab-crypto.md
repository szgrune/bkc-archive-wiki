** TikTok, TikTok (https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/2024/05/07/tiktok-divest-or-ban-qa-experts-forecast-legal-creative-impact/)
------------------------------------------------------------

A bill signed into law last month puts TikTok at risk of being banned in the US unless ByteDance divests to a new owner. The Institute for Rebooting Social Media spoke with legal scholars and industry experts—including RSM Visiting Scholars Anupam Chander, David Craig, and Jabari Evans—to analyze the bill’s implications and what it could mean for the future of social media. Read the Q&A here (https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/2024/05/07/tiktok-divest-or-ban-qa-experts-forecast-legal-creative-impact/) .

Plus, Anupam Chander provides an early analysis (https://medium.com/@AnupamChander/some-quick-thoughts-on-tiktok-v-garland-761a3a156661) of the lawsuit TikTok filed to stop the ban.

https://www.nytimes.com/2024/05/05/opinion/facebook-court-internet-meta.html

** Middleware & Meta (https://www.nytimes.com/2024/05/05/opinion/facebook-court-internet-meta.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

Ethan Zuckerman explains why he is seeking, with the Knight First Amendment Institute, a court ruling protecting the use of middleware apps that give users more agency over what they see on Facebook. "Giving users more control is important to establishing more of an equilibrium in an online world that is increasingly out of kilter."

https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/inequalities/2024/05/01/todays-colonial-data-grab-is-deepening-global-inequalities/

** Data colonialism (https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/inequalities/2024/05/01/todays-colonial-data-grab-is-deepening-global-inequalities/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Nick Couldry and Ulises A. Mejias argue that we are seeing data grabs that deepen global inequalities. "Over the past decade numerous scandals have become associated with social media platforms, scandals that are still largely unresolved...But those scandals are merely symptoms of a much wider inequality of power over how data is extracted, stored, processed, and applied."

https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2024-05-02/crypto-is-anything-but-strong-right-now

** Crypto's wild (https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2024-05-02/crypto-is-anything-but-strong-right-now)
------------------------------------------------------------

Molly White corrects the narrative that crypto's wild days are over, arguing that the state of crypto is still shaky. "Market manipulation is rampant on the hundreds of exchanges where crypto trades hands. Many in the sector seem to have accepted wash trading—self-dealing that makes the market look busier than it is—as a built-in."
