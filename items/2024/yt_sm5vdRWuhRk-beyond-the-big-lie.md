# Beyond the Big Lie

**Source:** [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sm5vdRWuhRk) · **Published:** 2024-11-15 · **By:** Berkman Klein Center · **Feed tags:** bkc-video

> Institute for Rebooting Social Media welcomes Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist and PolitiFact founder Bill Adair to discuss his new book, Beyond the Big Lie: The Epidemic of Political Lying, Why Republicans Do It More, and How It Could Burn Down Our Democracy. The conversation addresses patterns of deceit across the media landscape, the consequences these practices have on our democracy, and potential solutions to foster a healthier, more accurate political system.
