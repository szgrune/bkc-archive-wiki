** Mapping social media space (https://sciencepolicyreview.pubpub.org/pub/22y8h1p7/release/1)
------------------------------------------------------------

Nathaniel Lubin, Kalie Mayberry, Dylan Moses, Manon Revel, Luke Thorburn, and Andrew West identify two modes of mapping the space of social media regulation and discuss the trade-offs involved in different regulations. "While the maps are merely descriptive, they can be used by policymakers to understand...what kinds of intervention are most likely to achieve the outcomes they are interested in."

https://www.techpolicy.press/the-future-is-analog-if-you-can-afford-it/

** Tech-no futures? (https://www.techpolicy.press/the-future-is-analog-if-you-can-afford-it/)
------------------------------------------------------------

"Analog is back," writes Maroussia Lévesque, arguing that the ability to bypass digital technology reflects an inherent privilege akin to a sort of exceptionalism. "As [AI systems] increasingly mediate important aspects of our lives...the ability to secure a manual override becomes ever more advantageous."

https://www.niemanlab.org/2024/09/want-to-fight-misinformation-teach-people-how-algorithms-work/

** Fighting misinformation (https://www.niemanlab.org/2024/09/want-to-fight-misinformation-teach-people-how-algorithms-work/)
------------------------------------------------------------

A study led by Myojung Chung exposes how a deeper understanding of social media algorithms leads individuals to be more wary of misinformation. "It’s important to create algorithm literacy programs that are customized to meet the needs of different groups. By doing this, we can help ensure that everyone can navigate the digital world effectively."

https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2024/08/22/cyberlaw-clinic-supports-public-interest-patent-law-institute-w-comment-to-uspto-re-patents-and-artificial-intelligence/

** Inventing with AI (https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2024/08/22/cyberlaw-clinic-supports-public-interest-patent-law-institute-w-comment-to-uspto-re-patents-and-artificial-intelligence/)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Cyberlaw Clinic aided the Public Interest Patent Law Institute in filing comments with the US Patent and Trademark Office, stressing the importance of human inventorship and urging the USPTO to remain vigilant about compliance with the Patent Act. "Without further guidance, there will be too much uncertainty and inconsistency across examiner decisions and too great a likelihood that patents will issue that do not comply with the law."

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.10270

** LM value alignment (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.10270)
------------------------------------------------------------

With coauthors from HLS and OpenAI, Manon Revel introduces evaluative metrics for reward models' alignment with values expressed in training datasets. "The importance of having a high-quality alignment pipeline becomes paramount as powerful base models are open-sourced."

Alejandra Caraballo.
