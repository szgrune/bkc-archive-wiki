** LGBTQ safety and Meta (https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/meta-oversight-board-social-media-failing-lgbtq-1234947358/)
------------------------------------------------------------

With help from the Cyberlaw Clinic, GLAAD submitted comments that were cited by the Oversight Board in its judgment that Meta has failed to protect LGBTQ people online. “Meta has consistently failed to hold anti-LGBTQ and particularly anti-trans content as violative of its community standards,” Clinical Instructor Alejandra Caraballo tells Rolling Stone (https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/meta-oversight-board-social-media-failing-lgbtq-1234947358/) .

https://www.nature.com/articles/s44284-023-00003-7

** Regulating tech locally (https://www.nature.com/articles/s44284-023-00003-7)
------------------------------------------------------------

Aileen Nielsen discusses the potential for cities to contribute to the regulation of emerging technologies. "US cities are regulating private use of technology more actively than the federal government, but the likely effects of this phenomenon are unclear. City lawmaking could make up for national regulatory shortfalls, but only if cities can thread the needle of special interests and partisanship."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceQ_r8RBOMA

** Centering disability rights (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceQ_r8RBOMA)
------------------------------------------------------------

Current fellow Maitreya Shah joins a Center for Democracy and Technology panel on centering disability rights in discussions of AI policy. "People with disabilities face unique risks which are quite different from other identities that are at the center of the conversations on AI harms like race and gender."
