** Beyond TikTok (https://www.techpolicy.press/podcast-whats-next-for-tiktok-and-us-tech-policy/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Reacting to the new law that could ban TikTok, Anupam Chander underlines the need for greater accountability in the US government's tech regulation. "[L]et's make sure that we've reformed our national security law not just to give more power to the national security authorities, but also to make sure that when they exercise that power, they have to explain themselves. They have to subject themselves to proper judicial review."

https://www.yalelawjournal.org/forum/artificial-why-copyright-is-not-the-right-policy-tool-to-deal-with-generative-ai

** IP versus AI (https://www.yalelawjournal.org/forum/artificial-why-copyright-is-not-the-right-policy-tool-to-deal-with-generative-ai)
------------------------------------------------------------

Micaela Mantegna argues that copyright law is ill-equipped to deal with generative AI. "[T]he potential harm that copyright expansion could inflict on the entire Internet and cultural landscape could be avoided by resorting to other methods of consent, attribution, and retribution."

Micaela also discussed ethics and aesthetics of AI and copyright (https://gdcvault.com/play/1034178/ARTificial-Ethics-and-Aesthetics-of) on a Game Developers Conference panel.

https://datasociety.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Keywords_Public_Interest_Washington_Cheung_04242024.pdf

** Public interest? (https://datasociety.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Keywords_Public_Interest_Washington_Cheung_04242024.pdf)
------------------------------------------------------------

In an essay for Data & Society, Anne Washington and Joanne Cheung tackle the concept of "public interest" and highlight the power imbalances and embedded social hierarchies that exist within it. "Better serving the public interest in a more comprehensive sense means to make the implicit negotiations between varied interests explicit."

Plus, in the same collection of Keywords of the Datafied State (https://datasociety.net/library/keywords-of-the-datafied-state/) , current fellow Maitreya Shah co-authored the essay on "automation."

https://shepersistedpodcast.com/podcast-episodes/ep187

** Modern connections (https://shepersistedpodcast.com/podcast-episodes/ep187)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jeffrey Hall discusses friendship and loneliness among young people. "[C]an social media, specifically computer mediated communication...play a positive role in our social leisure time? Definitely. And if you are not finding that you feel thoroughly nourished, which means you're feeling alone, you're feeling disconnected, you should take action to move away from the entertainment uses of social media and towards the communication uses of social media."
