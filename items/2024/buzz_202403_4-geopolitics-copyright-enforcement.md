** Tech in Africa (https://scsp222.substack.com/p/episode-42-bulelani-jili-on-technology)
------------------------------------------------------------

Current Fellow Bulelani Jili explores the role of technology in Africa-China relations. "China has effectively sought to increase its influence on the continent by also offering access to Information and Communication Technology (ICT) and ICT Loans and those technologies generally range from basic infrastructure like fiberoptic cables all the way to things like facial recognition systems."

https://hls.harvard.edu/today/does-chatgpt-violate-new-york-times-copyrights/?utm_source=SilverpopMailing&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=Daily%20Gazette%2020240327%20(1)

** NYT v. ChatGPT (https://hls.harvard.edu/today/does-chatgpt-violate-new-york-times-copyrights/?utm_source=SilverpopMailing&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=Daily%20Gazette%2020240327%20(1))
------------------------------------------------------------

Mason Kortz discusses the legal arguments, possible resolutions, and potential implications of the NYT v. OpenAI lawsuit. "But normative questions about who’s in the right and who’s in the wrong here get very complicated, because the companies concerned about AI aren’t always properly compensating their own content creators."

https://www.abc.net.au/listen/programs/radionational-breakfast/ai-rumman-chowdhury-responsible-and-ethical-use/103632100

** Community-driven AI (https://www.abc.net.au/listen/programs/radionational-breakfast/ai-rumman-chowdhury-responsible-and-ethical-use/103632100)
------------------------------------------------------------

As part of a conversation on responsible integration of AI, Rumman Chowdhury emphasizes the importance of community engagement. “With generative AI...we’re creating ways of doing structured public feedback, ways in which we engage broader members of the population to be part of identifying the values these systems should hold and also being part of building these value-driving systems.”

https://www.techpolicy.press/policies-vs-enforcement-whats-up-with-metas-platforming-of-violent-extremist-hate-account-libs-of-tiktok/

** Weak enforcement (https://www.techpolicy.press/policies-vs-enforcement-whats-up-with-metas-platforming-of-violent-extremist-hate-account-libs-of-tiktok/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Alejandra Caraballo criticizes Meta's lack of enforcement of the company's own bullying, harassment, and hate speech policies. "The broader issue of Meta’s enforcement is its continual head in the sand approach towards these types of [anti-LGBT] accounts and their willful ignorance of the broader context that this account operates under."
