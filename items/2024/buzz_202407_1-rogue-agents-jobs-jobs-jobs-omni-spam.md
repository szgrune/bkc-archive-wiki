** AI agents (https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2024/07/ai-agents-safety-risks/678864/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jonathan Zittrain warns that we must act to regulate AI agents—"AIs that act independently on behalf of humans"—to avoid significant safety risks. "This routinization of AI that doesn’t simply talk with us, but also acts out in the world, is a crossing of the blood-brain barrier between digital and analog, bits and atoms. That should give us pause."

https://www.prospectmagazine.co.uk/ideas/technology/internet/66995/are-there-any-humans-left-on-the-internet

** Internet of Clippys (https://www.prospectmagazine.co.uk/ideas/technology/internet/66995/are-there-any-humans-left-on-the-internet)
------------------------------------------------------------

Ethan Zuckerman wonders where the humans have gone and where we can find non-spam online. "Perhaps it is time to fence off human-only spaces on the internet, leaving them free of roaming AIs. Perhaps we will someday look at the unvarnished human expression in a space like Metafilter as we do at the Grand Canyon or thousand-year-old redwoods."

https://yalebooks.yale.edu/2024/06/07/loneliness-and-screens-causes-and-consequences/

** Loneliness (https://yalebooks.yale.edu/2024/06/07/loneliness-and-screens-causes-and-consequences/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jeffrey Hall and Andy Merolla unpack the relationship between increased screen time and feelings of loneliness. "If loneliness is a virus, excessive screentime is like running a temperature. We must address the underlying causes of loneliness, which include access to mental health treatment, discrimination, financial precarity and numerous other societal and structural barriers to forming relationships and spending time with others."

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/johanna-wild-investigative-journalist-dcf63329feb9

** Digital OSINT (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/johanna-wild-investigative-journalist-dcf63329feb9)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sam Hinds interviews Johanna Wild on the benefits and risks of using novel open source intelligence (OSINT) tools to enable a broader, more transparent global knowledge base. "Open source researchers are often portrayed as some type of ‘nerdy hero’ who spends time on his laptop to research ‘the bad guys’ and is celebrated once he succeeds. The idea of one hero figure who solves all the research challenges is really the exact opposite of how open source research works best..."

https://www.techpolicy.press/biden-cannot-protect-privacy-or-defend-democracy-by-expanding-surveillance-powers/

** Surveillance crisis (https://www.techpolicy.press/biden-cannot-protect-privacy-or-defend-democracy-by-expanding-surveillance-powers/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Isadora Borges Monroy argues that democracy would be best served if President Biden curtails the expansion of the government's surveillance powers. "Mass surveillance treats everyone as guilty until proven innocent by the data. Turn your pockets and your inbox inside out perpetually—if you don’t want to, what are you hiding?"

https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20539517241252129

** Age restrictions (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20539517241252129)
------------------------------------------------------------

Zahra Stardust and coauthors show that automated age verification tools for pornography websites perform underwhelmingly and are fundamentally misconceived. "This is a case study where automated decision-making is being misapplied to try to solve a social, cultural and educational problem rather than a technical one."

https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4840383

** Regulating AI (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4840383)
------------------------------------------------------------

Woodrow Hartzog argues "if lawmakers do not address the dynamics of dangerous extraction, harmful normalization, and adversarial self-dealing, then AI systems will likely be used to do more harm than good." "Industry’s mandate to maximize shareholder value ensures that no human resource—including our thoughts, our relationships, our attention, our labor, and our environment—remains unexploited."

https://cyber.harvard.edu/getinvolved/jobs
