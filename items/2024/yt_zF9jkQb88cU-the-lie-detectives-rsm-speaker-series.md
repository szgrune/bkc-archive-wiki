# The Lie Detectives (RSM Speaker Series)

**Source:** [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zF9jkQb88cU) · **Published:** 2024-09-26 · **By:** Berkman Klein Center · **Feed tags:** bkc-video

> How have political campaigns prepared themselves to navigate the challenges of disinformation in 2024? What lessons have they learned from past elections, and what strategies are they deploying in an age where disinformation online continues to run rampant in the forms of conspiracy theories, memes, and now, AI?
> 
> The Institute for Rebooting Social Media is pleased to welcome journalist Sasha Issenberg to discuss his new book, The Lie Detectives: In Search of a Playbook for Winning Elections in the Disinformation Age, which presents a snapshot of a political class working to find acceptable solutions to counteracting mis/disinformation.
