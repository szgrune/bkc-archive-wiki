# Consumer Taste Sharing Is Driving the Online Music Business and Democratizing Culture

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2005/Consumer_Taste_Sharing_is_Driving_the_Online_Music_Business_and_Democratizing_Culture) · **Published:** 2005-12-13 · **By:** Derek Slater, Mike McGuire · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> New features of online music stores, as well as stand-alone products and services, are designed to help users share their taste in music and satisfy the inherent desire that music fans have to share their favorite music. Additionally, these tools have important potential cultural benefits that reinforce the value of music and the music economy.
> 
> Key Findings• Nearly one-fourth of frequent online music users say that the ability to share music with others in some fashion is an important criteria
