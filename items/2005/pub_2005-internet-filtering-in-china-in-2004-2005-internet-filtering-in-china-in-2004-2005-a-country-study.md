# Internet Filtering in China in 2004-2005: A Country Study

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2005/Internet_Filtering_in_China_in_2004_2005) · **Published:** 2005-04-13 · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> China's Internet filtering regime is the most
> sophisticated effort of its kind in the world. Compared to similar
> efforts in other states, China's filtering regime is pervasive,
> sophisticated, and effective. It comprises multiple levels of legal
> regulation and technical control. It involves numerous state agencies
> and thousands of public and private personnel. It censors content
> transmitted through multiple methods, including Web pages, Web logs,
> on-line discussion forums, university bulletin
