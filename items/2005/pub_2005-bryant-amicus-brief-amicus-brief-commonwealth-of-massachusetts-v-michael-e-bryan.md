# Amicus Brief: Commonwealth of Massachusetts v. Michael E. Bryant

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2005/Bryant_Amicus_Brief) · **Published:** 2005-12-15 · **By:** Phil Malone, Bruce P. Keller · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> Issue Presented for Review:
> 
> Should the application of Massachusetts GeneralLaw, Chapter 276, § 3A, which requires search warrants to be executed and returned within seven days ofissuance, be expanded to also require that the complex and time-consuming forensic review and analysis of thecontents of a computer seized pursuant to a warrant be completed within the same seven-day period?
