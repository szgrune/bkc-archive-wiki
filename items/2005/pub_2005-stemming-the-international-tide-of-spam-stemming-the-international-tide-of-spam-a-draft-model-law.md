# Stemming the International Tide of Spam: A Draft Model Law

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2005/Stemming_the_International_Tide_of_Spam) · **Published:** 2005-09-30 · **By:** John Palfrey · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> Executive Summary:
> 
> Spammers continue to run circles around the anti-spam police. Dozens of countries have anti spam laws on the books, yet enforcement of the statutes is costly, infrequent, and rarely, if ever,has any meaningful net effect on the amount of spam sent and received the next day. Each enforcement action is complex, frequently involving multiple jurisdictions, and more expensive than most developing countries can afford to undertake.
