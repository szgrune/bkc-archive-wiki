> This application report reviews the DollarRevenue application, produced by DollarRevenue.com.
> 
> We find that DollarRevenue is badware because it
> installs a Trojan horse application without disclosure. This Trojan
> horse reportedly downloads additional adware on the user's computer
> without informing the user. In addition, DollarRevenue encourages
> user's to install the application without explaining its own nature or
> functionality, and is nearly impossible to uninstall.
