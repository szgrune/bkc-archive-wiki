**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2006/Jessica_Simpson_Screensaver) · **Published:** 2006-05-15 · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> This application report reviews the Jessica Simpson Screensaver application, produced by Team Taylor Made and distributed via www.teamtaylormade.com.
> 
> We find that Jessica Simpson Screensaver is badware because it is bundled with Trojan horses, as well as undisclosed	adware, spyware, and a stealth dialer.
