# Illegal Internet Networks in the Developing World

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2004/Illegal_Internet_Networks_in_the_Developing_World) · **Published:** 2004-12-30 · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> Abstract:
> 
> Enabled by falling costs associated with constructing international voice and data networks, and motivated by high fees charged by incumbents for international telecom services, illegal Internet network operators are proliferating in many developing countries.
