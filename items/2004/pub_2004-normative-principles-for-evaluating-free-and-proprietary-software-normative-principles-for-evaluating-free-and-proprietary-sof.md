# Normative Principles for Evaluating Free and Proprietary Software

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2004/Normative_Principles_for_Evaluating_Free_and_Proprietary_Software) · **Published:** 2004-01-08 · **By:** Jonathan Zittrain · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> Abstract:
> 
> The production of most mass-market software can be grouped roughly
> according to free and proprietary development models. These models
> differ greatly from one another, and their associated licenses tend to
> insist that new software inherit the characteristics of older software
> from which it may be derived. Thus the success of one model or another
> can become self-perpetuating, as older free software is incorporated
> into later free software and proprietary software is embedded
