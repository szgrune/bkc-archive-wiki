Watch:  "Shaping Consumption: How Social Network Manipulation Tactics Are Impacting Amazon and Influencing Consumers" with Renee DiResta
https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/2018/luncheon/05/DiResta
Narrative manipulation issues - such as manufactured consensus, brigading, harassment, information laundering, fake accounts, news voids, and more - are increasingly well-documented problems affecting the entire social ecosystem. This has had negative consequences for information integrity, and for trust. This recent talk (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/2018/luncheon/05/DiResta) examines the ways that these same manipulative tactics are being deployed on Amazon, which is now the dominant product search engine and a battlefield for economically and ideologically motivated actors.
Watch the talk or listen to the podcast (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/2018/luncheon/05/DiResta)
