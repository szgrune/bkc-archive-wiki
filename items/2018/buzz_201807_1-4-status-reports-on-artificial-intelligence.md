** Algorithms and Justice (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/algorithms-and-justice-the-berkman-klein-center-for-internet-society-examines-the-role-of-the-84a3a7d90a6)

Christopher T. Bavitz (https://hls.harvard.edu/faculty/directory/10055/Bavitz)  and Kira Hessekiel (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/khessekiel)  look at how government institutions are incorporating artificial intelligence, algorithms, and machine learning technologies into their decisionmaking, and describe (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/algorithms-and-justice-the-berkman-klein-center-for-internet-society-examines-the-role-of-the-84a3a7d90a6)  an ambitious agenda for making sure that this use of technology is transparent, understandable, and fair.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/understanding-media-and-information-quality-in-an-age-of-artificial-intelligence-automation-a562620c7039

** Media and Information Quality (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/understanding-media-and-information-quality-in-an-age-of-artificial-intelligence-automation-a562620c7039)
------------------------------------------------------------
Looking at large datasets covering millions of stories, tweets, and Facebook shares, across diverse platforms, Yochai Benkler (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ybenkler) , Robert Faris (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/rfaris) , Hal Roberts (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/hroberts) , and Nikki Bourassa (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/nbourassa)  study if and how technology plays a meaningful role in the spread of propaganda and disinformation. Though the authors see some potential in AI tools (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/understanding-media-and-information-quality-in-an-age-of-artificial-intelligence-automation-a562620c7039) to improve information quality, "it doesn’t look like AI is coming to the rescue anytime soon, if ever."

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/global-governance-and-inclusion-66fa3e996f50

** Global Governance and Inclusion (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/global-governance-and-inclusion-66fa3e996f50)
------------------------------------------------------------
“I guarantee you that no one in my office is thinking about these issues,” one state law enforcement official remarked (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/global-governance-and-inclusion-66fa3e996f50) at a recent forum we convened on emerging AI issues. Ryan Budish (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/rbudish) , Urs Gasser (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ugasser) , and Amar Ashar (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/aashar)  reflect on the challenge of bridging the knowledge gaps between the developers of AI technology and the policymakers charged with preparing for societal impact.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/https-medium-com-aidajoa-autonomous-vehicles-149b68ec51a8

** Autonomous Vehicles (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/https-medium-com-aidajoa-autonomous-vehicles-149b68ec51a8)
------------------------------------------------------------
While developers are getting self-driving cars to navigate the busy streets of cities around the world, their lawyers need to navigate the maze of overlapping regulations governing everything from road safety to cybersecurity. Aida Joaquin Acosta (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ajoaquinacosta)  details 3 International Regulatory Discussions Lawyers Should Be Aware Of (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/https-medium-com-aidajoa-autonomous-vehicles-149b68ec51a8) .

The Supreme Court and Zombie Laws (https://medium.com/@zittrain/the-supreme-court-and-zombie-laws-2087d7bb9a75)
"I suspect that laws held unconstitutional, but not repealed, will be found to be biding their time for return. Because a Court activist enough to start reversing itself on a bunch of issues will, regrettably, be activist enough to want old laws to see new life," says Jonathan Zittrain (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jzittrain) in this Medium post (https://medium.com/@zittrain/the-supreme-court-and-zombie-laws-2087d7bb9a75) .

Designing Frameworks that Allow for Intentions, Commitments and Exuberance in AI (https://medium.com/@b.rakova/designing-frameworks-that-allow-for-intentions-commitments-and-exuberance-in-ai-f9782ac2d279)
"How should we think about the ethical implications of AI-powered products and services? What kinds of controls do we need to minimize AI’s potential harm to society and maximize its benefits?" asks Bobi Rakaova, a member of this year's Assembly (https://bkmla.org/) cohort, in this piece (https://medium.com/@b.rakova/designing-frameworks-that-allow-for-intentions-commitments-and-exuberance-in-ai-f9782ac2d279) .

Paid Trolls and Online Harassment: How a Journalist Confronts Azerbaijani Censorship (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/paid-trolls-and-online-harassment-how-a-journalist-confronts-azerbaijani-censorship-848c2f0072c7)
In Azerbaijan, "the authorities are very afraid of the Internet — they’re concerned with how much information gets out and how far it can reach," explains Arzu Geybullayeva (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ageybulla) in this interview about trolling and harassment from the Internet Monitor (https://thenetmonitor.org/) .
