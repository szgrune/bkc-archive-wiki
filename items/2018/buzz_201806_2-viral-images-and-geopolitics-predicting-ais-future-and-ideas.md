"Of course, AI will help in tackling cybercrime. And, of course, AI will help cybercriminals too. The real question is this: which side does the technology help more? We don’t know. This makes its future very hard to predict," explains Bruce Schneier (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/bschneier) in this interview (https://www.livemint.com/AI/Vb4RIepCjrd8rwfZyVz7pN/AI-is-a-very-surprising-tech-which-makes-its-future-hard-t.html) with LiveMint.
