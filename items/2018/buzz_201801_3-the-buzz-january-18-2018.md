It's the (Democracy-Poisoning) Golden Age of Free Speech (https://www.wired.com/story/free-speech-issue-tech-turmoil-new-censorship/)

In this article from Wired (https://www.wired.com/story/free-speech-issue-tech-turmoil-new-censorship/) , Berkman Klein faculty associate Zeynep Tufekci (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/zeynep)  questions the idea that the internet has facilitated a "golden age" of free speech. Rather, Tufekci writes, the methods of censorship have changed: "In the 21st century, the capacity to spread ideas and reach an audience is no longer limited by access to expensive, centralized broadcasting infrastructure. It’s limited instead by one’s ability to garner and distribute attention."

Communicating Use and Reuse in the Digital Collection Interface (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/communicating-use-and-reuse-in-the-digital-collection-interface-434d970c4d38)

L. Kelly Fitzpatrick (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/lfitzpatrick)  describes the importance of "open content identifiers," or visual indicators that help users navigate institutions' digital collections, in this article on Medium (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/communicating-use-and-reuse-in-the-digital-collection-interface-434d970c4d38) . "By reviewing how institutions are using open content identifiers, we can recognize current practice, and inform decisions and standards of practice in our collections moving forward," Fitzpatrick concludes.
