Morality in the Machines (https://today.law.harvard.edu/feature/morality-in-the-machines/)
This article (https://today.law.harvard.edu/feature/morality-in-the-machines/) from the Harvard Law Bulletin offers a snapshot of some of the work BKC and the MIT Media Lab are doing related to the ethics and governance of artificial intelligence.

"The AI initiative aims to establish rules to govern driverless cars before they hit the road en masse; to make social media news feeds more transparent and defend them against manipulation by Russian government-sponsored trolls and other propagandists; and to guard against racial bias seeping into seemingly objective courtroom tools. In short, Harvard and MIT want the public to understand and control these fast-growing technologies, before they control us."

Measuring Impact, Building Inclusive AI, and Bolstering Trust in the Digital Ecosystem
"A lesson from all pivotal innovations discussed is that AI will always reflect the values of its creators, from who sits on a corporate board to who the actual designers are, and that without intentionally fostering an inclusive environment of AI developers and stakeholders, we risk building AI that mirrors narrow and exclusive perspectives." reports Jenna Sherman (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jsherman) in this write-up (https://cyber.harvard.edu/sites/cyber.harvard.edu/files/%5BAIGD%5D%20APAC-US%20AI%20Workshop%20HK%202018%20Write-Up.pdf) for the Global AI Dialogue Series (https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LIW2MMRW8ai14oCORHfKX83ZqZSIHQmB/view) .

Why Gesture-Based Interfaces Haven’t Lived up to the Hype (https://medium.com/@caseorganic/why-gesture-based-interfaces-havent-lived-up-to-the-hype-9ab47aa3a94b)
"Does a gestural control interface invite imagination?" asks Amber Case (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/acase) , in this Medium post (https://medium.com/@caseorganic/why-gesture-based-interfaces-havent-lived-up-to-the-hype-9ab47aa3a94b) . "No. It demands precision. Those automatic hand dryers at the airport work against our human imprecision, instead of with it. When poorly implemented, gesture control interfaces can embarrass us. In some cases, they can even ignore us."

https://medium.com/@dsearls/why-personal-agency-matters-more-than-personal-data-a1cd520b9b5b

Why Personal Agency Matters More Than Personal Data (https://medium.com/@dsearls/why-personal-agency-matters-more-than-personal-data-a1cd520b9b5b)
"Privacy is personal. That means privacy is an effect of personal agency, projected by personal tech and personal expressions of intent that others can respect without working at it. We have that in the offline world. We can have it in the online world too," suggests Doc Searls (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/dsearls) , in this Medium post (https://medium.com/@dsearls/why-personal-agency-matters-more-than-personal-data-a1cd520b9b5b) .
