Banning Chinese Phones Won’t Fix Security Problems with our Electronic Supply Chain (https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/posteverything/wp/2018/05/08/banning-chinese-phones-wont-fix-security-problems-with-our-electronic-supply-chain/?noredirect=on&utm_term=.091221503c6c)
"All of our computerized systems are deeply international, and we have no choice but to trust the companies and governments that touch those systems. And while we can ban a few specific products, services or companies, no country can isolate itself from potential foreign interference," says Bruce Schneier (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/bschneier) in this Washington Post article (https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/posteverything/wp/2018/05/08/banning-chinese-phones-wont-fix-security-problems-with-our-electronic-supply-chain/?noredirect=on&utm_term=.091221503c6c) .

Hate Speech Laws in India (https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pDoIwlusnM3ys-1GAYbnTPmepU22b2Zr/view)
A new research report (https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pDoIwlusnM3ys-1GAYbnTPmepU22b2Zr/view) , Hate Speech Laws in India, co-written by Chinmayi Arun (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/carun) , has been released by the Centre for Communication Governance at the National Law University, Delhi. You can read the full report, described as, "an effort to map hate speech laws in India, and attempt to offer a clear overview to anyone interested in the regulation of hate speech in India," here (https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pDoIwlusnM3ys-1GAYbnTPmepU22b2Zr/view) .

Code Is Law - But It's Not the Only Law for Blockchains (https://www.coindesk.com/code-law-not-law-blockchains/)
Primavera De Filippi (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/pdefilippi)  provides an overview of "the multiple layers of governance affecting blockchain-based systems" in this piece from Coindesk (https://www.coindesk.com/code-law-not-law-blockchains/) .
