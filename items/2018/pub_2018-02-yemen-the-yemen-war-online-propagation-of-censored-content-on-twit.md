# The Yemen War Online: Propagation of Censored Content on Twitter

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2018/02/yemen) · **Published:** 2018-02-28 · **By:** Helmi Noman, Robert Faris, John Kelly · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> This study, conducted by the Internet Monitor project at the Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society, analyzes the sharing of information on Twitter among different political groups related to the ongoing conflict in Yemen. The study finds that the networks on Twitter are organized around and segregate along political lines.
