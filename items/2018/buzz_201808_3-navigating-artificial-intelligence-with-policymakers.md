Policymakers around the world increasingly find themselves at the center of discussions around AI technologies. "Many regulators did not want or need a grand governance framework for AI," says Ryan Budish (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/rbudish)  in this piece  summarizing (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/helping-global-policymakers-navigate-ais-challenges-and-opportunities-11b128687cad)  some of his recent conversations with policymakers. "They wanted practical steps to begin to close some of the many gaps they see and that worry them."

** How social media took us from Tahrir Square to Donald Trump (https://www.technologyreview.com/s/611806/how-social-media-took-us-from-tahrir-square-to-donald-trump/)
------------------------------------------------------------
To understand how digital technologies went from instruments for spreading democracy to weapons for attacking it, you have to look beyond the technologies themselves. In her piece (https://www.technologyreview.com/s/611806/how-social-media-took-us-from-tahrir-square-to-donald-trump/)  for MIT Technology Review Zeynep Tufekci (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/zeynep)  lays out some new reflections of how technology is being used in movements for social change.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/searching-browsing-and-filtering-open-access-content-in-digital-collections-9234d84a077

** Searching, Browsing, and Filtering Open Access Content in Digital Collections (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/searching-browsing-and-filtering-open-access-content-in-digital-collections-9234d84a077)
------------------------------------------------------------
By looking at the New York Public Library, the J. Paul Getty Museum, Europeana, and the Metropolitan Museum of Art, L. Kelly Fitzpatrick (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/lfitzpatrick) reviews different approaches (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/searching-browsing-and-filtering-open-access-content-in-digital-collections-9234d84a077)  to how we search, browse, and filter open content in digital collections.

** Dealing with an epidemic: preparing for the worst and hoping for the best. (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/dealing-with-an-epidemic-preparing-for-the-worse-and-hoping-for-the-best-52d192d15e9d)
------------------------------------------------------------
In this Medium (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/dealing-with-an-epidemic-preparing-for-the-worse-and-hoping-for-the-best-89555514fbb5)  piece, Ashveena Gajeelee (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/agajeelee)  examines the unique socio-economic characteristics that affect how Small Islands States cope with health epidemics.

https://citizenlab.ca/2018/08/how-wechat-filters-images-for-one-billion-users/

** How WeChat Filters Images for One Billion Users (https://citizenlab.ca/2018/08/how-wechat-filters-images-for-one-billion-users/)
------------------------------------------------------------
With over 1 billion monthly users, WeChat boasts the title of most popular chat application in China and the fourth largest in the world. However, a new report (https://citizenlab.ca/2018/08/cant-picture-this-an-analysis-of-image-filtering-on-wechat-moments/)  by Citizen Lab (https://citizenlab.ca/) researchers reveals exactly how the platform is able to censor images sent by these billion users.
