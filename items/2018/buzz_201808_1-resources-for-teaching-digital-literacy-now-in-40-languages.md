New Work:Sharing Learning Tools for Youth Digital Life

http://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2018-08/sharing-learning-tools-youth-digital-life

Over the past seven years, the Youth and Media (http://cyber.harvard.edu/research/youthandmedia) team at the Berkman Klein Center has worked with young people, educators, researchers, and practitioners on co-designing tools that can be used to learn and teach, in both formal and informal environments, about the digital world. Currently, over 100 such tools, which include curriculum modules, videos, visualizations, and learning experiences, are available through our Digital Literacy Resource Platform (DLRP) (https://dlrp.berkman.harvard.edu/) .

Today, we are happy to share the news that 18 of these open access educational resources have been included in Facebook’s new Digital Literacy Library for educators and will be translated over the coming weeks into over 40 languages, making them available to many more young people and educators around the globe.

Learn more about the DLRP (http://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2018-08/sharing-learning-tools-youth-digital-life)

In this three-part series for the Law and Political Economy blog (https://lpeblog.org/author/ybenkler/) , Yochai Benkler (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ybenkler) describes the limitations of the popular view of economic inequality and technology, summed up as “the robots will take all the jobs” and the legitimation of winner-take-all markets, and outlines a view of the political economy of technology.
