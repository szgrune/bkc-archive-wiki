** Practical lessons for regulating autonomous vehicles (https://news.itu.int/autonomous-vehicles-regulation-lessons/)
------------------------------------------------------------
"Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) are complex," says Berkman Klein Fellow Aida Joaquin Acosta (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ajoaquinacosta)  in this interview  (https://news.itu.int/autonomous-vehicles-regulation-lessons/) with ITU News (https://news.itu.int/autonomous-vehicles-regulation-lessons/) , summing up key points from 4 new policy papers on the subject (http://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2018-07/4-policy-papers-autonomous-vehicles) . "AVs may challenge cybersecurity, privacy, ethical norms, environmental and landscaping schemes, mobility and accessibility paradigms, use of resources, or sectorial employment."

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/the-uncertain-effects-of-https-adoption-on-access-to-information-worldwide-fa0c94ba124a

** The Uncertain Effects of HTTPS Adoption on Access to Information Worldwide (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/the-uncertain-effects-of-https-adoption-on-access-to-information-worldwide-fa0c94ba124a)
------------------------------------------------------------
More and more websites are adopting HTTPS as a method to secure users privacy and browsing information from prying eyes and surveillance. A new analysis by  (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/the-uncertain-effects-of-https-adoption-on-access-to-information-worldwide-fa0c94ba124a) Internet Monitor (https://thenetmonitor.org/)  shows the number of the most popular and filtering-vulnerable websites using HTTPS has increased from 40% to 58% over the last year. But this wider adoption could have uncertain long-term implications for online access to information around the world, according to author Casey Tilton (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ctilton) .

https://medium.com/@judithd/you-are-entering-an-ephemeral-bio-allowed-data-capture-zone-5ecafd2dbdaf

** You Are Entering an Ephemeral Bio-allowed Data Capture Zone (https://medium.com/@judithd/you-are-entering-an-ephemeral-bio-allowed-data-capture-zone-5ecafd2dbdaf)
------------------------------------------------------------
Faculty fellow Judith Donath  (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jdonath) argues against a ban on facial recognition in  (https://medium.com/@judithd/you-are-entering-an-ephemeral-bio-allowed-data-capture-zone-5ecafd2dbdaf) this  (https://medium.com/@judithd/you-are-entering-an-ephemeral-bio-allowed-data-capture-zone-5ecafd2dbdaf) Medium (https://medium.com/@judithd/you-are-entering-an-ephemeral-bio-allowed-data-capture-zone-5ecafd2dbdaf)  post (https://medium.com/@judithd/you-are-entering-an-ephemeral-bio-allowed-data-capture-zone-5ecafd2dbdaf) , suggesting instead that “we need to seek regulation based on the qualities and capabilities of the technology, not the technology itself.” Donath proposes a list of categories that could be used as a starting point for developing a taxonomy for thinking through privacy-enhanced identifying technologies.

** Toward a safe and open Internet in Pakistan (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/toward-a-safe-and-open-internet-in-pakistan-37528e8d0506)
------------------------------------------------------------
Berkman Klein intern Mary Meisenzahl (http://marymeisenzahl.com/)  interviews Nighat Dad (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ndad) , Executive Director of the Digital Rights Foundation (https://digitalrightsfoundation.pk/)  and affiliate at Berkman Klein, about the Foundation’s ongoing work to help women and vulnerable populations in Pakistan navigate and understand digital rights. “My mission was that the demand for good legislation, or demand for the government to do something about this issue should come from the public or Internet users, not digital rights organizations,” Dad says in this  (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/toward-a-safe-and-open-internet-in-pakistan-37528e8d0506) Medium (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/toward-a-safe-and-open-internet-in-pakistan-37528e8d0506)  post (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/toward-a-safe-and-open-internet-in-pakistan-37528e8d0506) .

** Don’t fear the TSA cutting airport security. Be glad that they’re talking about it. (https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/posteverything/wp/2018/08/07/dont-fear-the-tsa-cutting-airport-security-be-glad-that-theyre-talking-about-it/?noredirect=on&utm_term=.4e0a1d3cda95)
------------------------------------------------------------
Despite largely negative media coverage of the subject, it is a good thing that TSA is brainstorming ways to optimize security in airports, Berkman Klein Fellow Bruce Schneier (http://cyber.harvard.edu/people/bschneier)  argues in  (https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/posteverything/wp/2018/08/07/dont-fear-the-tsa-cutting-airport-security-be-glad-that-theyre-talking-about-it/?noredirect=on&utm_term=.4e0a1d3cda95) this piece (https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/posteverything/wp/2018/08/07/dont-fear-the-tsa-cutting-airport-security-be-glad-that-theyre-talking-about-it/?noredirect=on&utm_term=.25c303843fa8)  for the  (https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/posteverything/wp/2018/08/07/dont-fear-the-tsa-cutting-airport-security-be-glad-that-theyre-talking-about-it/?noredirect=on&utm_term=.4e0a1d3cda95) Washington Post
(https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/posteverything/wp/2018/08/07/dont-fear-the-tsa-cutting-airport-security-be-glad-that-theyre-talking-about-it/?noredirect=on&utm_term=.4e0a1d3cda95) . “We need to evaluate airport security based on concrete costs and benefits, and not continue to implement security theater based on fear,” Schneier says.
