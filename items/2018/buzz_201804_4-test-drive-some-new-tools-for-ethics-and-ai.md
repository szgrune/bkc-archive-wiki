Check Out: A suite of new projects and tools that address Ethics and Artificial Intelligence
http://bkmla.org/2018/projects.html

The 2018 Assembly cohort (http://bkmla.org/2018/people.html)  came together around the challenge of artificial intelligence and its governance. Over four months, they took part in a rigorous two week design thinking and team building sprint, participated in a spring term course—the Ethics of AI (https://h2o.law.harvard.edu/playlists/53282) —co-taught by Jonathan Zittrain and Joi Ito, and developed their projects throughout the three month development period.

Projects emerging from this cohort focus on helping cities and communities, tackling problems starting at the dataset layer, and analyzing and commenting on current practices in AI. Explore their work here (http://bkmla.org/2018/projects.html) .
