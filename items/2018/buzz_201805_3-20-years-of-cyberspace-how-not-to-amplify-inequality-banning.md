Censored, Suspended, Shadowbanned: User Interpretations of Content Moderation on Social Media Platforms (http://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1461444818773059)
The impacts of online content moderation go beyond questions of freedom of expression, writes Sarah Myers West (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/swest) , in this new article (http://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1461444818773059) , published in New Media & Society. West recommends "an educational, rather than punitive, model for content moderation systems."

https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ycordova

Minimize the Harmfulness of Bots, but Don't Ban Them (http://policyoptions.irpp.org/magazines/may-2018/minimize-harmfulness-bots-dont-ban/)
"As automated tools become increasingly common in the digital landscape, we need more transparent systems to identify bot accounts and ensure they are not causing harm to society. But calls for blanket bans and purges are overblown," write Arjun Bisen and Yasodara Córdova (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ycordova)  in this post (http://policyoptions.irpp.org/magazines/may-2018/minimize-harmfulness-bots-dont-ban/) for the Institute for Research on Public Policy.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/can-we-stop-technology-from-amplifying-societys-inequalities-perhaps-if-we-act-now-d51df2c5e3df

Can we stop technology from amplifying society’s inequalities? Perhaps, if we act now. (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/can-we-stop-technology-from-amplifying-societys-inequalities-perhaps-if-we-act-now-d51df2c5e3df)
"A common and incorrect assumption is that technology is neutral. The apps, algorithms and services we design ingrain choices made by its creators," writes Nani Jansen Reventlow (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/njansen) in this Medium post (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/can-we-stop-technology-from-amplifying-societys-inequalities-perhaps-if-we-act-now-d51df2c5e3df) .

Law as a Powerful Tool to Advance Global Health and Justice (https://medium.com/@ashveenag/law-as-a-powerful-tool-to-advance-global-health-and-justice-af15418031da)
"Although it seems difficult to explore the idea of health without thinking of justice and equity...  there is an obvious lack of connectivity between global health practitioners and the legal profession," explains Ashveena Gajeelee (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/agajeelee) in this Medium post (https://medium.com/@ashveenag/law-as-a-powerful-tool-to-advance-global-health-and-justice-af15418031da) .
