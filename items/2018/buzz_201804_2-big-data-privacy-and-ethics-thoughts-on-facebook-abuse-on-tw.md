Practical approaches to big data privacy over time (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2018/03/BigDataPrivacy)
This article (https://academic.oup.com/idpl/advance-article/doi/10.1093/idpl/ipx027/4930711) , authored by Micah Altman (http://informatics.mit.edu/people/micah-altman) , Alexandra Wood (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/awood) , David O'Brien (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/dobrien) , and Urs Gasser (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ugasser) , analyzes how privacy risks multiply as large quantities of personal data are collected over longer periods of time, draws attention to the relative weakness of data protections in the corporate and public sectors, and provides practical recommendations for protecting privacy when collecting and managing commercial and government data over extended periods of time.
https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/100163

Big Data, Health Law and Bioethics (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/100163)
This new book edited by I. Glenn Cohen (https://hls.harvard.edu/faculty/directory/10176/Cohen) , Holly Fernandez Lynch (http://petrieflom.law.harvard.edu/about/bio/lynch-holly-fernandez) , Effy Vayena (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/evayena) , and Urs Gasser (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ugasser)  explores key questions from a variety of perspectives, examining how law promotes or discourages the use of big data in the health care sphere, and considers what we can learn from other sectors. The book's introduction, which summarizes all of the chapters, is available for free download here on SSRN. (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3147243)
