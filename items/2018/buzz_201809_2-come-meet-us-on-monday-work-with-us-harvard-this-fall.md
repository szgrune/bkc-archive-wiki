https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2018/09/12/cyberlaw-clinic-helps-eliminate-seven-words-policy-for-registration-of-us-domain-names/

Cyberlaw Clinic Helps Eliminate “Seven Words” Policy for Registration of .US Domain Names (https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2018/09/12/cyberlaw-clinic-helps-eliminate-seven-words-policy-for-registration-of-us-domain-names/)
The Center's Cyberlaw Clinic (https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/)  and their team of super lawyers got involved in a case last year to eliminate arbitrary speech restrictions employed by the domain name registrar that oversees the ".us" TLD. Domain name geeks in particular should enjoy this write-up (https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2018/09/12/cyberlaw-clinic-helps-eliminate-seven-words-policy-for-registration-of-us-domain-names/) .

https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3247286

Algorithms at Work: Productivity Monitoring Platforms and Wearable Technology as the New Data-Centric Research Agenda for Employment and Labor Law (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3247286)
Recent work technology advancements such as productivity monitoring platforms and wearable technology have given rise to new organizational behavior regarding the management of employees and also prompt new legal questions regarding the protection of workers’ privacy rights. -- Ifeoma Ajunwa (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/iajunwa)

https://medium.com/@ayesharbajwa/what-we-talk-about-when-we-talk-about-bias-a-guide-for-everyone-3af55b85dcdc

What We Talk About When We Talk About Bias (A guide for everyone) (https://medium.com/@ayesharbajwa/what-we-talk-about-when-we-talk-about-bias-a-guide-for-everyone-3af55b85dcdc)
How data bias, model bias, learning algorithm bias, system bias, and human-in-the-loop bias are observed in machine learning systems. -- Ayesha Bajwa (https://medium.com/@ayesharbajwa?source=post_header_lockup)  shares insights from our Ethics and Governance of Artificial Intelligence (https://www.media.mit.edu/courses/the-ethics-and-governance-of-artificial-intelligence/) course co-taught with the MIT Media Lab
