Facebook’s Cambridge Analytica Problems are Nothing Compared to What’s Coming for All of Online Publishing (http://blogs.harvard.edu/doc/2018/03/23/nothing/?utm_source=nextdraft&utm_medium=email)
"What will happen when the Times, the New Yorker and other pubs own up to the simple fact that they are just as guilty as Facebook of leaking its readers’ data to other parties, for—in many if not most cases—God knows what purposes besides “interest-based” advertising?" asks Doc Searls (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/dsearls)  in a new post on his blog (http://blogs.harvard.edu/doc/2018/03/23/nothing/?utm_source=nextdraft&utm_medium=email) .

https://medium.com/@caseorganic/how-to-design-a-better-internet-calming-technology-humanizing-the-algorithm-and-a-new-xerox-parc-e4d702be8787

How to Design a Better Internet: Calming Technology, Humanizing the Algorithm, and a New Xerox PARC (https://medium.com/@caseorganic/how-to-design-a-better-internet-calming-technology-humanizing-the-algorithm-and-a-new-xerox-parc-e4d702be8787)
"The a priori algorithm has reached the limits of its usefulness: In the wake of US election tampering, Facebook and Twitter are still struggling to deal with the blowback from algorithmic systems architected to optimize ad revenue, but are ripe for exploitation — while also maximizing the chance of user depression," writes Amber Case (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/acase)  in this Medium post (https://medium.com/@caseorganic/how-to-design-a-better-internet-calming-technology-humanizing-the-algorithm-and-a-new-xerox-parc-e4d702be8787) .

https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/lstark

Start Treating Private Personal Data on Facebook Like Medical Data: It's Just as Sensitive to Nefarious Manipulation (http://www.nydailynews.com/opinion/start-treating-private-personal-facebook-data-medical-data-article-1.3891871)
Your personal Facebook data "is sensitive, and deserves to be treated that way. We should change the laws around how we in the United States protect health-related data to include these categories of sensitive social media data about you," argues Luke Stark (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/lstark)  in this piece from the NY Daily News (http://www.nydailynews.com/opinion/start-treating-private-personal-facebook-data-medical-data-article-1.3891871) . Stark also recently wrote about this topic in The Conversation (https://theconversation.com/dont-quit-facebook-but-dont-trust-it-either-93776) and about Canada's relationship to AI in this piece from the (https://www.theglobeandmail.com/opinion/article-canadas-risky-bet-on-ai/) Globe and Mail (https://www.theglobeandmail.com/opinion/article-canadas-risky-bet-on-ai/) .

https://www.cnn.com/2018/03/26/opinions/data-company-spying-opinion-schneier/index.html

It's Not Just Facebook. Thousands of Companies are Spying on You (https://www.cnn.com/2018/03/26/opinions/data-company-spying-opinion-schneier/index.html)
"[F]or every article about Facebook's creepy stalker behavior, thousands of other companies are breathing a collective sigh of relief that it's Facebook and not them in the spotlight," writes Bruce Schneier (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/bschneier) , in this piece (https://www.cnn.com/2018/03/26/opinions/data-company-spying-opinion-schneier/index.html)  for CNN.

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-018-0020-x

Expressions of Loss Predict Aggressive Comments on Twitter Among Gang-Involved Youth in Chicago (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-018-0020-x)
"Recent studies suggest social media shapes the transmission of firearm violence in high-poverty, urban neighborhoods. However, the exact pathways by which content on social media becomes threatening has not been studied." Desmond Patton (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/dpatton) 's research in this new co-authored article (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-018-0020-x) , published in Digital Medicine, fills in this gap.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/rebalancing-regulation-of-speech-hyper-local-content-on-global-web-based-platforms-1-386d65d86e32

Rebalancing Regulation of Speech: Hyper-Local Content on Global Web-Based Platforms (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/rebalancing-regulation-of-speech-hyper-local-content-on-global-web-based-platforms-1-386d65d86e32)
"There is too much harmful speech online but too much harmless opinion is being censored. There is more big data on harmful speech than ever before but no information on what the data means. This is the conundrum of our times," writes Chinmayi Arun (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/carun)  in this Medium article (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/rebalancing-regulation-of-speech-hyper-local-content-on-global-web-based-platforms-1-386d65d86e32) .

https://www.hiig.de/en/blog/no-youtubes-trolls-wont-destroy-wikipedia/

No, YouTube’s “Trolls” Won’t Destroy Wikipedia (https://www.hiig.de/en/blog/no-youtubes-trolls-wont-destroy-wikipedia/)
"YouTube’s decision to embed Wikipedia articles under controversial videos caused a huge uproar. Commentators and scholars chastised YouTube for being lazy, for outsourcing their responsibility, or for 'crushing' the online encyclopedia," describes Jonas Kaiser (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jkaiser) , in this blog post from the Alexander von Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society (https://www.hiig.de/en/blog/no-youtubes-trolls-wont-destroy-wikipedia/) .

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/inclusion-in-action-voices-and-perspectives-from-colombia-20d24dab84d9

Inclusion in Action: Voices and Perspectives from Colombia (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/inclusion-in-action-voices-and-perspectives-from-colombia-20d24dab84d9)
"Inclusion is a key process for sustaining, developing, and building democratic societies. Crossing multiple social dimensions, inclusion can help to ensure more equal opportunities for participation and access to markets, services, and spaces," explains Andres Lombana-Bermudez (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/alombana)  in this Medium post (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/inclusion-in-action-voices-and-perspectives-from-colombia-20d24dab84d9) .

https://medium.com/@CASBS/adventures-in-applied-data-science-7aefd34026ec

Adventures in Applied Data Science (https://medium.com/@CASBS/adventures-in-applied-data-science-7aefd34026ec)
"Many organizations have unprecedented access to data, experiments, and statistical inference. The diffusion of these resources has created pressure to develop the skills and practices necessary to use them," writes Aaron Shaw (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ashaw) in this Medium post (https://medium.com/@CASBS/adventures-in-applied-data-science-7aefd34026ec) . "However, the distribution of these skills and practices has an organizational component, leading some teams and organizations to harness social scientific insights far more effectively than others."
