From Westworld to Best World for the Internet of Things (https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/03/opinion/westworld-internet-of-things.html)
"While procrastination around security has been vital to the expansion of the internet, 'later' doesn’t mean 'never,'" explains Jonathan Zittrain (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jzittrain) in this NY Times piece (https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/03/opinion/westworld-internet-of-things.html) . "We can create incentives to design networked devices for both interoperability and safety, and to plan for remediation when some things inevitably go wrong."  (Here's a brief summary in this Twitter thread (https://twitter.com/zittrain/status/1004003120471527425) .)

What Could Tech Actually Do for Journalism (http://blogs.harvard.edu/workingpaper/2018/06/03/tech-for-journalism/)
In this blog post (http://blogs.harvard.edu/workingpaper/2018/06/03/tech-for-journalism/) , Andrew Gruen (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/agruen)  summarizes the recent discussion that he and Yvonne MacPherson (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ymacpherson)  led with BKC Fellows about how tech companies could potentially help today's journalism industry.

https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2018/jun/01/gdpr-data-protection-laws-shine-light-industry

Data Protection Laws Are Shining a Needed Light on a Secretive Industry (https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2018/jun/01/gdpr-data-protection-laws-shine-light-industry)
In this article (https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2018/jun/01/gdpr-data-protection-laws-shine-light-industry) for The Guardian, Bruce Schneier (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/bschneier) describes some new data protection laws aimed to regulate data brokers. "These new laws, and the ones that will follow, are slowly shining a light on this secretive industry," he writes.

New Technologies Won’t Reduce Scarcity, but Here’s Something That Might (https://hbr.org/2018/06/new-technologies-wont-reduce-scarcity-but-heres-something-that-might?utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=hbr)
"As the global community becomes more aware of how their abundance is dependent on other human beings and the stability of environments, more and more will see commons-based businesses as the way of the future," write Vasilis Kostakis (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/vkostakis)  and Andreas Roos, in this article (https://hbr.org/2018/06/new-technologies-wont-reduce-scarcity-but-heres-something-that-might?utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=hbr) from Harvard Business Review.
