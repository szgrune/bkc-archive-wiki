On Display: The Laughing Room, an AI powered experience
https://metalabharvard.github.io/projects/laughingroom/
The Laughing Room & The Control Room (https://metalabharvard.github.io/projects/laughingroom/)
at MIT & the Cambridge Public Library from November 16-18
Opening Reception 11/16 at 4pm
Artist Talkback 11/17 at 3pm

From metaLAB (https://metalabharvard.github.io) and BKC alum Jonny Sun (https://twitter.com/jonnysun) , THE LAUGHING ROOM (https://metalabharvard.github.io/projects/laughingroom/) is an interactive art installation in which participants enter an artificially intelligent room, designed to resemble a sitcom set, that plays a laugh track whenever the participants say something that the room’s algorithm deems to be worth laughing with.

The Laughing Room is connected to a machine learning algorithm trained on large dataset of audio transcripts of stand-up comedians. The algorithm will determine the patterns that precede laughter, and then react accordingly to the voices of the live participants.

The piece will also be recorded and live-streamed via video camera and audio to THE CONTROL ROOM, hosted in parallel at the MIT Libraries.

Buying your First AI or “Never Trust a Used Algorithm Salesman” (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/buying-your-first-ai-136cd2e6dd2)
"There was a time when more of us knew how to change the oil and change a timing belt, keep an engine running and fix the brakes. Cars got more complicated and we got less mechanical, but one thing hasn’t changed: if you are buying a used car, you can’t trust the seller to tell you what’s wrong with it," writes BKC affiliate Gretchen Greene (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/gretchen-greene)  in this Medium post (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/buying-your-first-ai-136cd2e6dd2) . "If you’re a government official buying an algorithm, whether marketed as predictive analytics, AI, or machine learning, the same thing is true. It’s great if you have a mechanic you trust. It’s even better if you also have some idea of what to look for yourself."
