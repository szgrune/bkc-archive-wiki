# Cybersecurity Originalism, or What Ben Franklin and John Jay Would Have Thought about Signal

**Source:** [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=70xgmgcUuTM) · **Published:** 2025-05-12 · **By:** Berkman Klein Center · **Feed tags:** bkc-video

> Our contemporary debates about cybersecurity, surveillance and the law are steeped in 21st century technology, but the problem of interception is not new.  Surveillance and information warfare played an essential role in the American revolution and in the negotiations that led to independence.  Ben Franklin, John Jay and other founders participated in these dark arts, with varying degrees of success.  This talk explores this history and considers what it means for us today.
