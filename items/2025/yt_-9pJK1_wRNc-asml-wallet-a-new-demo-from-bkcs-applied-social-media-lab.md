# ASML Wallet: A New Demo from BKC's Applied Social Media Lab

**Source:** [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-9pJK1_wRNc) · **Published:** 2025-09-23 · **By:** Berkman Klein Center · **Feed tags:** bkc-video

> Hear directly from engineers of the  Applied Social Media Lab (ASML) about their approaches to work and goals for the program’s second year, including safety, privacy, and public interest AI integration into social media.
> 
> Brendan Miller and Alberto Leon present ASML Wallet, a way of providing verifiable credentials for open social media platforms. Such credentials can, as the two examples demonstrated here show, (1) allow users to link their social media accounts to bolster trust and credibility, and (2) share mandatory information (e.g., to comply with age verification laws) while preserving users' privacy.
