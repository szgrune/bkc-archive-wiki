** Better recommenders (https://kgi.georgetown.edu/research-and-commentary/better-feeds/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Faculty Associates Arvind Narayanan and Elissa Redmiles and Affiliates Aviv Ovadya and Nathaniel Lubin contribute to a report that offers guidance for improving recommendation algorithms “through detailed design transparency, user choices and defaults, and assessments of long-term impact.” Read here » (https://kgi.georgetown.edu/research-and-commentary/better-feeds/)

https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13575

** DMCA versus NCIM (https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13575)
------------------------------------------------------------

A team of researchers, including RSM Visiting Scholar Eric Gilbert, used data from BKC’s Lumen database to highlight “the DMCA’s ineffectiveness in addressing NCIM [non-consensual intimate media] removals.” Their paper notes “the need for targeted NCIM legislation...to better protect NCIM victim-survivors.” Read here » (https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13575)

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qjWfrvMi-aI

** TikTok threat? (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qjWfrvMi-aI)
------------------------------------------------------------

BKC co-founder Jonathan Zittrain moderated a debate between Alan Rozenshtein and Faculty Associate Anupam Chander on the law requiring ByteDance to sell TikTok to a US firm or face a ban. About the Supreme Court's decision to uphold the law, Chander predicted, “This is a constitutional decision that will reverberate for the next century.” Watch here » (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qjWfrvMi-aI)

Plus, the debate was written up in the Harvard Gazette (https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2025/02/did-the-tiktok-ban-go-too-far/) .
