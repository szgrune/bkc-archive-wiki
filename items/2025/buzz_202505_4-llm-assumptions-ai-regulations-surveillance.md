In The Atlantic, Faculty Director Jonathan Zittrain probes policy questions raised by the “assumptions” large language models make about users. “Gleaning a model’s assumptions is just the beginning. To the extent that its generalizations and stereotyping can be accurately measured, it is possible to try to insist to the model that it ‘believe’ something different.” Read here » (https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2025/05/inside-the-ai-black-box/682853/?gift=GFVsesv7yr2B_e8FpoMTZ1YHTxzxzpBN6k87fsIFHxw)

** AI regulation map (https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13673)
------------------------------------------------------------

With Sacha Alanoca and Kevin Klyman, Affiliates Shira Gur-Arieh and Tom Zick “present a taxonomy to map the global landscape of AI regulation.” “[T]his taxonomy serves as a tool which can resist time and political change, to offer a common ground of understanding and inform future governance initiatives.” Read here » (https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13673)

** AI-generated law (https://foreignpolicy.com/2025/05/14/ai-generated-law-uae-legislation/?tpcc=gifting_article&gifting_article=YWktZ2VuZXJhdGVkLWxhdy11YWUtbGVnaXNsYXRpb24=&pid=PNILoiIJgqmxsxl)
------------------------------------------------------------

In Foreign Policy, Affiliates Bruce Schneier and Nathan Sanders take up recent proposals to use AI to draft laws. “The primary concern with using AI in lawmaking is that it will be wielded as a tool by the powerful to advance their own interests. AI may not fundamentally change lawmaking, but its superhuman capabilities have the potential to exacerbate the risks of power concentration.” Read here » (https://foreignpolicy.com/2025/05/14/ai-generated-law-uae-legislation/?tpcc=gifting_article&gifting_article=YWktZ2VuZXJhdGVkLWxhdy11YWUtbGVnaXNsYXRpb24=&pid=PNILoiIJgqmxsxl)

** AI surveillance (https://www.usnews.com/opinion/articles/2025-05-20/ai-trump-women-iran-nuclear-deal)
------------------------------------------------------------

Faculty Associate Ibtissam Bouachrine calls for political pressure to combat the use of AI-powered surveillance for enforcing dress code laws in Iran. “The fear that women already experience on the street, at the beach, inside a store or on social media is being transformed to constant paranoia under the ever-watchful eyes of AI surveillance.” Read here » (https://www.usnews.com/opinion/articles/2025-05-20/ai-trump-women-iran-nuclear-deal)

** BKC Happenings
------------------------------------------------------------

Fellow Ben Brooks (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ben-brooks) testified before the Rhode Island Senate Committee on Artificial Intelligence & Emerging Tech about the state’s proposed Artificial Intelligence Act and setbacks to proposals in other jurisdictions. Watch the hearing here (https://capitoltvri.cablecast.tv/show/11240) (Ben's testimony begins at 35:30) or read Ben's full remarks here (https://www.rilegislature.gov/senators/SenateComDocs/Artificial%20Intelligence%20%20Emerging%20Technologies/S0627%20Ben%20Brooks.pdf) .

Last week, Jonathan Zittrain (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jzittrain) , BKC Faculty Director and head of the HLS Library, spoke with NPR about the discovery of an authentic Magna Carta (https://www.npr.org/2025/05/15/nx-s1-5399171/magna-carta-harvard) (!) in Harvard's holdings.

Earlier in the semester, the Cyberlaw Clinic (https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/) celebrated 25 years of pro-bono legal services with a gathering of past and present students and instructors (https://hls.harvard.edu/today/a-milestone-in-the-pioneering-work-of-the-cyberlaw-clinic/) .

Outside their work with the Clinic, Clinical Instructor Alejandra Caraballo (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/alejandra-caraballo) recently launched an online publication, The Dissident (https://www.thedissident.news/the-digital-panopticon-nightmare/) , focused on power, technology, and civil rights, and Clinical Instructor Mason Kortz (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/mkortz) participated in an online discussion hosted by MuckRock about DOGE and government transparency (https://www.muckrock.com/news/archives/2025/may/14/meet-the-experts-working-to-pull-the-curtain-on-doge/) .
