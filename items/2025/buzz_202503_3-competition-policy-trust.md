** Trust issues (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-025-02294-x)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a new study, Faculty Associate Gregory Gondwe explores trends in African audiences' engagement with AI-generated news, illustrating demographic differences. Younger users are more receptive, while concerns about bias and transparency emphasize the importance of digital literacy and culturally relevant AI journalism. Read here » (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-025-02294-x)
