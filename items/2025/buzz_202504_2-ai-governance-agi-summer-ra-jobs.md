** Arms race (https://schedule.sxsw.com/2025/events/PP154559)
------------------------------------------------------------

At SXSW 2025, Faculty Associate Ifeoma Ajunwa, Affiliate Julie Owono, and Satwik Mishra explored how, amid an AI arms race, marginalized and global communities are crafting alternatives to top-down AI regulation. They offered grounded, collaborative models to guide AI toward the public good. Listen here » (https://schedule.sxsw.com/2025/events/PP154559)

** AGI agenda (https://www.sciencefriday.com/segments/artificial-general-intelligence-future/)
------------------------------------------------------------

On Science Friday, Knight Responsible AI Fellow Rumman Chowdhury emphasizes that Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is less about technological milestones and more about who defines intelligence and why. For Chowdhury, the future of AGI must center people—not just “progress.” Listen here » (https://www.sciencefriday.com/segments/artificial-general-intelligence-future/)

------------------------------------------------------------

Affiliate Bruce Schneier draws from a series of workshops to think through how democracy might be reimagined for today’s world. “Modern representative democracy was the best form of government that eighteenth-century technology could invent. The twenty-first century is very different: scientifically, technically, and philosophically.” Read here » (https://read.dukeupress.edu/common-knowledge/article/30/3/354/398268/Reimagining-Democracy?guestAccessKey=a65030ce-d698-4ee6-80aa-068effaec6d3)
