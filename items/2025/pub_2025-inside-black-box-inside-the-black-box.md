> Inside the Black Boxintroduces an interpretability dashboard developed by researchers at the Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society and Harvard’s Insight and Interaction Lab to make visible the internal assumptions AI systems form about users. By revealing how chatbots infer characteristics such as age, gender, race, and socioeconomic status, the tool sheds light on how these hidden models shape AI responses.
