** LLM v. ordinary meaning (https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/judges-shouldn-t-rely-on-ai-for-the-ordinary-meaning-of-text)
------------------------------------------------------------

Cyberlaw Clinic student Justin Curl and co-authors break down why judges should not use large language models to ascertain “ordinary meaning” in statutory interpretation. “Judges who outsource a key part of the decision-making process to third parties are not fulfilling their constitutional role.” Read in Lawfare » (https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/judges-shouldn-t-rely-on-ai-for-the-ordinary-meaning-of-text)

https://nyaspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nyas.15345

** Harm abatement (https://nyaspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nyas.15345)
------------------------------------------------------------

In Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences, Affiliates Nathaniel Lubin and Amanda Yarnell and co-authors look at social media in public health terms and argue that “court-based remedies—through both court order or negotiated settlement—can be a useful public health tool as a supplement or alternative to law and regulation imposed by state or federal regulators.” Read on arXiv » (https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10458)

https://theconversation.com/lady-gaga-bomb-plot-thwarted-plan-lifts-veil-on-the-gamification-of-hate-and-gendered-nature-of-online-radicalization-256199

** Radicalization (https://theconversation.com/lady-gaga-bomb-plot-thwarted-plan-lifts-veil-on-the-gamification-of-hate-and-gendered-nature-of-online-radicalization-256199)
------------------------------------------------------------

A bomb plot at a Lady Gaga concert in Brazil is the jumping-off point for Faculty Associate David Nemer and Arthur Coelho Bezerra's comment on the dynamics of online misogyny and the gamification of hate speech. “The future of a generation cannot be built on algorithms that reward hate and radicalization.” Read in The Conversation » (https://theconversation.com/lady-gaga-bomb-plot-thwarted-plan-lifts-veil-on-the-gamification-of-hate-and-gendered-nature-of-online-radicalization-256199)

https://www.techpolicy.press/why-small-businesses-must-not-get-left-behind-in-the-ai-boom/

** Small business & AI (https://www.techpolicy.press/why-small-businesses-must-not-get-left-behind-in-the-ai-boom/)
------------------------------------------------------------

With Camille Stewart Gloster, Affiliate Afua Bruce argues that, because “small businesses are integral to the global economy,” efforts must be made to help them adopt AI responsibly. “If they are left behind...the repercussions could be significant, not only for individual businesses but for society as a whole.” Read in Tech Policy Press » (https://www.techpolicy.press/why-small-businesses-must-not-get-left-behind-in-the-ai-boom/)

https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2025.1538227/full

** Commons governance (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2025.1538227/full)
------------------------------------------------------------

Faculty Associate Mark Esposito, Terence Tse, and Danny Goh consider the prospects of blockchain tech for community resource management. “Whether blockchain becomes a force for democratization or digital enclosure...will depend on how its governing architectures are designed, contested, and evolved by the communities that steward them.” Read in Frontiers » (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2025.1538227/full)
