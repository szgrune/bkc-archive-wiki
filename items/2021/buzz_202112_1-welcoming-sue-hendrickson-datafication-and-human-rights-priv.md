** Datafication and Human Rights (https://www.openglobalrights.org/what-a-datafied-worldview-means-for-human-rights/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Juan Ortiz Freuler sketches the challenges of datafication for human rights: “It is crucial for human rights practitioners to understand these processes and help shape them, guiding or combatting their designs and deployment as we move into the future.”

https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3970890

** What is Privacy? That's the Wrong Question (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3970890)
------------------------------------------------------------

Reflecting on Daniel Solove’s work, Woodrow Hartzog writes that “Privacy is still a concept in disarray. But that’s okay. There is now too much data that is collected by too many different entities and used in too many different ways for any singular definition of privacy to be legally useful anyway.”

https://www.lumendatabase.org/blog_entries/lumen-announces-new-submission-tool

** Easy Collection and Sharing of Takedown Notices (https://www.lumendatabase.org/blog_entries/lumen-announces-new-submission-tool)
------------------------------------------------------------

Lumen, the BKC project that collects requests to remove online material, has a created a clever bit of code that makes it easy for any online service provider (OSP) to receive takedown requests in a structured form and submit them into Lumen’s database. OSPs, give it a try! Researchers and the curious, the database is a great tool for investigating the Web’s takedown landscape; come through and search!
