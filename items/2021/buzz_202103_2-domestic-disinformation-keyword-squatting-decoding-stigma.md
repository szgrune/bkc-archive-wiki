** The Breakdown: Lisa Kaplan on domestic disinformation (https://youtu.be/9uHfTNR2-QQ)
------------------------------------------------------------

In this episode of The Breakdown, Oumou Ly is joined by Lisa Kaplan, founder of the Alethea Group, to unpack what accounts for the shift in focus from foreign to domestic disinformation and assess how current organizing around the disinformation problem can be reevaluated and repurposed to suit new challenges.

** How a racialized disinformation campaign ties itself to The 1619 Project (https://www.cjr.org/opinion/1776-keyword-squatting-right-wing-media.php)
------------------------------------------------------------

Joan Donovan and Brandi Collins-Dexter explain how right-wing media enabled a disinformation campaign around The 1619 Project. Using Media Cloud, they illustrate how the right used “keyword squatting” to associate their disinformation campaign with the project.

https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/decoding-stigma-designing-sex-worker-liberatory-futures

** Event: Designing for Sex Worker Liberatory Futures (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/decoding-stigma-designing-sex-worker-liberatory-futures)
------------------------------------------------------------

What would the Internet look like if it was designed by sex workers? Co-hosted by BKC, this panel offers new ways for regulators, ethicists, policy-makers, and technologists to think about community standards, technologies of violence, data privacy, online safety, and virtual intimacies, and will explore how we might code sex worker ethics into future design. Learn more and register. (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/decoding-stigma-designing-sex-worker-liberatory-futures)
