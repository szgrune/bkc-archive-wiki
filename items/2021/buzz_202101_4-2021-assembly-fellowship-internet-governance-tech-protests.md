The Latest

** Assembly announces 2021 Assembly Fellowship cohort (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2021-01/bkc-assembly-announces-2021-assembly-fellowship-cohort)
------------------------------------------------------------

Five teams from inside and outside academia return to Assembly to further develop interventions in the areas of disinformation, cybersecurity, and ethical AI.

** Gaining power, losing control (https://today.law.harvard.edu/gaining-power-losing-control/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jonathan Zittrain delivered the 2020 Clare Hall Tanner Lectures on Human Values last year. The first lecture explores the tension between free speech and public health online, and explains three eras of Internet governance: a “Rights” era, a “Public Health” era, and a “Legitimacy” era.

** Silicon Valley workers have had enough (https://www.nytimes.com/2021/01/26/opinion/silicon-valley-tech-workers.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

Moira Weigel co-authors an op-ed describing the collective action from within tech platforms. “With Mr. Trump’s departure from office, the sense of crisis that proved so mobilizing — and unifying — may fade. But the contracts and conditions that workers have protested remain in place, as do the networks they have formed.”
