------------------------------------------------------------

A new literature review in the HKS Misinformation Review finds gaps in the pre-Covid research on vaccine hesitancy in online spaces. The lit review, which emerged from BKC’s Misinformation Working Group, also identifies new paths for research.

https://techcrunch.com/2021/10/14/building-better-startups-with-responsible-ai/

** Building Better Startups with Responsible AI (https://techcrunch.com/2021/10/14/building-better-startups-with-responsible-ai/)
------------------------------------------------------------

For TechCrunch, affiliate Tom Zick offers five practical suggestions for building responsible AI systems that are also good for business. "If any AI will inevitably fail, then — like with a bureaucracy — we must keep humans in the loop and design with them in mind."

https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2021/10/problem-underestimating-tiktok/620354/

** The Problem with Underestimating TikTok (https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2021/10/problem-underestimating-tiktok/620354/)
------------------------------------------------------------

evelyn douek writes in The Atlantic that TikTok has managed to dodge government scrutiny because politicians have failed to take it seriously. "TikTok is all too often regarded as an unserious thing to write or read about. That’s a mistake, and it’s one that Congress is making as well."
