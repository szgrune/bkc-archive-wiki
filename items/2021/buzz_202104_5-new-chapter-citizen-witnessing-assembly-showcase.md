** Urs Gasser to embark on new role across the Atlantic (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2021-04/urs-gasser-embark-new-role-across-atlantic)
------------------------------------------------------------

BKC is pleased to announce that Urs Gasser will be named this fall to an inaugural professorship of public policy, governance, and innovative technology at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). This is bittersweet news, as Gasser will step down as Executive Director this summer after twelve years of service.

** Event: Assembly Project Fellowship Showcase (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/assembly-project-fellowship-showcase)
------------------------------------------------------------

To celebrate the fifth year of the program, Assembly (https://www.berkmankleinassembly.org/) invited five ongoing alumni project teams to return (virtually) to BKC to participate in the Assembly Project Fellowship. Join them for a final showcase, moderated by Jonathan Zittrain, on Monday, May 17th at 4pm ET. Learn more and register. (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/assembly-project-fellowship-showcase)
