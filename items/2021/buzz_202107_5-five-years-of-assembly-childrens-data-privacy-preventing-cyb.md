** Celebrating Five Years of the Assembly Program (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/five-years-of-assembly-e52809c41749)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Assembly Program (https://www.berkmankleinassembly.org/) explored topics like disinformation, produced a variety of prototypes and projects, and fostered a community that spanned across sectors and disciplines. "Assembly was the most interesting collection of people I’ve ever met working on some of the hardest, most important problems on the internet," said John Hess.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/why-all-data-governance-needs-to-consider-childrens-rights-8b218a825a08

** Data Governance and Children's Rights (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/why-all-data-governance-needs-to-consider-childrens-rights-8b218a825a08)
------------------------------------------------------------

Emma Day shares insights about how children's rights complicate data privacy issues — and urges governments to take steps to protect children online. "Children need to be afforded the agency to define who they are for themselves, without having their future pathways predetermined or their learning styles unduly narrowed down by algorithms."

https://www.lawfareblog.com/why-current-botnet-takedown-jurisprudence-should-not-be-replicated

** How to Reform Botnet Jurisprudence  (https://www.lawfareblog.com/why-current-botnet-takedown-jurisprudence-should-not-be-replicated)
------------------------------------------------------------

Asaf Lubin and João Marinotti explain the problems with current Congressional efforts to prevent cybercrime for Lawfare. "Not only are the current tools to fight against botnets ineffective, but the entire botnet-takedown ecosystem is flawed."
