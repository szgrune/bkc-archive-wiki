The Latest

** Deconstructing the ‘Karen’ meme (https://today.law.harvard.edu/deconstructing-the-karen-meme/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Apryl Williams put “Karen” memes in historical and cultural context during a BKC event on White Surveillance and Black Digital Publics (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/white-surveillance-and-black-digital-publics)  moderated by Allissa V. Richardson. “The power of memes is they connect the dots for people. They make it really easy to see that there’s a pattern,” Williams said. “It’s not just this one instance.”

** The Coronavirus Variants and The Vaccine (https://kpfa.org/episode/letters-and-politics-february-1-2021/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Margaret Bordeaux, Co-chair of the Berkman Klein Center’s Policy Practice: Digital Pandemic Response (https://cyber.harvard.edu/programs/bkc-policy-practice-digital-pandemic-response) Working Group, joined Letters and Politics talk show to discuss the COVID-19 vaccine.

** Event: Marginalized Women, Technology, COVID-19, and Intimate Partner Violence (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/marginalized-women-technology-covid-19-and-intimate-partner-violence)
------------------------------------------------------------

This event brings together practitioners and experts in law, psychology, technology, religion, communication, and ethics to discuss the concerns specific to intimate partner violence.  Learn more and register (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/marginalized-women-technology-covid-19-and-intimate-partner-violence) .
