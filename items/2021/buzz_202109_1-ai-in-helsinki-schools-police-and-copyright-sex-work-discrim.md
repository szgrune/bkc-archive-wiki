** Financial Discrimination Against Sex Work (https://tryst.link/blog/financial-discrimination-against-sex-workers-and-what-we-can-do-about-it/amp/?__twitter_impression=true)
------------------------------------------------------------

Zahra Stardust explains how the financial industry uses incorrect assumptions about sex work to unfairly deny workers service, for Tryst. "For the many sex workers who are sole traders, policies that refuse financial services to sex workers make it extremely difficult to access digital economies, to get paid, and to maintain a livelihood."
