The Latest

** International Program on Digital Ethics Explores COVID-19 and Education (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2021-02/research-sprint-recap-international-program-digital-ethics-explores-covid-19-and)
------------------------------------------------------------

At a moment of unprecedented global challenges, our three-month research sprint, "Digital Ethics in Times of Crisis: COVID-19 & Access to Education and Learning Spaces (https://cyber.harvard.edu/projects/digital-ethics-times-crisis-covid-19-access-education-and-learning-spaces) ," focused on the societal and ethical aspects of digital transformation and learning. Part of a series, the BKC-hosted sprint followed one hosted by the Alexander von Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society and is part of a larger initiative with the Network of Centers (https://networkofcenters.net) conducted under the patronage of the German Federal President and supported by Stiftung Mercator. Nearly 40 student participants from 21 countries spread over five continents joined the global effort.
https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2021/digital-ethics-times-crisis-covid-19-and-access-education-and-learning-spaces

** Publication: Digital Ethics in Times of Crisis (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2021/digital-ethics-times-crisis-covid-19-and-access-education-and-learning-spaces)
------------------------------------------------------------

The sprint produced an issues map – a concise synthesis of the program’s sessions – to advance dialogue at the intersection of science, politics, digital economy, and civil society.

https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2021-02/call-participants
