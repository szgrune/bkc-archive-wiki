------------------------------------------------------------

A BKC event reflected on early platforms, the state of newer platforms today, and what they could look like in the future. Panelists discussed topics including subcultures, content moderation, and the evolution of technology. Check out an excerpt from the event and watch and listen to the full conversation.

https://thehill.com/opinion/healthcare/544866-funding-black-and-latinx-community-institutions-is-the-key-to-vaccine

** Funding Black and Latinx community institutions is the key to vaccine equity (https://thehill.com/opinion/healthcare/544866-funding-black-and-latinx-community-institutions-is-the-key-to-vaccine)
------------------------------------------------------------

Mary Gray co-authors an op-ed in The Hill: “If we don’t learn our lesson now, the COVID-19 vaccination rush will come and go. Our public health crises will persist and we’re bound to find ourselves back where we started, perpetuating the economic, social, education and personal inequity that characterized this past year.”

https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/covid-19-margins

** Event: COVID-19 from the Margins (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/covid-19-margins)
------------------------------------------------------------

In the first pandemic of the datafied society, the disempowered were denied a voice in the heavily quantified mainstream narrative. On March 30 at 12 p.m. ET, panelists will explore the pandemic from the perspective of communities and individuals at the margins in the Global South and beyond. Learn more and register (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/covid-19-margins) .
