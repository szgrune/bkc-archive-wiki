** Have Trump’s Lies Wrecked Free Speech? (https://www.nytimes.com/2021/01/06/opinion/trump-lies-free-speech.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

A debate has broken out over whether the once-sacrosanct constitutional protection of the First Amendment has become a threat to democracy. Rebecca Tushnet weighs in on the question in the New York Times.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/movement-lawyering-for-alternative-futures-404ba2010bf2

** Movement Lawyering for Alternative Futures (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/movement-lawyering-for-alternative-futures-404ba2010bf2)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a post for the BKC Medium Collection, Roslyn M. Satchel, Afsaneh Rigot, Kendra Albert, Micaela Mantegna, and Zahra Stardust “speak about their vexed relationship to the law, tensions in their practice, their strategies and tactics, and the kinds of futures they are trying to build.”

https://www.ifrfa.org/about

** Law Students: Apply for the Initiative for a Representative First Amendment (https://www.ifrfa.org/about)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Initiative for a Representative First Amendment (IfRFA), based out of the Cyberlaw Clinic, provides financial support and career opportunities for law students from backgrounds traditionally underrepresented in First Amendment law. To learn more about the IfRFA fellowship, watch a conversation (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=713UMSJnfok)  between IfRFA Director Kendra Albert and two of the first year cohort fellows about their experiences.
Applications are due on January 11, 2021.
