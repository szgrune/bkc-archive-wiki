** The Push for Infrastructure Moderation (https://www.techdirt.com/articles/20210924/12012347622/inexorable-push-infrastructure-moderation.shtml)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jonathan Zittrain writes in the Tech Policy Greenhouse that the fervor for content moderation will bleed from social media into the Internet's building blocks, posing new threats to civil liberties. "Once the value or necessity of moderation is accepted at the application layer, the inevitable leakiness of it will push the same kinds of decisions onto providers of infrastructure."

** Researching Twitter's Image Cropping Algorithm  (https://www.morningbrew.com/emerging-tech/stories/2021/09/27/behind-the-scenes-of-twitter-s-decision-to-open-up-its-image-cropping-algorithm-to-researchers)
------------------------------------------------------------

Twitter’s recent opening of its image-cropping algorithm is a step forward in social media research, Sasha Costanza-Chock tells Morning Brew. But, they add, internal “bias and harms teams” are also needed to create a “robust, equitable, and accountable life cycle for AI development.”

https://money.com/what-is-cyberbullying-insurance/

** How does Cyberbullying Insurance Work? (https://money.com/what-is-cyberbullying-insurance/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sameer Hinduja shares insight on the psychological damage that online harassment can inflict on victims — and the limitations of cyberbullying insurance — in Money. "I wouldn’t want the availability of insurance to make society lazy or irresponsible with regard to doing their part to stay safe online."

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/big-brother-a-critique-of-the-4th-industrial-revolution-in-africa-fdcd1a5fdddd

** AI and the Fourth Industrial Revolution in Africa (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/big-brother-a-critique-of-the-4th-industrial-revolution-in-africa-fdcd1a5fdddd)
------------------------------------------------------------

Tunde Okunoye offers a critical view of Africa's latest developments in artificial intelligence and its uses. "Investment in transportation, agriculture, healthcare, and education should take priority over investment in surveillance technologies."
