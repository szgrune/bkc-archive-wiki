The Latest

** Inside the Assembly Student Fellowship (https://medium.com/@Assembly_Program/inside-the-assembly-student-fellowship-3ee6f2d5549e)
------------------------------------------------------------

This year, 19 students from 10 schools across Harvard University joined the Assembly Student Fellowship, to study disinformation on online platforms, from elections and public health to the forces that drive it. Throughout the year, the cohort participates in a seminar series, learning with experts in the field. Take a peek inside the program.

** Surveillance and the ‘New Normal’ of Covid-19: (https://covid19research.ssrc.org/public-health-surveillance-and-human-rights-network/report/)
------------------------------------------------------------

A report from the Social Science Research Council offers insights from a cross-disciplinary, multinational group of more than 40 experts, including Emma Day, Camille François, Urs Gasser, Hilary Ross, and Jonathan Zittrain, on the broader context of this historical moment for data ethics.

** Brandeis Marshall on the potential for data equity (https://digital.hbs.edu/data-and-analysis/brandeis-marshall-on-the-potential-for-data-equity/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Brandeis Marshall was interviewed by Harvard Business School’s Digital Initiative about the consequences of data inequity, the balancing act of qualitative and quantitative mindsets, and the critical importance of humanizing data systems.

** Event: Digital Witnesses (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/digital-witnesses-power-looking)
------------------------------------------------------------

Hannane Ferdjani, Nana Mgbechikwere Nwachukwu, and Allissa Richardson explore how young Black people around the world are utilizing tech tools to track and circumvent oppressive policies by repressive governments. The panelists will discuss how Black people of Nigeria, Uganda, and the United States are leveraging social media platforms for artistic expressions on political and social issues in their countries. Learn more and register (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/digital-witnesses-power-looking) .
