** Databases Should Facilitate Access to Public Information (https://blogs.harvard.edu/cyberlawclinic/2021/09/02/clinic-represents-media-orgs-in-foia-amicus/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In an amicus brief on behalf of the Center for Investigative Reporting, the Media Law Resource Center, and the MuckRock Foundation, the Cyberlaw Clinic (https://blogs.harvard.edu/cyberlawclinic/) argues that information in an ICE database should not be exempt from a FOIA request. "ICE wants to be able to lock up its data and throw away the key–or at least withhold the key as exempt."

https://lumendatabase-org.medium.com/apples-proposed-new-child-safety-features-and-the-problems-with-privacy-tradeoffs-1c8973c92bae

** Apple's Proposed Child Safety Features and Privacy Tradeoffs (https://lumendatabase-org.medium.com/apples-proposed-new-child-safety-features-and-the-problems-with-privacy-tradeoffs-1c8973c92bae)
------------------------------------------------------------

Lumen (https://cyber.harvard.edu/research/lumen) 's (https://cyber.harvard.edu/research/lumen)  Shreya Tewari shares the problems with proposals that compromise user privacy in favor of other important goals. There's "ample proof," Tewari writes, that "de-encryption and on-device tracking has a history of being misused."

https://fortune.com/2021/09/08/digital-privacy-patriot-act-9-11/

** Digital Surveillance Since 9/11 (https://fortune.com/2021/09/08/digital-privacy-patriot-act-9-11/)
------------------------------------------------------------

For Fortune, David O'Brien explains how the two decades since the September 11 terrorist attacks have involved a broad expansion of government surveillance powers. Discovering the extent of the government surveillance was like a "nuclear bomb" for tech companies, O'Brien said. "They were just absolutely livid."
