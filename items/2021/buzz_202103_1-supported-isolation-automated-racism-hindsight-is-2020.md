** Organizing, Budgeting, and Implementing Wraparound Services for People in Quarantine and Isolation (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2021-03/organizing-budgeting-and-implementing-wraparound-services-people-quarantine-and)
------------------------------------------------------------

Could spending money to support those who have been exposed or tested positive for COVID help states save in the long run? A primer from the Digital Pandemic Response Policy Practice (https://cyber.harvard.edu/programs/bkc-policy-practice-digital-pandemic-response)   explains how investing in supported isolation will not only save lives but also has the potential to reduce overall public costs (https://www.bostonglobe.com/2021/03/03/opinion/massachusetts-should-pay-people-quarantine/) . The memo also explores  the ways that New York State and the Navajo Nation were able to bootstrap innovative supported isolation and quarantine (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/organizing-budgeting-and-implementing-wraparound-services-people-quarantine-and-isolation) using a combination of community-based, local, state, and federal resources.
