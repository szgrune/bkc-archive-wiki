** Youth Participation in a Digital World (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2021/youth-participation-in-a-digital-world)
------------------------------------------------------------

Are you passionate about amplifying the youth voice? Would you like to meaningfully include youth in the work you do but are unsure where to start? Youth Participation in a Digital World: Designing and Implementing Spaces, Programs, and Methodologies seeks to share Youth and Media’s initial insights around ways different stakeholders — such as international organizations, companies, researchers, and educators — can build participation models that enable meaningful youth engagement in our digital world. The paper highlights four youth participation models: youth labs, learning and co-design spaces, youth boards, and participatory research.
