** Keeping Social Media Companies in Check (https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/medialse/2021/10/12/its-time-to-stop-trusting-facebook-to-engineer-our-social-world/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In the Media@LSE blog, Faculty Associate Nick Couldry writes that tighter regulations are needed to ensure that social media companies act in the public interest. "It is not good enough for Facebook now, under huge pressure, to claim credit for the 'reforms' and 'research' it conducted in earlier attempts to mollify an increasingly hostile public."

https://today.law.harvard.edu/is-it-time-to-swipe-left-on-social-media/

** Should We Swipe Left on Social Media? (https://today.law.harvard.edu/is-it-time-to-swipe-left-on-social-media/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jonathan Zittrain, evelyn douek, and John Palfrey share insights in Harvard Law Today on the consequences of social media after a Facebook whistleblower dominated headlines last week. "Regulators are going to have to step up and do something if we are really going to change the status quo.”
