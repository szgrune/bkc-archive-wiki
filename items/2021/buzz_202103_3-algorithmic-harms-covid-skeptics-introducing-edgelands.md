** Europe’s artificial intelligence blindspot: Race (https://www.politico.eu/article/europe-artificial-intelligence-blindspot-race-algorithmic-harm/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Nani Jansen Reventlow discusses algorithmic harms and governance with Politico. “It seems like there's a complete disconnect between reality, which is that automating bias, automating prejudice, automating racism that has huge impacts on huge groups within society, and this blind vision that anything that can be automated is a good thing.”

https://slate.com/technology/2021/03/covid-skeptics-critical-thinking-research.html

** COVID Skeptics Don’t Just Need More Critical Thinking (https://slate.com/technology/2021/03/covid-skeptics-critical-thinking-research.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

Slate interviewed Crystal Lee about her research on COVID skeptics. Lee leads the MIT Visualization Group, which published the work. “There’s a disconnect that is underlying the skepticism that leads [skeptics] to try to reapproach the data, reanalyze and represent the data in a way that makes more sense to them.”

https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/edgelands-institute-launch-event

** Edgelands Institute Launch (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/edgelands-institute-launch-event)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Edgelands Institute is an interdisciplinary pop-up institute to redraw our social contract in our era of mass-urbanization, surveillance, pandemics, and other emergencies. On April 8 at 1 p.m. ET, the institute will introduce its mission, methodology, and, true to its mission, bring together arts and research for an engaging session. Learn more and register. (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/edgelands-institute-launch-event)
