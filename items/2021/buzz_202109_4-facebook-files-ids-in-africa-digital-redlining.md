** How Government Mistrust Impacts Digital IDs in Africa (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9855H9sZN4)
------------------------------------------------------------

For the Alan Turing Institute, reseracher Babatunde Okunoye shares insight on why digital identity projects have faced slow user acceptance and adoption in parts of Africa. "Technologies implemented as developmental objectives reflect the politics or values of governments in power."

https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2021/09/16/chris-gilliard-sees-digital-redlining-in-surveillance-tech/

** Fighting Silicon Valley's Surveillance Machine (https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2021/09/16/chris-gilliard-sees-digital-redlining-in-surveillance-tech/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In an article for the Washington Post about tech-related biases against people of color, Joan Donovan gives her perspective on digital redlining. "The concept of redlining is old ... But it’s often that you have to demonstrate that the digital (realm) doesn’t erase these distinctions, but rather tends to focus them and exacerbate them.”
