** Can America Save the News? (https://www.lawfareblog.com/lawfare-podcast-can-america-save-news)
------------------------------------------------------------

For Lawfare, Martha Minow and evelyn douek discuss the government's role in reviving local news. "The United States ... created a more elaborate protection for public media to be shielded from direct choices about content by the government. But that does not mean — and has never meant — that the government cannot subsidize, pay for, incentivize, reinforce public media."
