** Deplatforming the far-right (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3867818)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a new study by Jonas Kaiser and Adrian Rauchfleisch, looks at the efficacy of taking down Youtube accounts spreading disinformation. “Our analysis shows that deplatforming is effective in minimizing the reach of disinformation and extreme speech, as alternative platforms that will allow this kind of content cannot mitigate the negative effect of being deplatformed on YouTube.”
