** Human Oversight of Government Algorithms: Policy Flaws (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3921216)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a new paper, Ben Green argues that human oversight policies allow the government to use flawed or controversial algorithms without addressing the fundamental issues with the tools — and proposes a new approach for determining how algorithms should be used.

** OnlyFans, Sex Work, and Section 230 (https://www.lumendatabase.org/blog_entries/onlyfans-bans-then-re-allows-pornography-section-230)
------------------------------------------------------------

Lumen (https://lumendatabase.org/) summer research assistant Anna Callahan writes that OnlyFans' recent controversy over hosting online sex work brings the debate around Section 230 (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/the-breakdown-daphne-keller-explains-the-communications-decency-act-84c93775f3b0) into sharp focus. "Legal scholars from both sides of the ideological spectrum have reached the consensus that the amendment, while well intentioned, failed to meaningfully prevent sex trafficking or protect sex workers."
