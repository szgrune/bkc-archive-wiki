** What the ephemerality of the Web means for your hyperlinks (https://www.cjr.org/analysis/linkrot-content-drift-new-york-times.php)
------------------------------------------------------------

A study (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2021/paper-record-meets-ephemeral-web)  by John Bowers, Clare Stanton, and Jonathan Zittrain found substantial linkrot and content drift on the New York Times website from 1996 through mid-2019, exposing the need for new practices, workflows, and technologies.

https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2021-05/building-global-open-access-knowledge-digital-self-determination

** Building global open-access knowledge on Digital Self-Determination (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2021-05/building-global-open-access-knowledge-digital-self-determination)
------------------------------------------------------------

BKC's latest Research Sprint (https://cyber.harvard.edu/projects/digital-self-determination-research-sprint) , co-hosted with Digital Asia Hub, brought together a cohort of 25 students (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2021-03/research-sprint-examines-digital-self-determination-increasingly-interconnected-world) from 21 different countries and six continents to advance our understanding of digital self-determination.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/a-deep-dive-on-digital-self-determination-368c48d87705

** A Q&A with Sprint Participant Rory Torres (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/a-deep-dive-on-digital-self-determination-368c48d87705)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sprint Participant Mary Rhauline “Rory” Torres, Harvard Law LL.M. ’21, joined the sprint from Mangaldan, Pangasinan, in the Philippines. We spoke with Torres about her experience in the program, what she learned, and how it informed her work.
