** How Police Officers Weaponize Copyright (https://www.lumendatabase.org/blog_entries/the-weaponization-of-copyright-by-police-officers-and-the-need-to-automate-fair-use)
------------------------------------------------------------

Lumen researcher Shreya Tewari writes that instances of police officers playing copyrighted music (https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2021/07/02/taylor-swift-california-cop/) to prevent recordings of them from spreading online show that Fair Use must be automated. Today's copyright algorithms, Tewari explains, "are almost by definition ill-equipped to take note of context."

https://a4ai.org/affordability-report/

** Is the Internet affordable? (https://a4ai.org/affordability-report/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sonia Jorge and the Alliance for Affordable Internet assess the obstacles facing internet equity for their annual report. "The Covid-19 pandemic has laid bare the scale and consequences of the digital divide and underlined the urgent need to find solutions to digital inequality."

https://www.lawfareblog.com/lawfare-podcast-facebook-shuts-down-research-itself

** The Challenges with Researching Facebook (https://www.lawfareblog.com/lawfare-podcast-facebook-shuts-down-research-itself)
------------------------------------------------------------

For Lawfare, evelyn douek talks with Quinta Jurecic and Alex Abdo about Facebook's recent moves to discourage independent researchers from collecting data on its website. "The terms of service has a result of inhibiting the work that is urgently needed," Abdo said.
