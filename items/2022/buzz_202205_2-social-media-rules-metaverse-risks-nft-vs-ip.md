** Opportunities, Risks, and Harms in the Metaverse (https://cyberbullying.org/metaverse)
------------------------------------------------------------

A new piece by Sameer Hinduja explores the nuanced social possibilities and pitfalls of immersion in the metaverse. "The reality is we simply don’t yet know a lot about what the possible risks are, and how common they might be."

https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/hermes-nft-artist-clash-over-whether-metabirkins-is-a-trademark

** A Legal Clash over NFTs and Trademarks (https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/hermes-nft-artist-clash-over-whether-metabirkins-is-a-trademark)
------------------------------------------------------------

Representing artist Mason Rothschild, who is being sued by luxury brand Hermès, Rebecca Tushnet worries about the suit's chilling effects. “[Rothschild] creates images of Birkins that don’t exist. He challenges us to say: What is luxury? Why do you value what you value?”

https://protocol.mlml.io/

------------------------------------------------------------

Living by Protocol queries the reflections of artists on, with, and by social media—simultaneously the topic, platform, and space of experimentation. From May 17 through July 3 at Harvard Art Museums' Lightbox Gallery.
