------------------------------------------------------------

Ethan Zukerman reflects on the case of a Nebraska mother and daughter whose private messages about abortion were obtained as evidence in their prosecution. "Given that platforms would face serious consequences were they to systemically refuse to honor legal requests, how should privacy-conscious individuals respond?"

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304422X22000493

** Understanding digital assistants (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304422X22000493)
------------------------------------------------------------

New research from Yong Jin Park et al. analyzes the contexts and uses of digital assistants: “inequalities operate in terms of material as well as socio-cultural bases, suggesting how digital opportunities instigated by digital assistants may not be built on level ground and continue to consolidate and reproduce existing inequalities in recursive ways.”
