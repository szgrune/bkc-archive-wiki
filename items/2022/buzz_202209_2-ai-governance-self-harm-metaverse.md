The Berkman Klein Center and Körber-Stiftung deliver the results of an intensive research project to develop policy recommendations for AI governance. ** Algorithmic accountability (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/20539517221115426)
------------------------------------------------------------

Burcu Baykurt explores how algorithmic accountability in U.S. cities is translated into action. "Algorithmic accountability, especially in public agencies, needs to focus on incompetence and asymmetries of power within a network of governments, tech companies, community groups, and technologies."

https://acamh.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/camh.12574

** Digital self-harm (https://acamh.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/camh.12574)
------------------------------------------------------------

In new research, Sameer Hinduja, along with coauthors Justin W. Patchin and Ryan C. Meldrum, describes links between digital self-harm and suicidiality in young people. “Considering the marked increase in sadness and hopelessness among US adolescents over the past 10 years, our findings serve as another indicator of worsening mental health status,” Hinduja says.

https://www.ted.com/talks/micaela_mantegna_how_to_stop_the_metaverse_from_becoming_the_internet_s_bad_sequel

** How to stop the metaverse from becoming the internet's bad sequel (https://www.ted.com/talks/micaela_mantegna_how_to_stop_the_metaverse_from_becoming_the_internet_s_bad_sequel)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a TED Talk, Micaela Mantegna maps the metaverse's potential to mimic, and to avoid, the pitfalls of the Internet. "The metaverse is already on fire, and we haven't even built it yet."

https://apo.org.au/node/319110

** Manifesto for sex-positive social media (https://apo.org.au/node/319110)
------------------------------------------------------------

Zahra Stardust and co-authors have released a new report outlining how to create sex-positive social media spaces. "While over-policing sexual content, platforms still lack a holistic response to addressing harassment, image-based abuse, malicious flagging, sexual racism, theft of sexual content and the unethical scraping of sexual databases."
