** New EU Digital Acts fall short (https://medium.com/@gregerwinnarr/4-ways-the-new-eu-digital-acts-fall-short-and-how-to-remedy-it-d16b681a88bc/)
------------------------------------------------------------

"What is on offer here...is a 1990s-grade neoliberal solution begging for a 21st century rethink that meets the challenges of the fast-expanding 21st century data economy." Nick Couldry, Velislava Hillman, Russell Newman, Mitzi László, and Gregory Narr address the wave of digital regulation in the EU—and how the measures can be improved.

https://medium.com/@Kendra_Serra/fear-uncertainty-and-period-trackers-340ab8fdff74

** Fear, uncertainty, and period trackers (https://medium.com/@Kendra_Serra/fear-uncertainty-and-period-trackers-340ab8fdff74)
------------------------------------------------------------

In the wake of Dobbs, Kendra Albert, with Maggie Delano and Emma Weil, evaluates the risks of data collection by the state via popular period trackers. “We should live in a world where folks are not forced to trade off their ability to use tools to know their own bodies against...corporate or government surveillance, but unfortunately, we’re not quite there yet.”

https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07523

** A model for activism (https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07523)
------------------------------------------------------------

Virgílio Almeida and collaborators explore a burgeoning activist network in Brazil, Sleeping Giants Brasil (SGB), working to combat the spread of misinformation. “Our results paint a nuanced portrait of internet activism....They suggest that SGB was successful in getting companies to boycott specific media outlets.”

https://www.euractiv.com/section/digital/opinion/building-sustainable-path-towards-planetary-system-of-knowledge/

** Sustainable knowledge (https://www.euractiv.com/section/digital/opinion/building-sustainable-path-towards-planetary-system-of-knowledge/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Juan Ortiz Freuler and Stefano Quintarelli consider the possible futures of the internet as a global system for human connection in the context of a collapsing natural world.  “This is not just a matter of conceptualizing the internet. On the contrary, we need the internet to actually start operating as a real network of knowledge.”
