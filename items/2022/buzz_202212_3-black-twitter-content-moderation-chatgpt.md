** Black Twitter & migration (https://www.wired.com/story/black-twitter-social-media/)
------------------------------------------------------------

With Chris Gilliard, Kishonna Gray discusses digital migration. "In truth, Twitter changed leadership from one mercurial billionaire to another, and in that regard it affirms that the site was never 'ours,' and cements how dangerous it is to think of these systems as the 'public square' no matter how many times people refer to it as such."

https://harvardlawreview.org/2022/12/content-moderation-as-systems-thinking/

** Fixing content moderation (https://harvardlawreview.org/2022/12/content-moderation-as-systems-thinking/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In The Harvard Law Review, Evelyn Douek describes how content moderation functions and how it might be better. "Content moderation should be understood as a project of mass speech administration and that, looking past a post-by-post evaluation of platform decision-making, reveals a complex and dynamic system that needs a more proactive and continuous form of governance."

https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2022/12/15/twitter-files-falling-flat-00073979

** "Twitter Files" failure (https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2022/12/15/twitter-files-falling-flat-00073979)
------------------------------------------------------------

Joan Donovan writes in Politico on Elon Musk's attempt to replicate the impact of The Facebook Files and why that attempt is falling embarassingly flat. "Musk is baiting mainstream media companies to cover a manufactured scandal about something that happened years ago and it is still not yielding returns."

https://www.prospectmagazine.co.uk/science-and-technology/what-we-would-lose-in-a-world-without-twitter-ethan-zuckerman-elon-musk

** Listening post (https://www.prospectmagazine.co.uk/science-and-technology/what-we-would-lose-in-a-world-without-twitter-ethan-zuckerman-elon-musk)
------------------------------------------------------------

Ethan Zuckerman meditates on the valuable aspects of Twitter connections and visibility, especially in journalism and activism. "For all the power Twitter gives to speakers, opening the possibility for their voices to be heard by vast and influential audiences, it’s an even more important tool for listeners."

https://www.nytimes.com/2022/12/09/podcasts/can-chatgpt-make-this-podcast.html?

** The highs and lows of ChatGPT (https://www.nytimes.com/2022/12/09/podcasts/can-chatgpt-make-this-podcast.html?)
------------------------------------------------------------

Aviv Ovadya joins the Hard Fork podcast to discuss OpenAI's new chatbot and platform democracy. "There’s ability to understand these systems academically in order to defend against them. There’s a lot of really good reasons to want this to be broadly accessible."
