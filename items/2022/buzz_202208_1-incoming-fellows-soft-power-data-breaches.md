https://www.ijlt.in/journal/recommender-systems-and-autonomy%3A-a-role-for-regulation-of-design%2C-rights%2C-and-transparency

** The power of recommender systems (https://www.ijlt.in/journal/recommender-systems-and-autonomy%3A-a-role-for-regulation-of-design%2C-rights%2C-and-transparency)
------------------------------------------------------------

Eduardo Magrani and co-authors make a detailed analysis of the "soft" power of near-ubiquitous recommendation algorithms in everyday life. "This exercise of power over individual and collective destinies necessitates regulatory action...on recommender systems and their creators."

https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/data-vu-why-breaches-involve-the-same-stories-again-and-again/

** Escaping the data breach loop (https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/data-vu-why-breaches-involve-the-same-stories-again-and-again/)
------------------------------------------------------------

For Scientific American, Woodrow Hartzog and co-author Daniel J. Solove address the recurrent "plot lines" of data breaches, even in instances when companies have invested heavily in security. "Hackers know that humans are the weak link. Many break-ins to company databases occur less by technological wizardry and more by con artistry."
