** Datafication and the Future of Human Rights Practice (https://www.openglobalrights.org/userfiles/file/Datafication_Report_JustLabs_2022.pdf)
------------------------------------------------------------

"Datafication is changing the way people interact with their surrounding space, and with each other," Juan Ortiz Freuler writes in a new report for JustLabs. "By 2030, this shift will have triggered a fundamental change in how rights are conceived and enforced. How should the the human rights field react to this process?"

https://carrcenter.hks.harvard.edu/publications/humanitarian-digital-ethics

** Humanitarian Digital Ethics (https://carrcenter.hks.harvard.edu/publications/humanitarian-digital-ethics)
------------------------------------------------------------

"Can humanitarian governance systems design mitigation
or subversion mechanisms to not lock people into future
harm, future inequity, or future indebtedness because of
technology design and intervention?" Aarathi Krishnan essays a Foresight and Decolonial Ethics and Governance Framework for the Carr Center for Human Rights Policy.

** Call for Participants: Takedowns & Transparency Research Sprint (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1puoVJMFpT-KZZn-Fj3dSjKc0yYidbKdVP9v112io4s0/edit#)
