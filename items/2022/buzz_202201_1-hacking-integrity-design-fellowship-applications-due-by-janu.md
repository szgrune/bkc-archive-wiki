** Treating Social Media Like a City (https://www.technologyreview.com/2021/12/20/1042709/how-to-save-social-media-treat-it-like-a-city/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sahar Massachi explores the potential benefits of making urban planning-like rules in online spaces to improve integrity, limit spam, and reduce harassment. "Social media companies need to prioritize integrity design over content moderation, and the public needs to hold them accountable about whether they do so."
