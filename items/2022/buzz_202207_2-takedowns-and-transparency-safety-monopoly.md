------------------------------------------------------------

** Celebrity voices in presidential elections (https://brazilian.report/podcast/2022/07/20/anitta-lula-bolsonaro-election/)
------------------------------------------------------------

David Nemer guests on The Brazilian Report podcast to speak to the massive influence of online celebrity influence in Brazil's contentious presidential race. "[Anitta's] fans are not necessarily aligned with a political party or a political ideology, but they are aligned with the preservation of democracy and the preservation of LBTQ rights."
