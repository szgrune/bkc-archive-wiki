** Understanding Chilling Effects (https://minnesotalawreview.org/article/understanding-chilling-effects/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jon Penney argues for a nuanced legal view of "chilling effects," increased surveillance deterring people from exercising their rights. Penney reaches across fields and disciplines to achieve "a more comprehensive and unified understanding."

https://open.spotify.com/episode/7jFaROyCUMRgXONobj4VAh

** The Heart of Online Abuse (https://open.spotify.com/episode/7jFaROyCUMRgXONobj4VAh)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Digital Public Interest Collective hosts Kishonna Gray to discuss the structural violence and harassment plaguing online gaming culture. "These are really lives and careers being impacted...Yes, it's a gaming space, but there are real-world consequences."
