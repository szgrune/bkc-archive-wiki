# IfRFA Presents First Amendment Careers

**Source:** [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4L9cSF-4DIE) · **Published:** 2022-01-06 · **By:** Berkman Klein Center · **Feed tags:** bkc-video

> This event showcased the work of several friends of IfRFA who work on a range of freedom of expression issues. Panelists discussed how they came into their current roles, provided tips to law students interested in First Amendment work, and offered their vision of where they see the field headed in the future.
