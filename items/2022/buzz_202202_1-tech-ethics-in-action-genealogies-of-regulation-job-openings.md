** Critical and Interdisciplinary Perspectives (https://www.benzevgreen.com/tech-ethics/)
------------------------------------------------------------
The new Special Issue of the Journal of Social Computing interrogates "the meaning and impacts of 'tech ethics': the embedding of ethics into digital technology research, development, use, and governance." Oriented around "real-world discourse and impacts," the articles explore tech ethics as a practical endeavor.

The issue was edited by Berkman Klein affiliate Ben Green, and it emerged from the Ethical Tech Working Group at BKC, co-founded in 2017 by Mary Gray and Kathy Pham. Every contributor was a regular participant in the Working Group's weekly meetings between 2017 and 2020:

https://www.benzevgreen.com/tech-ethics/

Jasmine E. McNealy, Lily Hu, Elettra Bietti, Salomé Viljoen, Luke Stark, Aden Van Noppen, Joanne Cheung, Jonnie Penn, Jenny Ungbha Korn, Maya Malik, and Momin M. Malik, with intellectual and logistical support from every corner of the BKC community!
