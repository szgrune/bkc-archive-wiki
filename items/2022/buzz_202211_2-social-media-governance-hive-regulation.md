** Twitter as hive mind? (https://techpolicy.press/on-elon-musks-vision-of-twitter-as-a-hive-mind/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Joe Bak-Coleman writes on Elon Musk's equivalence of Twitter to a hive mind. "If [Musk] truly believes in the promise of harnessing digital media to produce positive collective behavior, he should expand his commitment to working with researchers that study these questions, and provide them with the resources – both financial and data – to do the work. "

https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2022/11/elon-musk-twitter-regulations-legal-battles/671946/

** New regulatory landscape (https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2022/11/elon-musk-twitter-regulations-legal-battles/671946/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Evelyn Douek analyzes the dramatically shifting legal landscape around platform liability and responsibility for content, and how that will impact Elon Musk's vision of Twitter. "Musk has a lot less freedom than he realizes. He should brace himself for many more regulatory slaps in the face."

https://nebraskaexaminer.com/2022/11/08/q-and-a-with-cyberbullying-expert-who-is-slated-to-speak-in-omaha-nov-17/

** Campaign against cyber abuse (https://nebraskaexaminer.com/2022/11/08/q-and-a-with-cyberbullying-expert-who-is-slated-to-speak-in-omaha-nov-17/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sameer Hinduja sits down for a Q&A ahead of a large anti-cyberbullying event, speaking to patterns and tools for prevention. "We are definitely seeing an increase in identity-based harassment — where those who are in the minority group...are not only targeted disproportionately, but also experience a disproportionate amount of harm."
