------------------------------------------------------------

Sahar Massachi explains how integrity workers can make the internet a safer place. “Ideally what we’d have is a lot more detectives. Here’s a ring of people, they’re abusing the platform in this way. I’ve figured it out through patient work over the course of two weeks. And now I’m submitting a report that goes to a product team — here’s a loophole you should close.”

** Violence and Black Grief on Social Media (https://just-tech.ssrc.org/articles/examining-violence-and-black-grief-on-social-media-an-interview-with-desmond-upton-patton/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Desmond Upton Patton discusses social media’s ability to amplify violence — and shares about researchers’ misunderstandings around Black grief. “What bothers me the most in tech spaces is that we are not having nuanced conversations based on the human experience.”

https://slate.com/technology/2022/02/remote-learning-danger-lgbtq-students.html

** Remote Learning's Dangers for LGBTQ Students (https://slate.com/technology/2022/02/remote-learning-danger-lgbtq-students.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

The expansion of remote learning has placed LGBTQ students’ privacy in jeopardy, Alejandra Caraballo writes. "School is supposed to be a safe place for students, where they can access supportive staff and resources. Instead, schools may increasingly become a nightmare of surveillance used for the purposes of harming LGBTQ students."
