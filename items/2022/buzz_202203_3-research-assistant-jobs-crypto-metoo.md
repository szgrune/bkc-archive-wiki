** “Freedom to Transact” (https://www.cigionline.org/articles/cryptos-freedom-to-transact-may-actually-threaten-human-rights/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Elizabeth Renieris warns that a central appeal of cryptocurrency can also facilitate abuses of human rights. "What good is the individual’s freedom to transact when other basic rights and freedoms are not ensured, whether because of acts of war or of other atrocities that require a collective response or intervention?"

** Houselessness and “Innovation” (https://www.civicsource.info/p/why-innovation-around-houselessness)
------------------------------------------------------------

Katya Abazajian digs into the challenges of using data to support solutions for houselessness. “Much of the data that is collected isn’t actually designed to end houselessness, it’s designed to perpetuate a system of monitoring funds.”
