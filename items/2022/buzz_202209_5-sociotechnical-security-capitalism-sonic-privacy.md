** Beyond data security (https://www.nytimes.com/2022/09/26/opinion/uber-hack-data.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

Bruce Schneier writes in The New York Times about lessons from the recent Uber data hack. "More fundamental [than the proximate causes of the hack] are the underlying causes of security breaches: current economic and political forces incentivize companies to skimp on security at the expense of both personal and national security."

https://law.yale.edu/isp/publications/digital-public-sphere/healthy-digital-public-sphere/sonic-privacy

** Sonic privacy (https://law.yale.edu/isp/publications/digital-public-sphere/healthy-digital-public-sphere/sonic-privacy)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jasmine McNealy argues in a new paper for restraining machine interaction with people's sonic data. "The collection and use of sound, like that of all kinds of human-related data, allows for the creation of inferences and predictions about individuals and can modify individual behavior because of constant surveillance."
