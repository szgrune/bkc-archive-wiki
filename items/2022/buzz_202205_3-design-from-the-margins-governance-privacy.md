https://www.wired.com/story/technology-design-marginalized-communities/?redirectURL=https://www.wired.com/story/technology-design-marginalized-communities/

** Centering vulnerable users (https://www.wired.com/story/technology-design-marginalized-communities/?redirectURL=https://www.wired.com/story/technology-design-marginalized-communities/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In Wired, Afsaneh Rigot advocates Design From the Margins (https://www.belfercenter.org/publication/design-margins) , a process that centers the most marginalized users: "By understanding and establishing who is most impacted by distinct social, political, and legal frameworks, we can understand who would most likely be a victim of the weaponization of certain technologies. And, as an added benefit, technology which has recentered the extremes will always be generalizable to the broader usership."

https://www.asiapacific.ca/podcast/between-binary-tech-and-global-south/virgilio-almeida-creating-spaces-to-build-digital-governance-futures

** Creating new governance mechanisms (https://www.asiapacific.ca/podcast/between-binary-tech-and-global-south/virgilio-almeida-creating-spaces-to-build-digital-governance-futures)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Between the Binary podcast hosts Virgilio Almeida to discuss multi-disciplinary approaches to digital governance. "It's important that the design of new services, new interfaces, should be done using people from the Global South, in the Global South."

https://the1a.org/segments/extremely-online-data-privacy-after-roe-v-wade/

** Data privacy after Roe v. Wade (https://the1a.org/segments/extremely-online-data-privacy-after-roe-v-wade/)
------------------------------------------------------------

On 1A, Alejandra Caraballo and other guests discuss the misuses and limitations of reproduction health data in the tech sphere. "It’s incredible invasion of privacy. Tech companies really need to step up and say, even though it may impact their profitability, they have a broader moral responsibility to ensure people’s privacy is protected.”

https://www.nytimes.com/2022/05/19/opinion/privacy-technology-data.html

** A systematic response to digital surveillance (https://www.nytimes.com/2022/05/19/opinion/privacy-technology-data.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

Zeynep Tufekci writes for The New York Times on the urgent need for sweeping changes to curtail data surveillance. "What’s needed, for all Americans, is a full legal and political reckoning with the reckless manner in which digital technology has been allowed to invade our lives."
