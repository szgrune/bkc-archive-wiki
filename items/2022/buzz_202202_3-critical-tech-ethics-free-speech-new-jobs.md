** A Call for Critical Tech Ethics (https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9698150)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jenny Korn pushes for the creation of "critical tech ethics" as a way to improve technologies, including the Internet, and reduce the propagation of ongoing racial injustices. "Critical tech ethics is my call for action to influencers, leaders, policymakers, and educators to help move our society towards centering race, deliberately and intentionally, to tech ethics."
