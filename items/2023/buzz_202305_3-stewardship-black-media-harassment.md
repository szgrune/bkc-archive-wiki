** Stewarding technology (https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-01606-9)
------------------------------------------------------------

Joe Bak-Coleman, Carl Bergstrom, Jennifer Jacquet, James Mickens, Zeynep Tufekci, and Timmons Roberts advocate for creation of an intergovernmental panel on information technology. "The goal would not be to establish international consensus on how to manage the digital world, or to hand down regulatory recommendations — but to provide a knowledge base to underpin the decisions of actors such as governments, humanitarian groups and even businesses."

https://www.wired.com/story/unfair-automated-hiring-systems-are-everywhere-ftc/

** Employment discrimination (https://www.wired.com/story/unfair-automated-hiring-systems-are-everywhere-ftc/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Ifeoma Ajunwa calls on the FTC to begin regulating automated hiring systems to ensure fairness. "Because [automated hiring systems are] so loosely regulated, such systems have been shown to deny equal employment opportunity on the basis of protected categories such as race, age, sex, and disability."

https://open.spotify.com/episode/0rj6KTjihJFekecBNlp8hx?si=yRKUzOVjSEKDjgGp8qYtIw&nd=1

** Black digital communication (https://open.spotify.com/episode/0rj6KTjihJFekecBNlp8hx?si=yRKUzOVjSEKDjgGp8qYtIw&nd=1)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jabari Evans talks with Meredith Clark about media, journalism, and Black community. “On the one hand we want to create this space for someone who studies race and media, but then we have this old-school values system about how journalism works and why it works and how we produce individuals that go into the workforce—and at times those ideologies are very much at odds.”

https://cyberbullying.org/generative-ai-as-a-vector-for-harassment-and-harm

** AI-generated harassment (https://cyberbullying.org/generative-ai-as-a-vector-for-harassment-and-harm)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sameer Hinduja describes some of the potential negative uses of generative AI, including bullying and harassment. “In this environment of giddy euphoria about [generative AI’s] use...it seems wise to consider how it might be used to cause harm—and what we need to be doing to reduce potential fallout from its misuse.”

https://indianexpress.com/article/opinion/columns/a-call-for-algorithmic-justice-for-sc-sts-8607880/

** Anti-caste tech policy (https://indianexpress.com/article/opinion/columns/a-call-for-algorithmic-justice-for-sc-sts-8607880/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Siva Mathiyazhagan argues that India must enact inclusive anti-caste tech policy to prevent India’s caste system from expanding into emerging technologies. “To promote equity and justice in AI and metaverse technologies, it is crucial to address the gaps in India’s anti-caste tech policy.”
