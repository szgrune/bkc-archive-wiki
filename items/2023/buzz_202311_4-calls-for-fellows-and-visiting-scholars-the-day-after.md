** Scholars & Practitioners: Apply for BKC's 2024-2025 Fellowship Program (https://cyber.harvard.edu/getinvolved/fellowships/2425Fellows)
------------------------------------------------------------

BKC is now accepting fellowship applications for the 2024-2025 academic year through our annual open call. This opportunity is for scholars and practitioners who wish to spend the year in residence in Cambridge, MA, as part of the Center's vibrant community of research and practice, and who seek to engage in collaborative, cross-disciplinary, and cross-sectoral exploration of the Internet's most important issues. This year we will accept applications from PhD students and candidates as well. Deadline January 22.

https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/2023/11/21/call-for-2024-2025-rsm-visiting-scholars/

** Faculty: Apply to be a 2024-2025 Rebooting Social Media Visiting Scholar (https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/2023/11/21/call-for-2024-2025-rsm-visiting-scholars/)
------------------------------------------------------------

BKC's Institute for Rebooting Social Media welcomes applications for its 2024-2025 cohort of Visiting Scholars from faculty who have a project that investigates the problems and opportunities of networked communication and social media. This interdisciplinary cohort will undertake original projects while engaging with RSM faculty, staff, scholars, fellows, students, and the broader BKC and Harvard University communities and projects. Deadline January 8.

https://www.cigionline.org/articles/boardroom-drama-at-openai-portends-a-looming-ai-monoculture/

** AI Monoculture (https://www.cigionline.org/articles/boardroom-drama-at-openai-portends-a-looming-ai-monoculture/)
------------------------------------------------------------

"In late November 2023, the palace intrigue over control of OpenAI boiled over. But behind the drama lurks a deeper problem: the tremendous concentration of power in the artificial intelligence (AI) industry." Maroussia Lévesque reads the drama as a "a wake-up call for governments to actively curb the consolidation of AI power in the hands of a few corporate overlords."

https://www.sitra.fi/en/publications/enabling-the-responsible-use-of-technology-at-scale/

** Regulatory technology (https://www.sitra.fi/en/publications/enabling-the-responsible-use-of-technology-at-scale/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Paul Fehlinger showcases the critical role of regulatory technology, or RegTech, in powering a robust digital economy that deploys cutting-edge technologies. "RegTech solutions have the potential to become the enabling backbone spurring digital innovation and growth in Europe’s regulated digital economy."

https://ijoc.org/index.php/ijoc/article/view/21236/4384

** Gatekeeping practices (https://ijoc.org/index.php/ijoc/article/view/21236/4384)
------------------------------------------------------------

Juan Ortiz Freuler examines the handling of user requests to access Web content via links within mobile apps in the Apple iOS ecosystem. "Understanding gatekeeping behaviors enacted by different actors managing the doors across different information ecosystems is becoming more important as people continue to spend more time in increasingly immersive virtual environments."

https://time.com/6337667/day-after-tomorrow-cold-war-essay/

** Power of media (https://time.com/6337667/day-after-tomorrow-cold-war-essay/)
------------------------------------------------------------

David Craig recounts how a TV show changed the history of the Cold War and comments on how media can impact society now. "Storytellers and media professionals have agency. First, they learn how to navigate within media systems and harness the power of the medium. Then they develop strategies to deliver affective and enlightened messages."
