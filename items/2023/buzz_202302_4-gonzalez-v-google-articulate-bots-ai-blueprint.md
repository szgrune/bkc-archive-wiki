On Tuesday, RSM hosted a live commentary (https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/events/gonzalez-v-google-live-analysis/)  on Gonzalez v. Google oral arguments. Visiting Scholar Kate Klonick moderated an expert panel featuring Mary Anne Franks, Mike Godwin, James Grimmelmann, Gus Hurwitz, Jeff Kosseff, Emma Llanso, Alan Rozenshtein, Eugene Volokh, Benjamin Wittes, and Jonathan Zittrain. Prior to oral argument, RSM collected commentary from amici curiae (https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/2023/02/20/amici-blog-post/) . After the hearing, BKC Faculty Associate Sarah Sobieraj discussed the potential impact with The Boston Globe (https://www.bostonglobe.com/2023/02/23/business/questions-swirl-around-how-supreme-court-will-rule-internet-law-social-media/?p1=BGSearch_Advanced_Results) .

Google — and rounds up new work from RSM’s Visiting Scholars, including Greg Gondwe’s research on misinformation in countries with variations in digital access and literacy (https://scholarworks.lib.csusb.edu/comm-publications/2/)  and Jabari Evans’ talk with Catherine Knight Steele on his Uncommon Naledge (https://open.spotify.com/episode/21WCLszsLRMaxOVoBQbqPH?si=8772b80e8cd34a64&nd=1)  podcast.

** Bots, bots, bots, bots, bots (https://www.the-security-times.com/ifs-ands-and-bots/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jonathan Zittrain discusses security issues posed by chatbots. "The rise of articulate bots – that are indistinguishable from people and answerable to unknown parties with any range of agendas – places us into truly uncharted territory, and in a fix that will call upon a blend of government, commercial and interpersonal cooperation to see us through it."

** Building on the Blueprint (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/building-on-the-blueprint-9471276f2cce)
------------------------------------------------------------

Inspired by discussions within the Rebooting AI Working Group at BKC, Maroussia Lévesque, Tom Zick, Dennis Redeker, You Jeen Ha, Florian Martin-Bariteau, and Mason Kortz explore putting the principles of the White House’s Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights into practice.

https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2023/02/elon-musk-twitter-ethics-algorithm-biases/673110/

** Oversight of tech is overdue (https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2023/02/elon-musk-twitter-ethics-algorithm-biases/673110/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Rumman Chowdhury provides a firsthand account of Elon Musk’s takeover of Twitter. Chowdhury served as director of Machine Learning Ethics, Transparency, and Accountability (META) which “focused on community, public engagement, and accountability....Unsurprisingly, we were wiped out when Musk arrived.”
