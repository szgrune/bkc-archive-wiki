** Generative AI's future (https://policyreview.info/articles/news/let-me-tell-you-chatgpt-ai-will-not-change-our-world/1694)
------------------------------------------------------------

Yong Jin Park cautions against overexcitement and excessive worrying about generative AI. "Predictions [from both optimists and pundits] will turn out to be largely wrong. And I say this because OpenAI’s ChatGPT — and self-trainable AI in general — is shockingly underwhelming."

https://newsletter.mollywhite.net/p/the-venture-capitalists-dilemma

** The VC's dilemma (https://newsletter.mollywhite.net/p/the-venture-capitalists-dilemma)
------------------------------------------------------------

Molly White argues that the recent events with Silicon Valley Bank warrant a questioning of the current venture capital model. "The circumstances that led up to this disaster, and the people we allowed at the helm of the ship steering full-steam towards the iceberg, ought to be questioned."
