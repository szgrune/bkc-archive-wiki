------------------------------------------------------------

In The New York Times, Nathan E. Sanders and Bruce Schneier explore the exploitive potential of ChatGPT in the context of the democratic process. "An A.I. system with the sophistication of ChatGPT but trained on relevant data could selectively target key legislators and influencers to identify the weakest points in the policymaking system and ruthlessly exploit them."

https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/01/13/business/what-is-shadow-banning.html

** Shadow bans (https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/01/13/business/what-is-shadow-banning.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jonathan Zittrain speaks with The New York Times about social media platforms' clandestine practices for limiting post visibility. “Shadow banning is the worry by any user that they are howling into the void, that they have been placed in a bubble and it’s undisclosed,”

https://www.ifa.de/en/44-digital-technology-and-human-rights-mit-joana-varon-english-edition/#section3

** Internet life and human rights (https://www.ifa.de/en/44-digital-technology-and-human-rights-mit-joana-varon-english-edition/#section3)
------------------------------------------------------------

Joana Varon joins Die Kulturmittler podcast to outline the path to protect digital rights as well as democratic ideals online. "The algorithms on social media that we have now, they promote misinformation, and they promote hate because those kinds of content have much more engagement than the regular, sometimes boring truth."
