** Announcing BKC's new Applied Social Media Lab (https://asml.cyber.harvard.edu/press-release-bkc-lab-launch/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Frank McCourt and the 501(c)(3) non-profit Project Liberty will support the launch of BKC’s Applied Social Media Lab (https://asml.cyber.harvard.edu/) – a first-of-its-kind program to assess and build social media technology in the public interest.

The Lab gives industry-trained technologists the freedom to build social media solutions that center the public interest. The Lab members will work with BKC’s interdisciplinary community of experts from academia, government, civil society, and industry. Through these unique collaborations, the Lab will create new open-source software and protocols, new analyses, and new policy recommendations that will foster a more humane – and more human – experience online. ** Law and social data (https://lpeproject.org/blog/towards-a-legal-understanding-of-social-data/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Amanda Parsons and Salomé Viljoen explain why current laws are not well equipped to confront problems generated by the rise of informational capitalism. "To effectively regulate informational capitalism, legal actors in these regimes must understand how companies use social data and its predictive value to create both wealth and power for themselves and their investors."

https://techpolicy.press/centering-community-voices-how-tech-companies-can-better-engage-with-civil-society-organizations/

** Centering communities (https://techpolicy.press/centering-community-voices-how-tech-companies-can-better-engage-with-civil-society-organizations/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Nadah Feteih and Elodie Vialle argue that companies should involve at-risk communities in the design process of new technologies. "Partnered with affected individuals, these teams can brainstorm scenarios, situations, and simulate the implications of product and feature decisions through a multistakeholder design process to preemptively mitigate potential harms."

https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10217072

** Analyzing DAOs (https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10217072)
------------------------------------------------------------

Andrea Peña-Calvin, Jorge Saldivar, Javier Arroyo, and Samer Hassan provide insights on decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs). "Contrary to assertions made by blockchain enthusiasts, our analysis reveals that DAOs predominantly operate in financial and technological domains, primarily offering blockchain-based services."
