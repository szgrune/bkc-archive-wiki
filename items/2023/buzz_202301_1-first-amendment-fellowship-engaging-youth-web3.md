** A representative First Amendment (https://www.ifrfa.org/fellowship-info)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Initiative for a Representative First Amendment (IfRFA) is a unique program that supports legal students from backgrounds traditionally underrepresented in First Amendment law, so that they can explore opportunities that they would not otherwise be able to. IfRFA's flagship fellowship program is accepting applications for its next cohort until January 9.

https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2022/15-ways-engage-youth-within-your-company-and-why-you-should-do-it

** Engaging youth (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2022/15-ways-engage-youth-within-your-company-and-why-you-should-do-it)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sandra Cortesi writes on the importance of engaging young people in business and government settings. "Youth are increasingly voicing their questions, concerns, and hopes about the future. Input from the next generation is particularly crucial when it comes to navigating the challenges of new technologies."

https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4302681

** Cooperative web3 (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4302681)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jad Esber and Trebor Scholz worked over the last few months with other researchers, builders, and activists to discuss platform cooperativism and the intersection with the web3 movement. "We investigated how developers and emerging internet companies might learn from the shared history of cooperatives and how, in practice, they can construct systems that respect cooperative ideals."
