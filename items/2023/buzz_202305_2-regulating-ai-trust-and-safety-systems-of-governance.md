** Regulating AI (https://www.kqed.org/forum/2010101893064/artificial-intelligence-is-booming-how-should-it-be-regulated)
------------------------------------------------------------

Rumman Chowdhury joins KQED for an expansive conversation on whether regulation of AI is necessary and likely to be fruitful. In terms of government regulation of AI, one of Chowdhury's current top concerns is "ensuring that what is being promised is what's being delivered."

https://integrityinstitute.org/podcast/trust-in-tech-e19-eldritch-open-ai-gpt

** Inside T&S at OpenAI (https://integrityinstitute.org/podcast/trust-in-tech-e19-eldritch-open-ai-gpt)
------------------------------------------------------------

Sahar Massachi joins the Integrity Institute podcast for a conversation with OpenAI about safety. “If you build the tools to stop people from being terrible to each other...those same tools are open to executives or ad people or comms people...who might use those tools for a very different definition of what bad is, too, and so there’s sometimes actual security and safety in...not building the tools in the first place.”

https://www.polcommtech.com/post/the-use-of-ai-in-canadian-politics

** AI in Canadian politics (https://www.polcommtech.com/post/the-use-of-ai-in-canadian-politics)
------------------------------------------------------------

On a recent episode of Wonks and War Rooms, Elizabeth Dubois facilitates a lively discussion on how artificial intelligence is being deployed in Canadian political contexts. The topics range from mis- and disinformation, facial recognition, synthetic media, deep fakes, and voice cloning to technical terms like GANs and large language models.

https://cyberscoop.com/rethinking-democracy-ai/

** 21st-century democracy (https://cyberscoop.com/rethinking-democracy-ai/)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a keynote speech for the RSA Conference in San Francisco, Bruce Schneier explains the need to rethink governance systems to better suit the twenty-first century. "We need to recreate our system of governance for an era in which transformative technologies pose catastrophic risks as well as great promise."

https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3544549.3585825

** Blockchain learning curve (https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3544549.3585825)
------------------------------------------------------------

Samer Hassan and collaborators report on the experiences of first-time blockchain users. Such experiences are informative because "to permeate the mainstream, blockchain technology should be easily accessible to the general public. ... Results unveil that even users familiar with technology experienced severe difficulties using blockchain-based apps."
