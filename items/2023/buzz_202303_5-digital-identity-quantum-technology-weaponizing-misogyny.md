------------------------------------------------------------

We recap the Digital Identity in Times of Crisis (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2023-03/digital-identity-during-times-crisis-what-we-learned-during-fall-2022-research-sprint)  research sprint – including links to outputs, the syllabus, and more. https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4393248

** Quantum innovation (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4393248)
------------------------------------------------------------

A team of experts, including Urs Gasser, propose a framework for responsibly proceeding in quantum technology (QT) innovation. “Quantum innovation should be guided by a methodological framework for Responsible QT, aimed at jointly safeguarding against risks by proactively addressing them, engaging stakeholders in the innovation process, and continually advancing QT.”

https://www.cnn.com/videos/world/2023/03/25/exp-banning-tiktok-social-media-teens-mental-health--cnn-world.cnn

** Kids on socials (https://www.cnn.com/videos/world/2023/03/25/exp-banning-tiktok-social-media-teens-mental-health--cnn-world.cnn)
------------------------------------------------------------

On CNN Judith Donath advocates for emphasizing in-person relationships and increased education as opposed to outright social media bans. "This goes for both kids and adults, we really need a lot more literacy about understanding what is real online and one of the most important things is understanding to look to the source of your information."

https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2023/3/27/gender-based-harassment-journalism/

** Gender-based harassment (https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2023/3/27/gender-based-harassment-journalism/)
------------------------------------------------------------

At IfRFA's “Weaponizing Misogyny (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/weaponizing-misogyny-gender-based-harassments-impact-journalists-and-free-expression) ” event, BKC research assistant and gen-z activist Pratika Katiyar joined a panel of journalists to discuss gender-based harassment in journalism. “There needs to be a better implementation of support within newsrooms, and trainings to combat [harassment], because being offline is not always an option for a lot of young people.”
