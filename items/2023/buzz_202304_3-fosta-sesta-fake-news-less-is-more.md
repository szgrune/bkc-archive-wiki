** FOSTA-SESTA failures (https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2023/04/podcast-episode-safer-sex-work-makes-safer-internet)
------------------------------------------------------------

Kendra Albert and Danielle Blunt discuss the failures of FOSTA-SESTA, legislation purportedly aimed at stopping sex trafficking. "The criminal component actually makes it much more difficult for even sites that are engaged in anti-trafficking measures, but where they may want to support sex worker speech or sex worker advocacy, to do that in a way that doesn't run really significant risk of criminal liability."

https://www.braziloffice.org/en/articles/fake-news-bill-move-accordingly-and-preserve-society

** Regulating fake news (https://www.braziloffice.org/en/articles/fake-news-bill-move-accordingly-and-preserve-society)
------------------------------------------------------------

David Nemer breaks down important points of the "Fake News Bill" that Brazil's legislature is currently considering. "Brazil can seize the opportunity to set an example for the world in regulating Big Techs. However, the country must avoid adopting the reckless approach of 'Move Fast and Break Things' and instead embrace the challenge of 'Moving Accordingly and Preserving Society'."

https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/twitter-changes-hateful-conduct-policy-rcna80338

** Twitter's stealthy change (https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/twitter-changes-hateful-conduct-policy-rcna80338)
------------------------------------------------------------

Alejandra Caraballo comments on Twitter's quiet removal of language that expressly protected transgender people from online harassment in its hateful conduct policy. The change is "perpetuating this incentive to engage in the targeting of trans people, especially since it’s being favored by the algorithm because it’s creating outrage.”

https://www.nytimes.com/2023/04/19/technology/personaltech/tiktok-twitter-facebook-social.html?partner=slack&smid=sl-share

** Less social media (https://www.nytimes.com/2023/04/19/technology/personaltech/tiktok-twitter-facebook-social.html?partner=slack&smid=sl-share)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a New York Times article discussing how some users are turning to smaller social networks, Jonathan Zittrain points to Minus, an app developed by RSM Assembly Fellow Ben Grosser. Minus (https://minus.social/)  gives users only 100 posts. “It’s a performance art experiment” to provoke reimagining social media design.

https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/call-for-volunteer-reviewers/

** Seeking volunteer reviewers (https://rebootingsocialmedia.org/call-for-volunteer-reviewers/)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Institute for Rebooting Social Media is seeking volunteer reviewers for a research project focused on the liability of social media companies and digital platforms across 49 countries in Sub-Saharan Africa. Individuals with an interest or expertise in tech policy, internet governance, digital rights, and/or social media regulation are encouraged to apply.
