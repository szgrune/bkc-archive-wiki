** EU AI Act (https://www.codastory.com/authoritarian-tech/petra-molnar-ai-act/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Petra Molnar spoke with Coda Story about the EU's AI Act and its potential implications in the border surveillance space. "The AI Act — if we can push through certain amendments — is a really unique opportunity to try and think through how we can create oversight, accountability and governance on all sorts of technologies at the border."

https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/storytelling-migration-journeys-and-technology-photo-exhibit

Related: On March 28, BKC will host a photo exhibit (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/storytelling-migration-journeys-and-technology-photo-exhibit) that asks the audience to consider and evaluate the human cost of an increasingly automated world. Molnar and photographer Kenya-Jade Pinto will reflect on years of interdisciplinary work at the world’s borders, bridging visual storytelling with research and advocacy on the sharpest edges of technological development.
