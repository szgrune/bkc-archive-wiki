** Trickle-down monopoly (https://datasociety.net/library/amazons-trickle-down-monopoly/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Moira Weigel's new Data & Society report digs into Amazon's relationship with small businesses. "Amazon has not hurt or helped small businesses so much as transformed them, turning older forms of commerce into something more akin to day trading."

https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/10776990221129245?journalCode=jmqc

** Algorithmic personalization (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/10776990221129245?journalCode=jmqc)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a Journalism & Mass Communication Quarterly forum, Yong Jin Park and Jeong-Nam Kim discuss the notion that more data increases the quality of AI personalization. “Debunking a false logic of ‘the more data, the better AI personalization’ is the first step to understanding where the regulatory need for data protection should lie.”

https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2023-01/announcing-dr-rumman-chowdhury-berkman-klein-center-responsible-ai-fellow
