The Second Circuit issued an opinion in ACLU v. ICE, an important transparency case in which the Cyberlaw Clinic represented amici curiae in support of the appeal.  "The opinion is an important victory for government transparency in an age where government records are increasingly stored in structured datasets."

https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/10.1386/ghhs_00043_1#abstract_content

** Dance economies (https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/10.1386/ghhs_00043_1#abstract_content)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jabari Evans' new article in Global Hip Hop Studies examines the emotional and relational labor of Black women rappers and video models. "This study introduces insights on the digital evolution of hip hop culture’s relationship with sex work and reveals newfound ideological tensions faced by Black women rappers who are using sexual dance economies to adopt new direct-to-consumer business models."

https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/01/31/technology/elon-musk-tweets.html

** Musk's Twitter ecosystem (https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/01/31/technology/elon-musk-tweets.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

In a New York Time's dissection of Elon Musk's tweets, Joan Donovan comments on the power play of buying Twitter. “He is one of the richest men in the world buying a social media company in a move that is expressly about politics and influence on culture and on media.”
