------------------------------------------------------------

With Harvard Law School, BKC recently announced the Initiative on Artificial Intelligence and the Law. The initiative will be directed by Oren Bar-Gill and Cass Sunstein and will focus on new challenges and opportunities for the law created by the rise of artificial intelligence.

https://www.templelawreview.org/essay/collective-data-rights-and-their-possible-abuse/

** Collective data rights (https://www.templelawreview.org/essay/collective-data-rights-and-their-possible-abuse/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Asaf Lubin highlights the transnational risks associated with formal recognition of communitarian rights over data under international human rights law. "It is truly naïve to think there is some universal right to privacy or data protection, and the practice of states in cyberspace demonstrates just how frustrating the tension is between the mythical law in the books and the law in action."

https://www.templelawreview.org/essay/datafication-identity-and-the-reorganization-of-the-category-individual/

** Concepts of the individual (https://www.templelawreview.org/essay/datafication-identity-and-the-reorganization-of-the-category-individual/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Juan Ortiz Freuler explores how technological shifts are contributing to tension around the concept of identity. "The conceptualization of identity is being transformed by communication technologies ... this shift should be observed by examining how technology is reshaping the way others see us, how we perceive ourselves, and the language we rely upon to communicate with each other."

https://www.prospectmagazine.co.uk/ideas/technology/62163/want-to-stop-harmful-tech-just-say-no

** Avoiding harms (https://www.prospectmagazine.co.uk/ideas/technology/62163/want-to-stop-harmful-tech-just-say-no)
------------------------------------------------------------

Ethan Zuckerman discusses the dynamics between resistance, harm reduction, and refusal in the field of AI. "A responsible conversation should focus less on future scenarios of robot hyperintelligence and more on the actual, real-world harms that can be inflicted when we outsource decisions about human lives to machines."

https://www.hilltimes.com/story/2023/07/17/canadians-digital-safety-calls-for-strong-whistleblower-and-security-researcher-protection/392507/

** Digital safety (https://www.hilltimes.com/story/2023/07/17/canadians-digital-safety-calls-for-strong-whistleblower-and-security-researcher-protection/392507/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Florian Martin-Bariteau argues that the protection of whistleblowers and public interest researchers is necessary when crafting digital policies. "While AI promises considerable benefits, recent developments and obscure algorithms have raised multiple legal, political, and ethical issues, including issues of respect of fundamental rights, and the protection of those brave enough to disclose these issues."

https://slate.com/technology/2023/07/artificial-intelligence-microdirectives.html

** AI as law enforcement (https://slate.com/technology/2023/07/artificial-intelligence-microdirectives.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jon Penney and Bruce Schneier explain the threats to freedom that a substantially increased role of AI in law enforcement may bring on. "A future where A.I.s interpret, apply, and enforce most laws at societal scale like this will exponentially magnify problems around fairness, transparency, and freedom."
