** Accessible performing arts (https://www.americantheatre.org/2023/02/07/digital-access-research-project-launches-at-harvard/)
------------------------------------------------------------

metaLAB has launched The Digital Access Research Project (https://mlml.io/p/digital-access-research-project/) . The project is focused on the digital accessibility of performing arts events and aims to develop a set of guidelines for policymakers and performing arts organizations that would balance the digital accessibility needs of marginalized groups with performer and institutional copyrights.

https://www.sciencefriday.com/segments/chatgpt-microsoft-google-ai-future/

** Generative AI (https://www.sciencefriday.com/segments/chatgpt-microsoft-google-ai-future/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Rumman Chowdhury joined Science Friday to discuss the state of generative artificial intelligence. "When we think about these generative AI models, what I think about as someone who works in machine learning ethics and AI ethics, is what are the kinds of harms and stereotypes that exist in society that these machines can pick up on."

https://www.wired.com/story/bias-statistics-artificial-intelligence-healthcare/

** Health & algorithmic fairness (https://www.wired.com/story/bias-statistics-artificial-intelligence-healthcare/)
------------------------------------------------------------

With Brent Mittelstadt and Chris Russell, Sandra Wachter warns that typical conceptions of fairness in algorithms can be as dangerous to health care as traditional medical bias. To remedy this, Wachter argues that "what we need are systems that are fair through leveling up, that help groups with worse performance without arbitrarily harming others."

https://www.cnn.com/2023/02/15/opinions/genz-free-expression-activist-student-journalism/index.html

** Student speech (https://www.cnn.com/2023/02/15/opinions/genz-free-expression-activist-student-journalism/index.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

In an opinion piece for CNN, BKC Research Assistant Pratika Katiyar warns that freedom of expression is under threat in schools around the country. Still, she says, GenZ will not be silenced: "GenZ is the present and future of journalism, and that is why we continue fighting for our rights."
