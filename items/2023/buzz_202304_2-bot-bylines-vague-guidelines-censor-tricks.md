** Guidelines for all (https://medium.com/@jessicafjeld/one-law-to-rule-them-all-705e8c776e38)
------------------------------------------------------------

Jessica Fjeld and Sol Bensadon posit that forthcoming UN content regulation guidelines will be misused by authoritarian states to legitimize existing arbitrary restrictions on freedom of expression. "The devil is in the implementation of vague language about 'protecting democracy' that’s as likely to be weaponized against dissidents in some contexts as it is to curtail election misinformation in others."

https://thebulletin.org/2023/04/can-journalism-resist-a-chatbot-fueled-race-to-the-bottom/

** Bylines for bots (https://thebulletin.org/2023/04/can-journalism-resist-a-chatbot-fueled-race-to-the-bottom/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Joan Donovan weighs in on the value of qualitative human intelligence in producing reliable news, including where AI-generated stories fail hard on best practices. "Where it doesn’t work, of course, is in our most contentious issues, which have to do with inequality, racism, crime, places where context and integrity are going to matter."

https://www.wired.com/story/abortion-pill-comstock-free-speech-internet/

** Fighting for free speech (https://www.wired.com/story/abortion-pill-comstock-free-speech-internet/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Alejandra Caraballo and Kelly Capatosto explain how the long-dormant Hyde Amendment may be revived to substantially limit access to abortion by stifling speech online. "This is not just a battle for the future of abortion-related speech; it is a struggle to preserve the very essence of the internet as we know it today."

https://techpolicy.press/what-generative-ai-reveals-about-the-limits-of-technological-innovation/

** Adopt, harm, regulate (https://techpolicy.press/what-generative-ai-reveals-about-the-limits-of-technological-innovation/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Joe Bak-Coleman writes about the adoption and regulation of generative AI. Bak-Coleman explains that with generative AI, as with all new technologies, "our only choice is to wait until journalists and experts uncover harm, gather evidence too compelling to be undermined by PR firms and lobbyists, and convince polarized legislatures to enact sensible and effective legislation."

https://commonwealthmagazine.org/politics/a-digital-push-for-more-legislative-participation/

** MAPLE launch (https://commonwealthmagazine.org/politics/a-digital-push-for-more-legislative-participation/)
------------------------------------------------------------

CommonWealth covers the MAPLE (Massachusetts Platform for Legislative Engagement) launch and BKC's moderated discussion, Digital Public Spaces for a Healthy Democracy. During the discussion, panelists, including Nathan Sanders, explained their own experiences with democracy in the digital age and their hopes for MAPLE.

https://www.wired.com/story/italy-ban-chatgpt-privacy-gdpr/

** Regulatory woes (https://www.wired.com/story/italy-ban-chatgpt-privacy-gdpr/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Elizabeth Renieris speaks with Wired about the large regulatory issues relating to ChatGPT and other chatbots. "There is this rot at the very foundations of the building blocks of this technology—and I think that’s going to be very hard to cure."
