** Goods, services, & algorithms (https://academic.oup.com/jla/article/15/1/1/7246686?login=false#414681453)
------------------------------------------------------------

Oren Bar-Gill, Cass Sunstein, and Inbal Talgam-Cohen discuss the use of algorithmic harms in consumer markets. "Since algorithms exacerbate the harm caused to imperfectly informed and imperfectly rational consumers, their increasing use provides fresh support for existing efforts to reduce information and rationality deficits, especially through optimally designed disclosure mandates."

https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4539452

** LLMs as search engines (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4539452)
------------------------------------------------------------

Beatriz Bocero Arcila argues that certain large language models, especially those generally being used for search purposes, should be regulated like search engines in the EU. "Because many of the risks these systems raise are risks to the information ecosystem, in Europe they can and should be addressed, at the outset, with current content moderation law."

https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/epdf/10.1086/726042

** ChatGPT in social work (https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/epdf/10.1086/726042)
------------------------------------------------------------

Desmond Patton, Aviv Landau, and Siva Mathiyazhagan explore opportunities to use ChatGPT in social work and call for the development of an ethical framework for using AI in research. "Social innovation, ethics, and critical reflexivity are essential for advancement in social work science, opening opportunities for social work scientists to provide an inclusion and justice lens for advances in AI science and social work."

https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-lawfare-podcast-can-we-build-a-trustworthy-future-web

** Trust & safety (https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-lawfare-podcast-can-we-build-a-trustworthy-future-web)
------------------------------------------------------------

On The Lawfare Podcast Tech, Camille François and Rose Jackson discuss the challenges in the future as trust and safety scales to new ecosystems. "We need to make sure that the trust and safety field can ... be agile and rigorous."
