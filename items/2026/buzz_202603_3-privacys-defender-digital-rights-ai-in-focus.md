** From Ideas to Impact: BKC Launches Inaugural Student Essay Contest
------------------------------------------------------------

Are you a current Harvard student with something to say (or write) about artificial intelligence’s impact on society?

In February, BKC’s Student Engagement Team—including Program Manager Valerie Gomez, Harvard College student Maya Dummet, and Graduate School of Design student Ami Mehta—hosted an info session and “writing gym” designed to help students prepare for the Center’s inaugural Student Essay Contest co-organized with Tech Policy Press.

Check out this write-up (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2026-02/ideas-impact-bkc-launches-its-inaugural-student-essay-contest) , as well as this recording of the session (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KWE9NZknls) , featuring Tech Policy Press CEO and Editor Justin Hendrix. If you are a current degree-seeking Harvard student interested in writing, learn more and submit your work here (https://cyber.harvard.edu/projects/student-essay-contest-spring-2026) .

** APPLY: AI and the Law
------------------------------------------------------------

BKC is now accepting applications for AI and the Law: Navigating the New Legal Landscape (https://go.execed.law.harvard.edu/l/868411/2025-11-25/n8k6mx) , developed in partnership between the BKC and Harvard Law School Executive Education.

This three-day course will give senior leaders a foundational understanding of AI and how this new technology is reshaping legal frameworks and the practice of law. Using hands-on learning components, participants will also learn how to apply these novel tools within their own organizations. Participants will learn from an interdisciplinary group of experts across AI, including Executive Director Alex Pascal.

Learn more about the course and apply here. (https://go.execed.law.harvard.edu/l/868411/2025-11-25/n8k6mx)
