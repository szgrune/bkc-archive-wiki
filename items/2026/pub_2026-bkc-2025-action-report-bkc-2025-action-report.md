# BKC 2025 Action Report

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2026/bkc-2025-action-report) · **Published:** 2026-02-23 · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> 2025 was a year of renewed alignment and sharpened focus around a strong AI research agenda at BKC. From hiring our first Chief AI Scientist to launching open-source tools designed to improve trust and transparency to convening critical dialogues among experts, students, technologists, and policymakers about the issues at the frontier of AI's impact on society, we invite you to learn more about our work in 2025 and beyond.
