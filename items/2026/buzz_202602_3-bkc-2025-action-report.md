** From the Executive Director:
------------------------------------------------------------

Dear colleagues and members of the BKC community,

I’m thrilled to share our 2025 Action Report. Since joining BKC in July of 2025, I’ve been struck by the depth (and breadth!) of thought, generosity of spirit, and sense of shared purpose and affection that define this community. In a year distinguished by rapid advances in generative AI and competing visions for how these technologies should be shaped and stewarded, BKC has leaned into the moment with rigor, creativity, and urgency.

Our 2025 Action Report (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2025report) captures that work and momentum.
Read the Report (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2025report)

You’ll find highlights from a year of sharpening focus and expanding impact: the arrival of our first Chief AI Scientist and the growth of our AI research initiatives; new open-source tools from the Applied Social Media Lab designed to improve people’s online experiences; and a dynamic slate of classes, workshops, and programs bridging disciplines, sectors, and ideas that equipped students and scholars, practitioners and the public to shape the digital environment in the public interest.

You’ll also meet the faculty, fellows, students, and partners whose work is helping us sort signal from noise and look around corners at what will matter next.

The pace of technological change can feel overwhelming. At BKC, we approach that uncertainty not with resignation, but with curiosity and resolve. Our mission—to make sense of the digital environment and ensure that it promotes human agency, dignity, and genuine connection—has never been more urgent.

I hope you’ll take a few minutes to explore the interactive, digital report and see what this community made possible in 2025. You can also download the full report (https://cyber.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/2026-02/BKC_2025ActionReport_Digital.pdf) .

Thank you for your partnership and engagement.

With appreciation,
Alex Pascal
Executive Director
