# Journalism in the Age of AI: From Acceleration to Reimagination by Seth C. Lewis, Rodrigo Zamith, and Tomás Dodds

**Source:** [journalismandai.com](https://journalismandai.com/) · **Published:** 2026-06-18 · **Feed tags:** bkc-happenings, community

> New book by Faculty Associate Tomás Dodds (with Seth Lewis and Rodrigo Zamith), who writes: " I’m excited to share this with you: Seth C. Lewis, Rodrigo Zamith, and I have a new book coming from Polity in August… Although it centers on journalism, the book is really about a set of questions facing anyone living through the AI transition: where creative and intellectual labor is headed, how a handful of platforms are accumulating power over public knowledge, and what it means to learn, think, and stay informed when machines mediate so much of what we know. Journalism, as an institution society depends on to make sense of the world, is where these dynamics are playing out visibly. "
