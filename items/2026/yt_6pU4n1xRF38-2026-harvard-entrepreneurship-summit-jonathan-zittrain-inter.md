# 2026 Harvard Entrepreneurship Summit: Jonathan Zittrain Interviews Polymarket's Shayne Coplan

**Source:** [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6pU4n1xRF38) · **Published:** 2026-05-11 · **By:** Berkman Klein Center · **Feed tags:** bkc-video

> At the keynote session of the 2026 Entrepreneurship Summit hosted by Xfund & the Harvard Business School Entrepreneurship Club, Berkman Klein Center Faculty Director and Xfund Advisor Jonathan Zittrain interviewed Polymarket CEO Shayne Coplan and discussed the emergence of prediction markets and their broader societal implications.
> 
> Questions covered the promise and tensions of prediction markets, with Zittrain asking about potential market manipulation, insider information, bet resolution, geopolitics, regulation, and the broader implications of turning uncertainty into a tradable product.
