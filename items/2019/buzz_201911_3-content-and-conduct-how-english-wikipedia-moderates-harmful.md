** Content and Conduct: How English Wikipedia Moderates Harmful Speech (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2019/content-and-conduct)
------------------------------------------------------------
Our new report concludes that English-language Wikipedia is largely successful at identifying and quickly removing a majority of harmful content. Wikipedia’s approaches and strategies provide valuable lessons to other community-reliant online platforms.

BKC researchers used complementary approaches to assess the degree to which Wikipedia is successful in addressing harmful speech: qualitative interviews with Wikipedians and text analysis using machine learning classifiers trained to identify several variations of problematic speech. Evidence suggests efforts to remove malicious content are more effective on Wikipedia articles compared to efforts on talk pages.

The report focused on the removal of harmful content. A related question is the effectiveness of efforts to prevent harmful speech from occurring on the platform in the first place. Future research could adopt similar tools and approaches to this report to track the incidence of harmful speech and test different interventions to foster pro-social behavior.
