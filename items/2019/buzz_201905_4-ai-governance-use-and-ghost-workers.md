** How Silicon Valley’s successes are fueled by an underclass of ‘ghost workers’ (https://www.theverge.com/2019/5/13/18563284/mary-gray-ghost-work-microwork-labor-silicon-valley-automation-employment-interview)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Verge interviews BKC's Mary Gray about her book, “Ghost Work: How to Stop Silicon Valley from Building a New Global Underclass.”

“I would be hard-pressed to find any business that sells itself as AI that either didn’t deeply rely on ghost work to generate their basic product or isn’t very much reliant on it today,” Gray says.

Read more in The Verge (https://www.theverge.com/2019/5/13/18563284/mary-gray-ghost-work-microwork-labor-silicon-valley-automation-employment-interview)
