Job Opportunities at BKC

** We're Hiring! (https://cyber.harvard.edu/getinvolved/jobs)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Berkman Klein Center is looking to fill a variety of high-impact positions. Help us spread the word!

** Director of Communications (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-08/opportunity-director-communications)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Director of Communications will develop the Berkman Klein Center’s communications strategy and priorities and define how we tell the stories of the Center’s research, education, tools, and global impact.

** Digital Media Manager (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-08/opportunity-digital-media-manager)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Digital Media Manager will lead the production of the Center’s web, social media, and print communications with the goal of increasing the visibility, accessibility, understanding, and reach of the Center.

** Youth and Media Research Assistants (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-08/paid-opportunities-hiring-three-opportunities-youth-and-media-berkman-klein-center)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Youth and Media (YaM) team is looking for new team members with fresh ideas, serious curiosity, passion, and creative drive who are ready to be part of a collaborative team and help YaM with a suite of projects around youth and digital technologies.
Get Involved with BKC

** Join the Assembly 2020 Fellows Cohort (https://www.bkmla.org/fellowship-2020apply)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Assembly Fellowship (https://www.bkmla.org/fellowship-2020about) is an intensive non-residential four-month fellowship for technologists, managers, and policymakers. Assembly Fellows confront problems related to disinformation by creating collaborative provocations or prototypes aimed at better understanding, drawing attention to, and countering disinformation campaigns. Over the course of the fellowship, Assembly Fellows learn and build together with the support of our community.  Apply for the fellowship! (https://www.bkmla.org/fellowship-2020apply)

Harvard Students: Apply for the Assembly Student Fellowship!  (https://www.bkmla.org/studentfellowship-2020apply)

Applications for both fellowships are open until September 15th, 2019.
