** Public opinion lessons for AI regulation (https://www.brookings.edu/research/public-opinion-lessons-for-ai-regulation/)

Baobao Zhang explains how public opinion will likely shape the regulation of three applications of AI in the US: facial recognition technology used by law enforcement, algorithms used by social media platforms, and lethal autonomous weapons.

an exterior image of people working in an office building

** The “Ghost Workers” Underpinning the World’s AI Systems (https://www.cigionline.org/articles/ghost-workers-underpinning-worlds-artificial-intelligence-systems)

Mary Gray discusses the unseen labor that powers the internet and the need for a better social contract when it comes to the rights of informal workers.

The AI project team reflects on their work on the Ethics and Governance of AI Initiative, spanning topics from algorithms and justice, to disinformation, global governance and inclusion, and more.
