** Can Tech be Governed? (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/can-tech-be-governed)
------------------------------------------------------------
Tuesday, September 10 | 12:00pm ET | Harvard Law School Campus & Online

Jonathan Zittrain kicks off the BKC Luncheon Series with "Can Tech be Governed?"

The twenty-odd year mainstream digital revolution has transformed in the public eye from one of promise to threat. This pessimism is reflected in assessments of the latest pervasive technology: AI generally, and machine learning specifically. How different is this technology from what preceded it, and do we need new ways to govern it? If so, how would they come about?
