Youth and Media

** Digital Citizenship+ (Plus) Resource Platform featured by hundrED (https://hundred.org/en/innovations/digital-citizenship-resource-platform-dcrp)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Youth and Media (YaM) team’s educational platform — the Digital Citizenship+ (Plus) Resource Platform (https://dcrp.berkman.harvard.edu) (DCRP) — was featured as an educational innovation in Digital Wellbeing by the non-profit HundrED (https://hundred.org/en) ! On YaM’s HundrED page (https://hundred.org/en/innovations/digital-citizenship-plus-resource-platform-dcrp) , you can explore how and why the team developed the educationals tools on the DCRP, the global reach of the tools, and tips for implementation.

The DCRP is an evolving collection of over 100 educational tools — including learning experiences, visualizations, and podcasts — co-designed with and for youth that can be used to learn and teach about different areas of youth’s (ages 11-18) digitally connected lives. From the home to the classroom to libraries and museums, YaM’s learning tools can be deployed in different spheres and for supporting both individual and group learning. A subset of tools are available in over 35 languages, with more translated content to be added over time. Learn more about the DCRP (https://dcrp.berkman.harvard.edu/about) .

Interested in helping to develop additional educational tools around the digital world? Contact Alexa Hasse (mailto:youthandmedia@cyber.harvard.edu) .
