** Youth and Cyberbullying: Another Look (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2019/youth-and-cyberbullying/another-look)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Youth and Media (YaM) team is excited to share a new spotlight — Youth and Cyberbullying: Another Look (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2019/youth-and-cyberbullying/another-look) — an overview of recent, primarily academic literature on youth (12-18-year-olds) and cyberbullying. The paper explores the nuances around defining cyberbullying and the prevalence of online bullying in and beyond the U.S., and presents practical, impactful guidance on preventing and responding to cyberbullying.

The paper is one of the YaM team’s “spotlights” — short briefing documents that showcase the ways youth engage with the digital landscape, and what opportunities and challenges emerge from it. Additional spotlights will be released over the coming weeks.
