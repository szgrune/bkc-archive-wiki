** Cyberlaw Clinic Announces Launch of New First Amendment Fellowship Program (http://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2019/10/01/cyberlaw-clinic-announces-launch-of-new-first-amendment-fellowship-program/)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Initiative for a Representative First Amendment, directed by Kendra Albert, aims to expand the study of First Amendment, free speech, and freedom of expression issues to include the active participation of legal practitioners and practitioners-in-training who exist at the intersection of multiple marginalized identities. The Initiative seeks to cultivate a broader and more diverse community of freedom of expression practitioners, allowing for heightened engagement on a wide range of free expression issues.
