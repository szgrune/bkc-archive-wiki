Open Calls: Two Upcoming Workshops

** May 23, 2019
------------------------------------------------------------

** Can Data Become Part of a Development Strategy? (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-04/can-data-become-part-development-strategy)
------------------------------------------------------------
Although many governance frameworks and data architectures abound, there is not a single one that fits the needs of digital transformation in developing countries.

Join this day long workshop (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-04/can-data-become-part-development-strategy) hosted by the Digital Initiative at the Harvard Kennedy School and a number of BKC Community members to explore how data can enable new models of cooperative growth.

Abstracts due

** May 31, 2019
------------------------------------------------------------

** Comparative Approaches to Disinformation (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-04/comparative-approaches-disinformation-call-extended-abstracts)
------------------------------------------------------------
From misleading news stories around the 2018 Brazil elections to a lynching linked to false social media messages in India in 2019, the deluge of digital disinformation is affecting communications in many countries around the world.

The Workshop on Disinformation (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-04/comparative-approaches-disinformation-call-extended-abstracts) to be held at Harvard University on October 4, 2019 invites submissions that make new theoretical or empirical contributions to existing bodies of knowledge in this area.

** The Quantified Worker and Worker Surveillance (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-04/quantified-worker-and-worker-surveillance)
------------------------------------------------------------

Job applications are now screened through automated systems, workers are being asked to use wearables like Fitbit for worker wellness programs, and employers are spying on worker smartphones. BKC's Ifeoma Ajunwa discusses how emerging technologies are changing the 21st-century workplace.

Listen and Read more at Tech Empire... (https://soundcloud.com/yaleuniversity/the-quantified-worker-with-ifeoma-ajunwa)
