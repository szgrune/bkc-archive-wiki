** A User-Focused Transdisciplinary Research Agenda for AI-Enabled Health Tech Governance (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-02/user-focused-transdisciplinary-research-agenda-ai-enabled-health-tech-governance)
------------------------------------------------------------

AI-enabled health technology holds significant promise for improving health outcomes and clinical workflows. However, it also generates challenges for health data governance and security. This paper sets forth a research agenda for stakeholders to proactively collaborate and design AI technologies that work with users to improve their health and wellbeing.

------------------------------------------------------------

"In his 2008 white paper that first proposed bitcoin, the anonymous Satoshi Nakamoto concluded with: 'We have proposed a system for electronic transactions without relying on trust.' He was referring to blockchain, the system behind bitcoin cryptocurrency. The circumvention of trust is a great promise, but it’s just not true. Yes, bitcoin eliminates certain trusted intermediaries that are inherent in other payment systems like credit cards. But you still have to trust bitcoin — and everything about it."

** Template License and Collaboration Agreements for AI Art (http://blogs.harvard.edu/cyberlawclinic/2019/02/04/template-license-and-collaboration-agreements-for-ai-art/)
------------------------------------------------------------

Developers and artists are looking for guidance on how to handle rights for AI-generated art.

With this in mind, the Cyberlaw Clinic has launched a template project with agreements for the two most common situations:
* A license (https://github.com/schwettmann/generism/blob/master/Template%20Art%20AI%20License.docx?raw=true)  template, for when someone undertaking an AI art project wants to use existing works of art (music, visual art, writing, etc.) as inputs for their system, for example as training data
* A collaboration agreement (https://github.com/schwettmann/generism/blob/master/Template%20AI%20Art%20Collaboration%20Agreement.docx?raw=true)  template, for when two or more people are working together to build, train, and run an AI art system

Read more at the Cyberlaw Clinic... (http://blogs.harvard.edu/cyberlawclinic/2019/02/04/template-license-and-collaboration-agreements-for-ai-art/)

** Transparency, diversity, philanthropy: The Knight Commission’s final recommendations for 21st-century journalism (http://www.niemanlab.org/2019/02/transparency-diversity-philanthropy-the-knight-commissions-final-recommendations-for-21st-century-journalism/)
------------------------------------------------------------

“We, as individual citizens of a great nation, need to take measures now, not next year, to maintain the democracy that has developed over nearly two and a half centuries,” according to a new report from the 27-member Knight Commission on Trust, Media, and Democracy, which includes the Berkman Klein Center's Jonathan Zittrain. “Democracy and the news media are inextricably intertwined, and it is clear that both are in crisis. American democracy suffers not only from a decline of trust in the ‘other’ to govern, but also from a breakdown of our shared concept of citizenship."
