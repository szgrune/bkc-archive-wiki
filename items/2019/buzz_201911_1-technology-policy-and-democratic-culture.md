** German President Steinmeier discusses digital technology ethics at BKC event (https://today.law.harvard.edu/at-harvard-law-german-president-steinmeier-discusses-digital-technology-ethics/)
------------------------------------------------------------
The President of Germany, Dr. Frank-Walter Steinmeier (https://www.bundespraesident.de/EN/Home/home_node.html) , joined the Berkman Klein Center for a special event to discuss the Ethics of the Digital Transformation. Steinmeier began his talk with a question that he said is key to his presidency and to the future of liberal democracy: How do social media and the internet, particularly Twitter and Facebook, change the democratic culture of debate?

President Steinmeier was joined in conversation by Professors Jeanette Hofmann of the Free University of Berlin; Matthew Liao of New York University; Melissa Nobles of MIT; Wolfgang Schulz of the University of Hamburg; Eva Weber-Guskar of the Humboldt University of Berlin; and Crystal S. Yang of Harvard Law School. BKC Executive Director Urs Gasser hosted and moderated the panel.

Watch the interdisciplinary panel discussion (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=83zjx0k2fTw)  which covered topics ranging from the risks and opportunities of AI to whether law and ethics are equipped to govern the digital transformation.

Berkman Klein Executive Director Urs Gasser is featured in a Harvard Gazette article detailing the long history between Harvard University and Germany. Gasser serves on Angela Merkel’s German Digital Council (https://www.bundeskanzlerin.de/bkin-en/news/the-faces-behind-the-digital-council-1504894) , which advises her government on topics like the role of data and digitizing systems and works on projects such as streamlining applications.
