** Race and Resistance Amid Feminism, Priming, and Capitalism: (https://adanewmedia.org/2018/11/issue14-korn/)
The (surprisingly-globalized) Visual of an Asian American Woman Activist
------------------------------------------------------------

Building upon her research that shows Asian Americans are battling the stereotype that Asian Americans are apathetic about politics, BKC Fellow Jenny Korn uses critical autoethnography, informed by critical race theory and intersectional feminist theory, to examine the origin, selection, and circulation of visuals related to protest, and situates how certain photos are related to race, feminism, activism, and resistance.

** Data Feminism (https://bookbook.pubpub.org/data-feminism)
------------------------------------------------------------

Data Feminism is a book by Catherine D'Ignazio and Lauren Klein for the MIT Press "Strong Voices" series edited by David Weinberger. Building on the past forty years of intersectional feminist scholarship, it sketches a broad outline for what a feminist approach to data science looks like and showcases the work of data scientists, journalists, artists, and activists who are using data for equity and justice.

Read more at BookBook...  (https://bookbook.pubpub.org/data-feminism)

** Data Epistemologies, Coloniality of Power, and Resistance (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1527476419831640)
------------------------------------------------------------

Data assemblages amplify historical forms of colonization through a complex arrangement of practices, materialities, territories, bodies, and subjectivities. In this article, BKC Fellow Paola Ricaurte develops a theoretical model to analyze the coloniality of power through data and explores the multiple dimensions of coloniality as a framework for identifying ways of resisting data colonization.

Read more... (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1527476419831640)
