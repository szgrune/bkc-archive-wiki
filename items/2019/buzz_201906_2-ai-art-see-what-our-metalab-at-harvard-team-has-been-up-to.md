AI + Art at metaLAB: Two Years In
https://metalabharvard.github.io/projects/aiandart/
metaLAB (at) Harvard is the Berkman Klein Center's idea foundry, knowledge-design lab, and production studio experimenting in the networked arts and humanities.

Over the past two years (https://metalabharvard.github.io/projects/aiandart/) , metaLAB (at) Harvard has been engaging with AI + Art through a series of artistic research projects that explore the social and cultural impacts of Artificial Intelligence. The work focuses on pressing questions—some long-standing, others emergent— arising across a host of topics, including privacy, surveillance, human-machine relations, screen-based culture, value alignment, and algorithmic accountability.
Learn More (https://metalabharvard.github.io/projects/aiandart/)

** Making AI topics more accessible.
------------------------------------------------------------
Hundreds of people, young and old, traversed through “The Laughing Room” at Cambridge Public Library in November. An interactive art installation in which participants enter an artificially intelligent room, designed to resemble a sitcom set, The Laughing Room plays a laugh track whenever the participants say something that the room’s algorithm deems to be worth laughing with. The project addresses the increasing social and cultural roles of responsive technologies in public and private spaces, among other big questions.

Read more in Boston Magazine (https://www.bostonmagazine.com/arts-entertainment/2018/11/09/jonny-sun-sitcom-cambridge-laughing-room/)

** Shining a light on invisible human tendencies.
------------------------------------------------------------
"How will we know when a machine becomes sentient?"
"What does it mean to be moral?"

Such are the questions posed in Moral Labyrinth (https://metalabharvard.github.io/projects/morallabyrinth/) , an interactive installation intended for participants to meditate on what can we learn about ourselves by how we engage and interact with technology.

Read more at the Crimson (https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2019/3/7/what-is-metalab/)  and at MozFest (https://medium.com/mozilla-festival/navigating-ais-moral-labyrinth-at-mozfest-40950b59a221)
