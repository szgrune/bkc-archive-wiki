The Latest: Caselaw Access Project Nominated for a Webby!
https://vote.webbyawards.com/PublicVoting#/2019/websites/general-websites/law
Case.law (https://case.law/)  — a tool developed by the Library Innovation Lab that makes more than 360 years of US Caselaw searchable and accessible — was nominated for a Webby award for best law-related website of the year!

The project digitizes roughly 40 million pages of court decisions contained in roughly 40,000 bound volumes owned by the Harvard Law School Library, and makes all published US court decisions freely available to the public online, in a consistent format.
