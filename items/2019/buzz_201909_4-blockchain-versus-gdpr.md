** Forget erasure: why blockchain is really incompatible with the GDPR (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/forget-erasure-why-blockchain-is-really-incompatible-with-the-gdpr-9f60374e90f3)
------------------------------------------------------------
Elizabeth M. Renieris assesses blockchain's compliance with the GDPR's core principles: lawfulness, fairness, and transparency; purpose limitation; data minimization; accuracy; storage limitation; integrity and confidentiality; and accountability.

** A facial recognition ban is coming to the US, says an AI policy advisor (https://www.technologyreview.com/s/614362/a-facial-recognition-ban-is-coming-to-the-us-says-ai-policy-advisor/)
------------------------------------------------------------
Mutale Nkonde discussed potential federal restrictions on the use of facial recognition technology at EmTech 2019. “The marginalization of minority groups by facial recognition is step one [toward a ban],” Nkonde said.  When it is used to target groups with more power, it will be outlawed, she said.

** No Rage Against the Machines (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3455501)
------------------------------------------------------------
The threat of automation does not change policy preferences,  Baobao Zhang finds in a new paper.  Workers informed that automation threatens American jobs “failed to change [their] preferences on welfare, immigration, and trade policies."
