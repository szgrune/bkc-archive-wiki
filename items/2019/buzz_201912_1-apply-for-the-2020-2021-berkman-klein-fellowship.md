** Open Call for Fellowship Applications, Academic Year 2020-2021 (https://cyber.harvard.edu/getinvolved/fellowships/2021Fellows)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society at Harvard University is now accepting fellowship applications for the 2020-2021 academic year through our annual open call.

This opportunity is for those who wish to spend 2020-2021 in residence in Cambridge, MA as part of the Center’s vibrant community of research and practice, and who seek to engage in collaborative, cross-disciplinary, and cross-sectoral exploration of some of the Internet’s most important and compelling issues.

Check out the full call for applications for more information about our program and funding opportunities, as well as application instructions. Applications are due on January 31, 2020, at 11:59pm Eastern Time.

From Our Community

** AI Innovators Should Be Listening to Kids (https://www.wired.com/story/ai-innovators-should-be-listening-to-kids/)

Urs Gasser makes the case for including young people in our conversations about artificial intelligence. “Young people have a right to participate as we make critical choices that will determine what kind of technological world we leave for them and future generations. They also have unique perspectives to contribute as the first generation to grow up surrounded by AI shaping their education, health, social lives, leisure, and career prospects.”

** Robots Are Taking Over (the Rental Screening Process) (https://www.wsj.com/articles/robots-are-taking-over-the-rental-screening-process-11574332200)

Mutale Nkonde spoke to The Wall Street Journal about how landlords are using artificial intelligence to vet prospective renters. She sees “red flags” in algorithmic screening but “because the algorithms are protected by intellectual property laws, we have no way of scrutinizing them.”

** What should newsrooms do about deepfakes?  (https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/11/what-should-newsrooms-do-about-deepfakes-these-three-things-for-starters/)

John Bowers, Tim Hwang, and Jonathan Zittrain argue the dangers of deepfakes are overstated, but they will still require journalists to give thought to how they handle potentially fabricated media.

New Opportunity for Law Students!
** (https://www.ifrfa.org/about)

** Apply for the Initiative for a Representative First Amendment (https://www.ifrfa.org/about)

The Initiative for a Representative First Amendment (IfRFA) aims to expand the study of First Amendment, free speech, and freedom of expression issues to include the active participation of legal practitioners and practitioners-in-training who exist at the intersection of multiple marginalized identities.

IfRFA, based out of the Cyberlaw Clinic, provides financial support and career opportunities for law students from backgrounds traditionally underrepresented in First Amendment law. ** APPLY NOW (https://www.ifrfa.org/about)
