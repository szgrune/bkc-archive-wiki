** Cautionary Notes on Disinformation and the Origins of Distrust (https://mediawell.ssrc.org/expert-reflections/cautionary-notes-on-disinformation-benkler/)
------------------------------------------------------------
“Nonstop coverage of propaganda efforts and speculation about their impact, without actual evidence to support that impact, feeds the loss of trust in our institutions to a greater extent than the facts warrant,” writes Yochai Benkler.

** Facebook’s new Oversight Board is a step forward – but it can’t help Kashmiris (https://scroll.in/article/940020/facebooks-new-oversight-board-is-a-step-forward-but-it-cant-help-kashmiris)
------------------------------------------------------------
Facebook's Oversight Board is potentially a game-changing step but will do nothing about the platform's over-compliance with governments' efforts to censor speech, argue Chinmayi Arun and Agustina Del Campo.

** Cyberlaw Clinic teams up with Cathy O’Neil for comment re: algorithmic discrimination (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-10/cyberlaw-clinic-teams-cathy-oneil-hud-comment)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Cyberlaw Clinic collaborated with Cathy O’Neil (https://mathbabe.org/) on a comment filed with the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. The comment concerns a proposed rule that would make it harder for plaintiffs to sue in housing discrimination cases when housing eligibility determinations are made algorithmically.
