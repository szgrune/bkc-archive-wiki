# Zoning Speech on the Internet: A Legal and Technical Model

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/1999/Zoning_Speech_on_the_Internet) · **Published:** 1999-12-15 · **By:** Larry Lessig · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> Introduction:
> 
> Speech, it is said,1 divides into three sorts — (1) speech that everyone has a right to (political speech, speech about public affairs); (2) speech that no one has a right to (obscene speech, child porn); and (3) speech that some have a right to but others do not (in the United States, Ginsberg2 speech, or speech that is “harmful to minors,” to which adults have a right but kids do not).
