------------------------------------------------------------

The Network of Centers launched a project on the practice of digital ethics, engaging interdisciplinary networks of scholars, students, and civil society groups to advance outputs with social relevance. Watch German Federal President Dr. Frank-Walter Steinmeier announce the project (https://www.bundespraesident.de/SharedDocs/Reden/EN/Frank-Walter-Steinmeier/Reden/2020/08/200817-ethics-of-digitalisation.html) and representatives from participating centers, including Amar Ashar of BKC and faculty associate Malavika Jayaram of Digital Asia Hub, discuss the importance of the initiative (https://www.bundespraesident.de/SharedDocs/Berichte/EN/Frank-Walter-Steinmeier/2020/200817-Ethics-of-Digitalisation.html?nn=9042654) .

Chan School of Public Health, for a second installment of "Covid State of Play," to discuss COVID-19 testing, school reopenings, sick buildings, and ventilation.

** Beware of Apps Bearing Gifts in a Pandemic (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/beware-of-apps-bearing-gifts-in-a-pandemic-490fabaade01)
------------------------------------------------------------
Woodrow Hartzog, Christo Wilson, and a team of researchers share concerns about free services and public health interventions by tech companies. “Tech companies are taking advantage of our generosity, fear, isolation, and resignation caused by the pandemic to collect more data than ever.”
