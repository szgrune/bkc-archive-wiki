** Building a Public Sector Health Intelligence Capability (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/covid-state-play-building-public-sector-health-intelligence-capability)
------------------------------------------------------------

Tracking the spread of COVID-19 has proved critical, but to do so, public health officials need to collect and utilize large amounts of data. Tarah Wheeler joins Margaret Bourdeaux and Jonathan Zittrain to investigate how the United States Public Health System can do this differently and responsibly.
