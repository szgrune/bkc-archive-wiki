A new story on ‘Their Names,’ a project from metaLAB, gathers the stories of 28,000 people, from Jan. 1, 2000, to May 25, 2020. Created by Matthew Battles and Kim Albrecht, the visualization shares names of people killed by police. “The visualizations on this data all focus...on statistics, and how this problem developed over the years.
