** New paper: The false positive problem of automatic bot detection (https://cyber.harvard.edu/news/2020-03/false-positive-problem-automatic-bot-detection-social-science-research)
------------------------------------------------------------
The identification of bots is an important and complicated task. The bot classifier Botometer was successfully introduced as a way to estimate the number of bots in a given list of accounts and has been frequently used in academic publications. However,  new research from Adrian Rauchfleisch and BKC’s Jonas Kaiser shows that the Botometer scores are imprecise when it comes to estimating bots, especially in a different language. They further show that Botometer's thresholds, even when used very conservatively, are prone to variance, which, in turn, will lead to bots being classified as humans and humans being classified as bots.
