Governments are increasingly using digital technologies and big data analytics to address the Covid-19 pandemic. At this stage, these technologies may not deliver their promise and will further entrench surveillance in our societies and erode the rule of law. Beatriz Botero Arcila argues that it doesn’t need to be that way.
