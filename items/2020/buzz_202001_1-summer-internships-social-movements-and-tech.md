** We’re Hiring Summer Interns! (https://cyber.harvard.edu/getinvolved/internships2020)
------------------------------------------------------------
Projects across the Berkman Klein Center are seeking undergraduate and graduate students to join their teams as interns for summer 2020!

In addition to contributing substantially to Berkman Klein projects, interns will participate in special lectures with BKC faculty and fellows, engage each other through community experiences like weekly intern discussion hours, and attend Center-wide events and gatherings with members of the wider BKC community.
LEARN MORE & APPLY (https://cyber.harvard.edu/getinvolved/internships2020)
The Cyberlaw Clinic is also looking for US law students to apply for the Cyberlaw Clinic Legal Internship—details about this program, including the application, can be found here (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2019-12/opportunity-cyberlaw-clinic-interns-summer-2020) .
