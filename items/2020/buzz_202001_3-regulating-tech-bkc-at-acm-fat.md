** The Secretive Company That Might End Privacy as We Know It (https://www.nytimes.com/2020/01/18/technology/clearview-privacy-facial-recognition.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

Woodrow Hartzog spoke to The New York Times about the harrowing consequences of facial recognition
“I don’t see a future where we harness the benefits of face recognition technology without the crippling abuse of the surveillance that comes with it. The only way to stop it is to ban it,” Hartzog said.

abstract illustration of faces and money

** We’re Banning Facial Recognition. We’re Missing the Point. (https://www.nytimes.com/2020/01/20/opinion/facial-recognition-ban-privacy.html)
------------------------------------------------------------

“We need to have a serious conversation about all the technologies of identification, correlation and discrimination, and decide how much we as a society want to be spied on by governments and corporations — and what sorts of influence we want them to have over our lives,” writes Bruce Schneier.

An illustration of a pixelated gavel

** Google and Microsoft shouldn't decide how technology is regulated (https://www.fastcompany.com/90454760/google-and-microsoft-shouldnt-decide-how-technology-is-regulated)
------------------------------------------------------------

“Looking only to the perspectives of large tech companies, who’ve already established themselves as dominant players, is asking the fox for guidance on henhouse security procedures," writes Jessica Fjeld. "We need to take a broader view."
