** Martha Minow on Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg's legal legacy (https://www.wgbh.org/news/national-news/2020/09/21/boston-public-radio-full-show-9-21-20)
------------------------------------------------------------

“One of the truly great, great qualities of Ruth Bader Ginsburg was not only was she brilliant, but she was humane. She never forgot that there were human beings involved in these cases. And that informed her writing and her judgments, and made her a truly great justice,” said Martha Minow, joining Boston Public Radio.
