** BKC Affiliate Armando Guio Español Shares Reflections on Drafting Colombia’s AI Ethical Framework and His Time at BKC (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/q-a-armando-guio-español-on-ai-ethics-and-policy-9aabb756ec13)
------------------------------------------------------------
Colombia recently released a draft AI ethics framework,“Ethical Framework for Artificial Intelligence in Colombia.” (https://cyber.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/2020-12/Colombia_AI_Ethical_Framework.pdf) Drafted by Armando Guio Español, HLS alum, BKC affiliate, and former BKC fellow, the framework is an important step in implementing Colombia’s national AI policy. Read our Q&A (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/q-a-armando-guio-español-on-ai-ethics-and-policy-9aabb756ec13) with Armando to hear his reflections on the framework and how working at BKC impacted and contributed to his experience creating the draft.

------------------------------------------------------------

BKC Policy Practice: AI (https://cyber.harvard.edu/programs/bkc-policy-practice-artificial-intelligence)  offered feedback to the Colombian government on its draft AI ethical framework. At a launch event for the draft framework (https://youtu.be/CgUhnPsZ-OY) , Urs Gasser discussed with Colombian President Iván Duque Márquez the important role that governments can play in shaping AI policy.

** Law Students: Apply for the Initiative for a Representative First Amendment (https://www.ifrfa.org/about)
------------------------------------------------------------
The Initiative for a Representative First Amendment (IfRFA), based out of the Cyberlaw Clinic, provides financial support and career opportunities for law students from backgrounds traditionally underrepresented in First Amendment law. To learn more about the IfRFA fellowship, watch a conversation (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=713UMSJnfok)  between IfRFA Director Kendra Albert and two of the first year cohort fellows about their experiences.
Applications are due on January 11, 2021.

IfRFA will also be hosting "A More Representative First Amendment?" (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/more-representative-first-amendment) a discussion with Professors Khaled Beydoun and Justin Hansford and Kendra Albert of the ways in which First Amendment work could better engage with critical race theory. Learn more and register here (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/more-representative-first-amendment) .
