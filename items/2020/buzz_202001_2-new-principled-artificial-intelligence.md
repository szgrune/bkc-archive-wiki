** Principled Artificial Intelligence (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2020/principled-ai)
------------------------------------------------------------
Berkman Klein’s latest research report Principled Artificial Intelligence: Mapping Consensus in Ethical and Rights-based Approaches to Principles for AI and its associated data visualization compare the contents of thirty-six prominent AI principles documents side-by-side.

The results uncovered a growing consensus around eight key thematic trends: privacy, accountability, safety and security, transparency and explainability, fairness and non-discrimination, human control of technology, professional responsibility, and promotion of human values.
