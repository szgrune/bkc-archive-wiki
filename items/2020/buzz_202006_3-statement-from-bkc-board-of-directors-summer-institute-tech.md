** Introducing the BKC Summer Institute (https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2020-06/bkc-summer-institute-teaches-students-about-critical-areas-internet-society)
------------------------------------------------------------
A cohort of 23 undergraduate and graduate students from around the world will embark on a four- week educational experience through BKC's new Summer Institute, a fully online program that focuses on AI Policy and Inclusion, Media and Content Moderation, and Information for Decision Makers.

https://theirnames.org/

** metaLAB Releases “Their Names” Visualization (https://theirnames.org/)
------------------------------------------------------------
“Their Names” is a visualization that contends with the stories of over 28,000 police-involved deaths, 2000–2020, based on the Fatal Encounters (https://fatalencounters.org/)  dataset. Beyond listing first names and initials, the site makes available the news and police reports from which the data were gleaned.

** Brian Scully on Government Response to Disinformation (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/the-breakdown-brian-scully-on-government-response-to-disinformation-c1e21f20fe0)
------------------------------------------------------------

In the latest episode of The Breakdown, BKC staff fellow Oumou Ly interviews Brian Scully of the Department of Homeland Security and a 2019–20 member of the Assembly professional fellowship cohort to discuss how DHS is responding to the pandemic and preparing for the 2020 election.

https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/technology-and-contact-tracing-627c09ec5061

** Technology and Contact Tracing (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/technology-and-contact-tracing-627c09ec5061)
------------------------------------------------------------

Experts from tech and public health sectors, including BKC’s Jonathan Zittrain and Mary Gray, share open questions for stakeholders engaging in tech and contact tracing at a panel hosted by TechCrunch, NYU’s Alliance for Public Interest Technology, Betaworks Studios, Hangar, and BKC.
