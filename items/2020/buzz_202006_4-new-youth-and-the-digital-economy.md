** Youth and the Digital Economy (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2020/youth-and-digital-economy)
------------------------------------------------------------
As people engage in a digital platform ecosystem where the boundaries between leisure, play, and work are blurred, what are the challenges and opportunities they face? Who can develop skills through online activities and benefit from earning different kinds of capital, and who cannot? What can we all learn by taking a closer look at young people in today’s digital economy?

Youth and the Digital Economy: Exploring Youth Practices, Motivations, Skills, Pathways, and Value Creation (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2020/youth-and-digital-economy) , a newly released report from Youth and Media (YaM) at BKC, in collaboration with the Nordic Centre for Internet and Society, highlights both the tremendous opportunities for young people to participate as economic actors as well as the amplification of structural inequalities and the complex risks involved.
