# Copyright for Librarians: an online open curriculum on copyright law

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2010/copyright_for_librarians) · **Published:** 2010-03-23 · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> In March, 2010, the Berkman Center launched an online, open access curriculum, "Copyright for Librarians," developed in conjunction with eIFL.net. "Copyright for Librarians" aims to inform librarians about copyright law in general, as well as the aspects of copyright law that most affect libraries, especially those in developing and transition countries.
