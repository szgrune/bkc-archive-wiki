The Interplay Between Decentralization and Privacy
------------------------------------------------------------
Inthis paper (http://peerproduction.net/issues/issue-9-alternative-internets/peer-reviewed-papers/the-interplay-between-decentralization-and-privacy-the-case-of-blockchain-technologies/) in the
Journal of Peer Production, Primavera De Filippi (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/pdefilippi)  "analyses the case of Bitcoin and other blockchain-based networks, as an example of decentralized infrastructures which suffers from radical transparency. While they provide a series of privacy benefits to end-users, the characteristics of these networks present both advantages and risks to the privacy of end-users."

New Research from the Berkman Klein Publication Series (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications)
https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99639Translating Research for Action: Ideas and Examples for Informing Digital Policy
This series of four papers (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99639) aims to build a bridge between academic research and policymaking in the networked world by helping to identify opportunities in key areas related to digital technology and innovation. The papers include: Networked Policy Making Avenues: Assessing the Role of Academics in Digital Policy (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2016/NetworkedPolicymaking) ; and three research briefings: Student Privacy and Ed Tech (K-12) (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2016/StudentPrivacyBriefing) ; Privacy and Open Data (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2016/OpenDataBriefing) ; and Privacy and Cybersecurity (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2016/CybersecurityBriefing) .
