# Does Public Attention Reduce the Influence of Interest Groups?

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2016/PolicyPositionsonSOPA-PIPA) · **Published:** 2016-12-12 · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> By Dr. Ulrich Matter and Prof. Dr. Alois Stutzer
> 
> In this paper, researchers investigate the role that public attention plays in determining the effect that campaign contributions funded by interests groups have on legislators’ policy positions. In so doing, we exploit the Internet service blackout of January 2012 as a quasi-experiment in which a shock increases the salience of the SOPA/PIPA bills aimed at securing stronger protection of property rights on the Internet.
