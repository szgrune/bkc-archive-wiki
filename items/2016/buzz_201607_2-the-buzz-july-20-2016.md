** How the Internet Saved Turkey’s Internet-Hating President.
------------------------------------------------------------
In this piece (http://www.nytimes.com/2016/07/20/opinion/how-the-internet-saved-turkeys-internet-hating-president.html?_r=0) for The New York Times, Zeynep Tufekci (https://cyber.law.harvard.edu/people/zeynep) describes the role of the technology, social media, and an open Internet played a significant role in the failed coup in Turkey last week. "A free press and open internet have proved essential to everyone — even those at the height of power," she writes.

TheDAO and the $50M Hack.
------------------------------------------------------------
In this piece (http://motherboard.vice.com/read/thedao) for Motherboard, Primavera De Filippi (https://cyber.law.harvard.edu/people/pdefilippi) examines how a $50M hack tested the values of communities run by code. Police robots and deadly force.
------------------------------------------------------------
"If appropriate rules and regulations can be agreed upon by law enforcement and society, the use of robots by the police is very promising," writes Yana Welinder (https://cyber.law.harvard.edu/people/ywelinder) inthis piece (http://www.nytimes.com/roomfordebate/2016/07/14/what-ethics-should-guide-the-use-of-robots-in-policing/police-robots-could-reduce-the-use-of-deadly-force) for The New York Times. "It is important that law enforcement establishes precedents for using minimal force now, before those precedents are set incidentally."

Is it really illegal to share your Netflix password?
------------------------------------------------------------
It's complicated, as Josephine Wolff (https://cyber.law.harvard.edu/people/jwolff) explains in this piece (http://www.slate.com/articles/technology/future_tense/2016/07/is_it_really_illegal_to_share_your_netflix_password.html) for Slate about the confusion over the 30-year-old Computer Fraud and Abuse Act. "Often," she writes, her students "have questions about what those rulings might mean in the context of their own lives and behavior—and all too often, those questions have no clear answers."
Watch: Michel Bauwens: Are We Shifting to a New Post-Capitalist Value Regime?
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CltUp19s9lc
Every 500 years or so, European civilization and now world civilization, has been rocked by fundamental shifts in its value regime, in which the rules of the game for acquiring wealth and livelihoods have dramatically changed. In this recent talk (https://cyber.law.harvard.edu/events/luncheons/2016/05/Bauwens) , Michel Bauwens, the founder and director of the P2P Foundation, argues that there is consistent evidence that the structural crises of the dominant political economy is leading to responses that are prefigurative of a new value regime.
