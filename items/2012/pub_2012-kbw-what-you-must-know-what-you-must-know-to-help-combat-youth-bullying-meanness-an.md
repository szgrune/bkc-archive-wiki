# What You Must Know to Help Combat Youth Bullying, Meanness, and Cruelty

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2012/kbw_what_you_must_know) · **Published:** 2012-02-28 · **By:** John Palfrey, danah boyd · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> In order to empower youth to create a kinder and braver world, we must
> begin by making sure that youth are safe.  Youth are not safe when they
> are being bullied, harassed, or threatened.  Thus, one of the first
> things that we must do to help youth be safe is combat the culture of
> meanness and cruelty that is at the root of bullying, peer violence, and abuse. Bullying is a systems problem and many well-intended people
> don’t realize the complexity of the issue.  This document
