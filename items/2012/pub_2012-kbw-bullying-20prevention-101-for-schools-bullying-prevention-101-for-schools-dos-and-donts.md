# Bullying Prevention 101 for Schools: Dos and Don’ts

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2012/kbw_bullying%20prevention_101_for_schools) · **Published:** 2012-02-23 · **By:** Susan Swearer, Mia Doces, Lisa Jones, Anne Collier · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> This document provides a list of dos and don’ts for schools in
> developing anti-bullying practices and policies. The purpose of this
> document is to provide concrete ways in which schools 1) can assess if
> they’re doing the right things; 2) have tactical recommendations aimed
> at improving their school culture, curricula, and school policies.
> Findings are grounded in research findings on actions and activities
> that have been shown to help schools improve anti-bullying efforts.
