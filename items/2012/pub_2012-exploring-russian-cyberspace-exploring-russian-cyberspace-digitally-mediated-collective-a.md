# Exploring Russian Cyberspace: Digitally-Mediated Collective Action and the Networked Public Sphere

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2012/exploring_russian_cyberspace) · **Published:** 2012-03-02 · **By:** Bruce Etling, Robert Faris, John Palfrey, Urs Gasser, Hal Roberts, John Kelly, Karina Alexanyan, Vladimir Barash · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> This paper summarizes the major findings of a three-year research project to investigate the Internet’s impact on Russian politics, media and society. We employed multiple methods to study online activity: the mapping and study of the structure, communities and content of the blogosphere; an analogous mapping and study of Twitter; content analysis of different media sources using automated and human-based evaluation approaches; and a survey of bloggers; augmented by infrastructure mapping,
