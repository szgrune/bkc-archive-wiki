# Changing the Culture: Ideas for Student Action

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2012/kbw_changing_the_culture) · **Published:** 2012-02-23 · **By:** Susan Swearer, Mia Doces, Lisa Jones, Anne Collier · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> The purpose of this document is to provide positive action items that
> students can take to make their schools and communities healthier places and to challenge meanness and cruelty. It is presented as a list of
> ideas. While none of these initiatives have been evaluated, they are
> grounded in a research-driven understanding of interventions, practices, and actions that can be helpful in improving school culture.
