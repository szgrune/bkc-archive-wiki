# Out of the Box: Positive Development & Social Change Through the Arts

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/97239) · **Published:** 2012-12-17 · **By:** Ping Ho · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> From early childhood to adolescence, the arts can foster youth movements for social change through positive development. Students who experience high levels of arts enrichment show greater academic performance, enrollment in and graduation from high school and college, civic and community service, news consumption, and engagement in local politics.
