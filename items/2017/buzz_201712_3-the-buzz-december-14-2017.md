** Berkman Klein at International Governance Forum 2017 (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/100118)
------------------------------------------------------------
This week (December 17-21) marks the 12th annual meeting of the Internet Governance Forum (IGF) (https://www.igf2017.swiss/) , a multistakeholder forum for policy dialogue on issues of Internet governance, held this year in Geneva, Switzerland.

The Berkman Klein Center is contributing to key discussions about some of the most pressing issues of our increasingly networked world, including the ethics and governance of artificial intelligence (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zaoTefpWhWU) , AI and inclusion (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xDDt0l-zX7w) , and harmful speech online (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ItE4JYl8UDs) .
