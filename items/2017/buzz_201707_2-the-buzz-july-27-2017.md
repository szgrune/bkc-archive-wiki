Reminder: Applications are due Monday to join The Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society and the MIT Media Lab for Assembly 2018 (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99891) .
https://cyber.harvard.edu/newsroom/2017_2018_community

Assembly 2018 (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99891) is a program that brings together 15 to 20 participants from various backgrounds to collaborate on one or more projects that address a specific tech problem. The topic for 2018 will be artificial intelligence and governance. Applications are open until Monday, July 31, 2017, at 11:50pm ET.
Find Out More! (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99891)
