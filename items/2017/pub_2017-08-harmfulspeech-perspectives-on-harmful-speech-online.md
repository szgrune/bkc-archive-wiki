# Perspectives on Harmful Speech Online

**Source:** [cyber.harvard.edu](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/08/harmfulspeech) · **Published:** 2017-08-14 · **By:** Nani Jansen Reventlow, Jon Penney, Amy Johnson, Reynol Junco, Casey Tilton, Kate Coyer, Grace Mutung'u, Susan Benesch, Andres Lombana-Bermudez, Helmi Noman, Kendra Albert, Anke Sterzing, Felix Oberholzer-Gee, Simin Kargar, J. Nathan Matias · **Feed tags:** bkc-publication

> This collection of essays includes perspectives on and approaches to harmful speech online from a wide range of voices within the Berkman Klein Center community. Recognizing that harmful speech online is an increasingly prevalent issue within society, we intend for the collection to highlight diverse views and strands of thought and to make them available to a wide range of audiences.
> 
> We issued an open call to our community for short pieces that respond to issues related to harmful speech
