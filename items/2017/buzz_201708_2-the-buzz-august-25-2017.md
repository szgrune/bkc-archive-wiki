New Research
https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99982
This week the Berkman Klein Center released (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99982) a comprehensive analysis of online media and social media coverage of the 2016 presidential campaign. The report, "Partisanship, Propaganda, and Disinformation: Online Media and the 2016 U.S. Presidential Election (http://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/08/mediacloud) ," documents how highly partisan right-wing sources helped shape mainstream press coverage and seize the public’s attention in the 18-month period leading up to the election.

Read more about the study in The New York Times (https://www.nytimes.com/2017/08/16/magazine/breitbart-alt-right-steve-bannon.html?_r=0) , The Washington Post, (https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/the-fix/wp/2017/08/22/trump-backers-disturbing-reliance-on-hoax-and-conspiracy-theory-websites-in-1-chart/) Salon.com (http://www.salon.com/2017/08/23/unfair-and-unbalanced-media-defined-trump-by-his-key-issues-and-hillary-clinton-by-her-phony-scandals/) , Mother Jones (http://www.motherjones.com/kevin-drum/2017/08/fake-news-its-mostly-a-right-wing-phenomenon/) , the Daily Dot (https://www.dailydot.com/layer8/trump-clinton-news-consumption/) , and at billmoyers.com (http://billmoyers.com/story/future-democracy-read-media-bias-report/) .

Algorithms in the Criminal Justice System (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99985)
There is an obscure, but fast-growing area in the field of criminal justice: risk assessment software, powered by sophisticated and sometimes proprietary algorithms, to predict whether individual criminals are likely candidates for recidivism. A new paper (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99985)  from the Responsive Communities (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99650https://cyber.harvard.edu/research/responsivecommunities) project focuses on the incorporation of risk assessment software into the criminal sentencing process, and offers a set of key considerations and questions for further research that can help local policymakers who are currently implementing or considering implementing similar systems.
