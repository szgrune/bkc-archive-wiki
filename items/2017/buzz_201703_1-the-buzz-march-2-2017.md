Open Data Privacy: a new Responsive Communities report
Responsive Communities recently published the Open Data Privacy Playbook (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/02/opendataprivacyplaybook) , a report about how cities can handle data governance better. While sharing data has important benefits, a citizen's privacy is also of the utmost concern. Read the report (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/02/opendataprivacyplaybook) by Ben Green, Gabe Cunningham, Ariel Ekblaw, Paul Kominers, Andrew Linzer, and Susan Crawford to learn more about how cities can manage their data and protect their citizens.

Accessing deceased relatives' emails (https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2017/02/27/clinic-files-amicus-brief-supporting-familys-right-to-access-dead-relatives-emails/)
When a relative passes away, what happens to their digital life? Last week, the Cyberlaw Clinic (https://cyber.harvard.edu/teaching/cyberlawclinic) filed an Amicus Brief (https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2017/02/27/clinic-files-amicus-brief-supporting-familys-right-to-access-dead-relatives-emails/)  that supports the position that families should be able to access their dead relatives' data, particularly that with sentimental or financial value. Learn more (https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2017/02/27/clinic-files-amicus-brief-supporting-familys-right-to-access-dead-relatives-emails/) about the Brief and the case.
