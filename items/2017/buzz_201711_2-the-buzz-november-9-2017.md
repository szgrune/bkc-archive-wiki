Read: Recent news and commentary from around the community
Risk Assessment Tools and Criminal Justice  (https://medium.com/@cbavitz/the-following-letter-signed-by-harvard-and-mit-based-faculty-staff-and-researchers-chelsea-7a0cf3e925e9)
In this open letter (https://medium.com/@cbavitz/the-following-letter-signed-by-harvard-and-mit-based-faculty-staff-and-researchers-chelsea-7a0cf3e925e9) to the Massachusetts Legislature, researchers from Harvard and MIT, including members of BKC faculty and staff, outline areas for further study and policy development related to the adoption of risk assessment tools as part of ongoing criminal justice reform efforts in the Commonwealth.
How Kids Can Help Teach Us About AI (http://www.hyperorg.com/blogger/2017/11/05/liveblog-stefania-druga-on-how-kids-can-help-teach-us-about-ai/)
MIT Media Lab's Stefania Druga (https://www.media.mit.edu/people/sdruga/overview/) , led a recent discussion hosted by the Ethics and Governance of Artificial Intelligence Initiative (https://cyber.harvard.edu/research/ai) about research with children and artificial intelligence devices. David Weinberger (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/dweinberger)  recorded the talk in this live blog post (http://www.hyperorg.com/blogger/2017/11/05/liveblog-stefania-druga-on-how-kids-can-help-teach-us-about-ai/) . "The real question," he writes is "How can children and parents leverage intelligent agents for learning, or for other good ends?"
Instagram, Meme-Seeding, and the Truth about Facebook Manipulation, Pt. 1 (https://medium.com/@d1gi/instagram-meme-seeding-and-the-truth-about-facebook-manipulation-pt-1-dae4d0b61db5)
In this Medium post (https://medium.com/@d1gi/instagram-meme-seeding-and-the-truth-about-facebook-manipulation-pt-1-dae4d0b61db5) , BKC Faculty Associate Jonathan Albright (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jalbright) argues that Instagram "Instagram — a service larger than Twitter and Snapchat combined — should be seen as a major influence, targeting and engagement hub for the spread of political propaganda."
Humanitarian Disasters in the Networked Age (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/humanitarian-disasters-in-the-networked-age-f81fbbce94ce)
In a recent BKC lunch talk (https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/2017/luncheon/10/Raymond) , Nathan Raymond of the Harvard Humanitarian Initiative (HHI) argued that we need a rethinking of humanitarian technological interventions through the lens of human rights. David Talbot (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/98856) and Donica O'Malley report on the talk in this Medium post (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/humanitarian-disasters-in-the-networked-age-f81fbbce94ce) .
Bruce Schneier on the Equifax Breach (https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2017/11/me_on_the_equif.html)
BKC Fellow Bruce Schneier (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/bschneier)  testified before the House Energy and Commerce Committee last week regarding the Equifax hack. "Unless Congress acts to protect consumer information in the digital age, these breaches will continue,"   he said. You can read the full testimony on his blog (https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2017/11/me_on_the_equif.html) .
