#AI: The Robots Are Coming (http://www.bostonmagazine.com/news/2017/11/12/ai-research-boston/)
In this Boston Magazine article (http://www.bostonmagazine.com/news/2017/11/12/ai-research-boston/) , Dave Talbot (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/98856)  reports on efforts by Harvard and MIT academics (including Chris Bavitz (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/cbavitz) ) and business leaders to anticipate and combat the potential negative effects of artificial intelligence. These researchers are "are pushing ahead to give users control, democratize powerful technologies, and make sure they are used for the public good," Talbot writes.
#AI Does Intermediary Immunity Keep the Rest of Us Healthy? (https://www.law.com/therecorder/sites/therecorder/2017/11/10/cda-230-then-and-now-does-intermediary-immunity-keep-the-rest-of-us-healthy/?slreturn=20171015090245)
Section 230 of the US Communications Decency Act gave internet content platforms immunity from things that their users publish, such as posts in a comments section. However, as BKC Co-Founder Jonathan Zittrain (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jzittrain)  explains in this article from The Recorder (https://www.law.com/therecorder/sites/therecorder/2017/11/10/cda-230-then-and-now-does-intermediary-immunity-keep-the-rest-of-us-healthy/?slreturn=20171015090245) , "The internet environment of 2017 is a lot different than that of 1997, and some of those two-people-in-a-garage ventures are now among the most powerful and valuable companies in the world." He asks, "does it make sense to maintain §230’s immunities today?"
#AI: The Real Danger of Artificial Intelligence: It’s Not What You Think (https://hackernoon.com/the-real-danger-of-artificial-intelligence-its-not-what-you-think-f7fdc7059cf8)
João Duarte summarizes key points from the Global Symposium: Artificial Intelligence & Inclusion (https://blogs.harvard.edu/aiandinclusionsymposium/)  (co-hosted by BKC), in this piece from Hacker Noon (https://hackernoon.com/the-real-danger-of-artificial-intelligence-its-not-what-you-think-f7fdc7059cf8) . Some of his takeaways include: "AI is forcing us to rethink ethical and moral principles," "there is no such thing as AI neutrality," and finally, "AI needs diversity for ensuring success."
Online Harassment and Democracy (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/online-harassment-of-women-journalists-and-international-law-not-just-a-gender-issue-but-a-b8c6a5c7e128)
"Online harassment of women journalists hinders the free press from operating as it should, which negatively affects the democratic process," writes Nani Jansen Reventlow (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/njansen) in this Medium piece (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/online-harassment-of-women-journalists-and-international-law-not-just-a-gender-issue-but-a-b8c6a5c7e128) . "There is a clear obligation of States to protect these rights and guarantee a pluralistic media landscape — a media landscape that includes women’s voices."
P2P Models and a new collaborative economy (http://p2pmodels.eu/)
BKC Faculty Associate Samer Hassan (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/shassan)  has been awarded a European Research Council grant for his project, P2P Models, described as "a large research project to build Blockchain-powered organizations which are decentralized, democratic and distribute their profits, in order to boost a new type of Collaborative Economy." You can learn more about the project here (http://p2pmodels.eu/)  and from this Twitter stream (https://twitter.com/samerP2P/status/928820944705130498) .
Positive Social Relationships, Mentorship, and Constructive Dialogue (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/positive-social-relationships-and-mentorship-connected-camps-governance-strategy-for-kid-friendly-94a6f98502da)
Inthis case study (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/positive-social-relationships-and-mentorship-connected-camps-governance-strategy-for-kid-friendly-94a6f98502da) , BKC Fellow Andres Lombana-Bermudez (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/alombana) , analyzes  the governance strategy and moderation scheme designed and implemented by Connected Camps, a private company that offers learning experiences for 15-18 year olds. "In contrast to other “kid-friendly” virtual worlds that focus on policing, rule enforcement, and protecting youth, Connected Camps’ governance strategy emphasizes constructive and productive dialogue between players and moderators," he writes.
