The Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society at Harvard University and the MIT Media Lab are excited to announce the launch of the 2018 Assembly program (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99891) , a program that brings together 15 to 20 participants from various backgrounds to collaborate on one or more projects that address a specific tech problem. Enabling Governments to be Ready for Big Data and Artificial Intelligence (http://ict4dev.org/enabling-governments-ready-big-data-artificial-intelligence/)
How can governments catch up on big data and artificial intelligence? Berkman Klein Affiliate Olivier Alais (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/oalais) has written a blog post arguing that governments should improve their ICT policies to focus on data governance to be ready for artificial intelligence, while respecting data privacy, net neutrality, cyber security and competition policy.
http://www.cbsnews.com/news/the-great-divide-the-media-war-over-trump/
The Great Divide: The Media War Over Trump (http://www.cbsnews.com/news/the-great-divide-the-media-war-over-trump/)
Ted Koppel interviewed Yochai Benkler (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ybenkler) , Berkman Klein faculty co-director and Berkman Professor of Entrepreneurial and Legal Studies, for CBS News. Benkler discusses a recent study on political news ecosystems (https://www.cjr.org/analysis/breitbart-media-trump-harvard-study.php) , which analyzes hyperlinking patterns, social media sharing patterns, and topic and language patterns to show how news coverage takes shape on the Internet.
