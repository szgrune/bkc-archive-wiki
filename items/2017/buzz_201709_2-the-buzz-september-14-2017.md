New Research
https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/09/berklett
Cross-border data access reform may be on the legislative agenda in late 2017, with recent House and Senate judiciary committee hearings revisiting the topic. This brief primer, (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/09/berklett) written by Tiffany Lin (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/tlin) and Mailyn Fidler (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/mfidler) , explains how how cross-border data access requests currently work, options for reform, and major challenges to reform ahead and provides a high-level background review of the debate as it currently stands, particularly focusing on the DOJ’s 2016 proposal for reform.

Don't waste your breath complaining to Equifax about data breach (http://www.cnn.com/2017/09/11/opinions/dont-complain-to-equifax-demand-government-act-opinion-schneier/index.html)
In this piece for CNN (http://www.cnn.com/2017/09/11/opinions/dont-complain-to-equifax-demand-government-act-opinion-schneier/index.html) , BKC fellow Bruce Schneier (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/bschneier) argues that government regulation is the only way to truly protect ourselves from data breaches like the one recently announced by Equifax. "By all means, take the recommended steps to protect yourself from identity theft in the wake of Equifax's data breach, but recognize that these steps are only effective on the margins, and that most data security is out of your hands," he writes.

Artificial Intelligence's Fair Use Crisis (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3032076)
"In the United States, the copyright law mechanism most likely to facilitate machine learning’s uses of protected data is the fair use doctrine. However, current fair use doctrine threatens either to derail the progress of machine learning or to disenfranchise the human creators whose work makes it possible," writes affiliate Ben Sobel (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/bsobel) in this paper (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3032076##) for the Columbia Journal of the Law & the Arts. (Sobel discusses his research in this interview (https://www.ip-watch.org/2017/08/23/dilemma-fair-use-expressive-machine-learning-interview-ben-sobel/) with Intellectual Property Watch.)
