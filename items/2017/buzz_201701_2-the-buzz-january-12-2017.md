The humans working behind the AI curtain.
"The truth is, AI is as “fully-automated” as the Great and Powerful Oz was in that famous scene from the classic film, where Dorothy and friends realize that the great wizard is simply a man manically pulling levers from behind a curtain," writes Mary L. Gray (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/mgray) and Siddharth Suri in this piece (https://hbr.org/2017/01/the-humans-working-behind-the-ai-curtain) for the Harvard Business Review about the humans who cover for AI "when it can't do something on its own."

The hole in the digital economy.
"If the next president intends to improve American infrastructure and expand economic opportunities, there’s no better place to start than with the millions of people who still lack broadband access and computer skills," writes David Talbot (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/98856) in this piece (https://www.technologyreview.com/s/603083/the-hole-in-the-digital-economy/) for the MIT Tech Review.
https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/malper
Giving voice.
"Mobile technologies are often hailed as a way to “give voice to the voiceless.” Behind the praise, though, are beliefs about technology as a gateway to opportunity and voice as a metaphor for agency and self-representation. In her new book Giving Voice (https://mitpress.mit.edu/books/giving-voice) , Meryl Alper (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/malper) explores these assumptions.... and finds that despite claims to empowerment, the hardware and software are still subject to disempowering structural inequalities."

New publications
The Transparency Toolkit: Reporting Guide & Template
https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99763
This new resource provides a practical starting point for companies beginning or improving their transparency reporting of US Government requests for user data.

We're pleased to announce (https://cyber.harvard.edu/node/99763) the publication of this guide and template, which builds off our survey of best practices (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2016/transparency_memos) , released last year. Produced in collaboration with theOpen Technology Institute (https://www.newamerica.org/oti/) , this guide aims to provide companies with the tools they need to publish transparency reports that are more consistent, easier to understand, and more effective.
