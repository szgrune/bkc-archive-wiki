New Briefing: Recommendations for Approaching the Topic of Zero Rating
https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/10/zerorating

Zero Rating & Internet Adoption (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/10/zerorating)

The Berkman Klein Center gathered a diverse group of stakeholders from academia, the media, the government sector, industry, and the open software community to discuss the use of zero rating as a means to improve Internet adoption in the developing world and how and when it could be an effective tool, if at all. This paper (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/10/zerorating) captures the resulting dialogue and recommendations.
Read More Here (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/10/zerorating)
