Voices from inside and outside of the US Intelligence community have been discussing the evidence of hacking of Democratic National Committee emails during the 2016 election. In this review of technical claims for The Nation (https://www.thenation.com/article/a-leak-or-a-hack-a-forum-on-the-vips-memo/)  Berkman Klein affiliate Nathan Freitas (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/nfreitas) lays out "several scenarios in which the DNC could have been hacked from the outside, although he does not rule out a leak," as a culprit for the release.
