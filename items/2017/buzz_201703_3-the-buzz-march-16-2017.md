How political polarization affects the left and right (http://news.wgbh.org/2017/03/15/politics-government/major-new-study-shows-political-polarization-mainly-right-wing)
A new report (http://news.wgbh.org/2017/03/15/politics-government/major-new-study-shows-political-polarization-mainly-right-wing) from Media Cloud examines what news sources are shared amongst liberals and conservatives. By analyzing over 1.25 million articles, Yochai Benkler (http://hls.harvard.edu/faculty/directory/10071/Benkler) , Robert Faris (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/rfaris) , Hal Roberts (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/hroberts) , and Ethan Zuckerman (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ezuckerman) found that there is asymmetrical polarization in conservatives' news consumption.
