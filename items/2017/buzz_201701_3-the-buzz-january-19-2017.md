Who's killing the towns of western Massachusetts?
"What new American generations will stay in a place that is essentially unconnected to the world? What new businesses and ways of making a living will emerge there? None and none," writes Susan Crawford (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/scrawford) in this piece (https://backchannel.com/who-is-killing-the-towns-of-western-massachusetts-5dcf6bcd770#.nzr8uyoh9) for Backchannel about the efforts, roadblocks, and potential opportunities for high-speed Internet access in the rural western part of the state.

Do workplace wellness programs pose a risk to your health data?
"Without proper oversight, the health data collection that’s part of workplace wellness programs may put employees’ privacy, and potentially even employment, at risk," writes Ifeoma Ajunwa (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/iajunwa) in this piece (https://hbr.org/2017/01/workplace-wellness-programs-could-be-putting-your-health-data-at-risk) for the Harvard Business Review that outlines some of the issues of concern and offers some ideas for mitigating risk.
Watch: The challenges and opportunities of AI
Our new series of short videos highlights some of the people from the BKC and Harvard communities working on issues related to artificial intelligence.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jf5o1Dlv28wJonathan Zittrain, faculty director of the Berkman Klein Center and Professor at Harvard Law School, considers the role that both regulators and oversight groups might play as AI becomes increasingly integrated into society.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ywQj8K_OnZMKate Darling of the MIT Media Lab and the Berkman Klein Center considers how interacting with artificially intelligent human agents can impact human behavioral development.
