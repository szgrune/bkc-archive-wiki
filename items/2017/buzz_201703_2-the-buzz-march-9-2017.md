Shaping the 2016 election media agenda (http://www.cjr.org/analysis/breitbart-media-trump-harvard-study.php)
In this piece (http://www.cjr.org/analysis/breitbart-media-trump-harvard-study.php) for the Columbia Journalism Review, Yochai Benkler (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ybenkler) , Robert Faris (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/rfaris) , Hal Roberts (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/hroberts) , and Ethan Zuckerman (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ezuckerman) explain the results of their study that reviewed 1.25 million stories that were published online during the election cycle. Their findings reveal emerging patterns that shape the media's agenda and polarization in the press.

http://blogs.cfr.org/cyber/2017/03/07/south-africa-introduces-revised-cybercrime-legislation-acknowledging-criticism/ South Africa introduces new cybercrime legislation (http://blogs.cfr.org/cyber/2017/03/07/south-africa-introduces-revised-cybercrime-legislation-acknowledging-criticism/)
In this article (http://blogs.cfr.org/cyber/2017/03/07/south-africa-introduces-revised-cybercrime-legislation-acknowledging-criticism/) for the Council on Foreign Relations, Mailyn Fidler (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/mfidler) discusses South Africa's new cybercrime legislation — a direct result of widely-opposed legislation from 2015. While the government has reshaped the bill, issues remain with its content.

How the new immigration agenda affects student privacy (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/student-privacy-equity-and-digital-literacy-newsletter-deeper-dive-installment-one-edea800a3a36#.hap0y9g76)
How can schools protect students' privacy under new immigration laws? In this Medium post (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/student-privacy-equity-and-digital-literacy-newsletter-deeper-dive-installment-one-edea800a3a36#.8oeyqq87p) , Leah Plunkett (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/lplunkett) examines the issue through an analysis of constitutional protection for students, and suggests ways that decision-makers can strengthen student digital data privacy protections.
https://medium.com/misinfocon/building-technical-standards-for-credibility-59ef9ee4ab73#.3wuzj133m
Building technical standards for credibility (https://medium.com/misinfocon/building-technical-standards-for-credibility-59ef9ee4ab73#.3wuzj133m)
In this Medium post (https://medium.com/misinfocon/building-technical-standards-for-credibility-59ef9ee4ab73#.3wuzj133m) , An Xiao Mina (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/axmina) examines standards of credibility for online content? Labeling a piece of journalism as credible isn't enough, she argues. Instead, we need a system that will better define and identify credible content.

How (not) to refute a lie (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/how-to-refute-a-lie-bb315d3b93fc#.79dwwjpra)
2017 is what Judith Donath (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jdonath) calls "The Year of Deception."
