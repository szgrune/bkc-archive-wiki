Hate vs. Debate on Facebook (http://www.hyperorg.com/blogger/2017/09/19/bkc-hate-speech-on-facebook/)
Monika Bickert (https://www.facebook.com/mbickert1) , Facebook’s Head of Global Policy Management joined Jonathan Zittrain (http://cyber.law.harvard.edu/people/jzittrain)  for a conversation on how the world's largest social network decides what posts stay up and what gets taken down. In his liveblog (http://www.hyperorg.com/blogger/2017/09/19/bkc-hate-speech-on-facebook/)  of the talk, David Weinberger (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/dweinberger)  writes that questions of how to deal with hate speech are "literally impossible problems... to do with covering their butt at the expense of protecting the vulnerable. E.g., they treat all protected classes equally, even though some of us — er, me — are in top o’ the heap, privileged classes. The result is that FB applies a rule equally to all, which can bring inequitable results."
Read More (http://www.hyperorg.com/blogger/2017/09/19/bkc-hate-speech-on-facebook/)
https://medium.com/digital-sociology-at-vcu/artificial-intelligence-gaydar-7eb9fd812120
Artificial Intelligence “Gaydar” (https://medium.com/digital-sociology-at-vcu/artificial-intelligence-gaydar-7eb9fd812120)
The debate around some recent AI research highlights the challenges of translating complex academic studies into the broader public sphere. Sociologist Tressie McMillan Cottom (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/tcottom)  says of some media interpretation of a Stanford study on algorithmic detection of sexual orientation, "like most debates in social science the guiding concern should be does the practical significance of a study outweigh the risks to individuals and actors?"
Read More (https://medium.com/digital-sociology-at-vcu/artificial-intelligence-gaydar-7eb9fd812120)
https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/about-faceid-8691c741780a
About FaceID (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/about-faceid-8691c741780a)
Apple's newly unveiled facial identification system for unlocking iPhones raises a lot of security and privacy questions, which Jason Griffey (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/jgriffey)  highlights in this essay for the Berkman Klein Medium (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/about-faceid-8691c741780a) . But, he says, "FaceID is no less secure than the systems we have now on Apple devices... the knee-jerk response that somehow Apple is building a massive catalog of faces is neither true, nor possible given the architectures of their hardware and software."
Read More (https://medium.com/berkman-klein-center/about-faceid-8691c741780a)

Campaign ’16: How coverage rerouted (https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2017/09/conservative-media-dominated-coverage-of-2016-campaign-report-finds/)
The Harvard Gazette (https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2017/09/conservative-media-dominated-coverage-of-2016-campaign-report-finds/) covers Berkman Klein's recent research (https://cyber.harvard.edu/publications/2017/08/mediacloud) on media and the 2016 election. One lesson to take away from the proliferation of disinformation in 2016 is that "editors and reporters must recognize how political campaigns actively create click bait for journalists."

https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2017/sep/19/white-supremacist-statues-must-fall-scientists
It's time to take the 'great' white men of science off their pedestals (https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2017/sep/19/white-supremacist-statues-must-fall-scientists)
The national conversation in the US over how Confederate figures are publicly commemorated should inspire a similar conversation around how we recognize figures in the sciences, Berkman Klein affiliate Yarden Katz (https://cyber.harvard.edu/people/ykatz)  argues in The Guardian (https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2017/sep/19/white-supremacist-statues-must-fall-scientists) . "Racist and sexist ideologies can and do make their way into scientific theories," says Katz. "What is needed is an honest re-examination of science’s history and politics."
Read More (https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2017/sep/19/white-supremacist-statues-must-fall-scientists)
