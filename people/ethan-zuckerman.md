# Ethan Zuckerman

A foundational BKC figure: co-founder of [[global-voices]] (2005) and longtime Center
affiliate, now director of the Initiative for Digital Public Infrastructure at UMass
Amherst. He is one of the few names that recurs across the **entire** span of the
archive — from BKC's own earliest publications (2003) through the present — and his
throughline is remarkably consistent: a search for **civic, public-interest
alternatives to commercial media power**. BKC's publications backfill shows the
earlier form of that search: "Global Attention Profiles" (2003), *Media Re:public*
(2008, on the future of digital journalism), and a decade of
[[internet-filtering-and-openness|OpenNet Initiative]] circumvention-tool research
alongside [[john-palfrey|John Palfrey]] and [[hal-roberts|Hal Roberts]]. The later
items map the project's present form directly ("Can Public Infrastructure Fix Social
Media?", "The Good Web," "Beyond Facebook Logic," a social-network taxonomy), while his
journalism work anchors [[journalism-and-the-news-business]]. A natural hub page
linking [[platform-power-and-antitrust]], [[internet-filtering-and-openness]], and
[[digital-colonialism-and-global-south]].
