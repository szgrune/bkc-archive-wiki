# Lawrence Lessig

A BKC co-founder, Harvard Law professor, and founder of **Creative Commons** — the
intellectual origin of the archive's [[copyright-and-open-access|copyright & open-access]]
thread, now documented back to its actual source texts via the BKC publications
backfill: *Code and Other Laws of Cyberspace* (1999, "code is law"), the 1999 essay
collection *Open Code / Open Content / Open Law* with [[jonathan-zittrain|Jonathan
Zittrain]] and Charles Nesson, *The Future of Ideas* (2001), *Free Culture* (2004), and
*Code: Version 2.0* (2006) — his "free culture" argument that overbroad copyright
chills creativity shaped the Center's earliest agenda and recurs throughout the Buzz
years. The archive then tracks his later turns: a continued critique of copyright
maximalism (opposing Congress's copyright-term extension) and, most recently, the
argument that **AI and TikTok are producing a free-speech crisis** — connecting the old
IP fights to today's [[content-moderation-and-speech]] and [[ai-governance-and-regulation]]
debates.
