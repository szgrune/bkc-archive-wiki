# Hal Roberts

The technical core of the [[internet-filtering-and-openness|OpenNet Initiative's]]
circumvention-measurement work (17 publications, 2009–2020) — the researcher who
actually built and ran the annual circumvention-tool usage/evaluation studies and DDoS
attack reports that [[robert-faris|Robert Faris]], [[john-palfrey|John Palfrey]], and
Ethan Zuckerman's OpenNet Initiative depended on. Later co-author, with Yochai Benkler
and Faris, of *Network Propaganda* and its follow-on 2020 election-disinformation
studies — the same media-cloud measurement approach turned from circumvention tools
onto the US media ecosystem itself.
