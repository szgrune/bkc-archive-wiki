# William Fisher

WilmerHale Professor of Intellectual Property Law at Harvard and founder of BKC's
**CopyrightX** course — the archive's earliest author by publication date: "American
Legal Realism" (1993, with Morton Horwitz) is the single oldest item in the entire
corpus. His BKC work centers on **copyright's proper scope in the digital age**:
*Promises to Keep* argues for compensating creators without criminalizing file-sharing,
and he co-signs the amicus brief in *MGM v. Grokster* (2005) alongside
[[jonathan-zittrain|Jonathan Zittrain]] and [[john-palfrey|John Palfrey]] — one of the
foundational P2P-liability cases underlying [[copyright-and-open-access]].
