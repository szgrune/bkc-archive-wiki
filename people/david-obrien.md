# David O'Brien

The technical-privacy researcher behind BKC's **differential-privacy** research
program (10 publications, 2012–2018), recurring alongside [[urs-gasser|Urs Gasser]],
Alexandra Wood, and Harvard statistician Salil Vadhan on the "Integrating Approaches
to Privacy Across the Research Lifecycle" series and its culmination, *Differential
Privacy* (2018) — technical work on formal privacy guarantees for research data, cited
alongside [[bruce-schneier|Bruce Schneier]] and [[jonathan-zittrain|Jonathan
Zittrain]] as a co-author of "Don't Panic," the encryption "Going Dark" debate report.
