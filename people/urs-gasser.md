# Urs Gasser

BKC's former **executive director** and, by a wide margin, the single most-published
author in the Center's own publications archive (86 items, 2003–2023) — a career arc
that traces the Center's own intellectual history. He begins in **digital-copyright
doctrine** (iTunes/DRM case studies, the EU Copyright Directive) and **interoperability**
(digital identity, cloud computing, "breaking down digital barriers"), co-authors
*Born Digital* with [[john-palfrey|John Palfrey]] in 2008, then spends the 2010s
building out the **Youth and Media** research program (youth privacy, student data,
digital literacy) with [[sandra-cortesi|Sandra Cortesi]] — before turning, from 2016,
to **institutional AI and data governance**: co-author of "Don't Panic" (the encryption
"Going Dark" debate) and, in 2017, "A Layered Model for AI Governance," an early
framework that anticipates the archive's entire 2025 [[ai-governance-and-regulation]]
thread by eight years. Few figures better embody this wiki's "link toward items"
architecture — nearly every BKC-authored publication topic eventually runs through him.
