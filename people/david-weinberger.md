# David Weinberger

A longtime BKC fellow, technologist and philosopher of the web — co-author of *The
Cluetrain Manifesto* (2001, with Doc Searls and Rick Levine) and author of the trilogy
*Small Pieces Loosely Joined* (2003), *Tagging and Why It Matters* (2005), and
*Everything is Miscellaneous* (2007), plus later *Too Big to Know* and *Everyday
Chaos*. One of the most frequently featured voices of the Buzz era — his "From David"
columns recur for years — and a bridge from the early-web optimism to the
machine-learning present. His preoccupation is **epistemology**: how networks, and now
AI, change the shape of knowledge, order, and prediction — a thread traceable from
*Small Pieces*'s argument that the web's looseness is a feature, through *Everything is
Miscellaneous*'s case against single taxonomies, to the later machine-learning items
("confidence everywhere," *Everyday Chaos*, "keeping marketing human"), making him a
useful entry point to [[ai-safety-and-agents]] and [[ai-copyright-and-knowledge]].
