# Sandra Cortesi

Director of BKC's **Youth and Media** research program (22 publications, 2012–2019) —
[[urs-gasser|Urs Gasser]]'s closest recurring co-author and the researcher who carried
the *[[digital-natives-and-youth-privacy|Digital Natives]]* project from its
literature-review phase into original global fieldwork. Her arc moves outward twice:
first from privacy attitudes (with Pew's Mary Madden and Amanda Lenhart) to
information-quality and credibility, then from a US-centered research base to the
explicitly global *Digitally Connected* and Latin America studies. Her final items in
this corpus (2019) turn to **youth and AI** — the direct ancestor of the archive's
2025 AI-and-youth concerns.
