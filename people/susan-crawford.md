# Susan Crawford

Telecom-law scholar and civic-technology advocate whose BKC publications (9 items,
2004–2017) span two connected concerns: **internet governance as peer production**
(with [[john-palfrey|John Palfrey]] and David Johnson, 2004) and, a decade later,
**municipal broadband and data-smart city government** — co-author of the
[[net-neutrality-and-internet-access|Municipal Fiber]] case studies (WiredWest,
Leverett, DC/Seattle/SF) and *The Responsive City*, on using civic data to make local
government more accountable.
