# danah boyd

Founder and president of [[data-society|Data & Society]], partner researcher at
Microsoft Research, and a longtime BKC affiliate. She is the archive's defining voice on
**youth, social media, and privacy** — her 2014 book *It's Complicated* and the
"Privacy at the Margins" agenda (the basis of the [[2014-privacy-at-the-margins|first
event page]]) reframe privacy as unevenly distributed and structurally shaped rather
than a uniform individual right. BKC's own publications backfill traces this back to
2007's "Why Youth Heart Social Network Sites" and a run of youth-safety work
co-authored with [[john-palfrey|John Palfrey]] and Urs Gasser — the Internet Safety
Technical Task Force's *Enhancing Child Safety and Online Technologies* (2009), COPPA
misinterpretation research (2010), and youth-bullying literature (2012) — directly
anticipating the later [[digital-natives-and-youth-privacy]] research program. The Buzz
years feature her steadily ("corrupt personalization," teens and "selling out," new
survey findings on teen privacy), and through Data & Society she remains central to
[[misinformation-and-elections]] and [[privacy-and-surveillance]].
