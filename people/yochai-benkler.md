# Yochai Benkler

Harvard Law professor and one of the field's most cited theorists, whose *The Wealth
of Networks* (2006) — arguing that **commons-based peer production** can out-compete
proprietary, market-incentive models for producing information and culture — supplies
[[copyright-and-open-access]]'s intellectual backbone alongside
[[lawrence-lessig|Lawrence Lessig]]'s work. *The Penguin and the Leviathan* (2011)
extends the cooperation argument beyond the internet. From 2013 he becomes a
recurring co-author with [[robert-faris|Robert Faris]] and [[hal-roberts|Hal Roberts]]
on the empirical media-ecosystem side of [[internet-democracy-and-networked-protest]] —
mapping the SOPA-PIPA debate, then *Network Propaganda* (2018) and its 2020
election-disinformation studies.
