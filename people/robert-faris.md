# Robert Faris

Long-time BKC research director and the connective figure across the Center's
**Internet & Democracy** and **OpenNet Initiative** research, appearing in 32
publications (2008–2020) — nearly always as co-author rather than sole author, which
is itself the signal: he is the throughline linking [[bruce-etling|Bruce Etling]],
John Kelly, [[hal-roberts|Hal Roberts]], and [[yochai-benkler|Yochai Benkler]] across
two decades of the same research program (see [[internet-democracy-and-networked-protest]]
for the full arc). Co-leads the **Internet Monitor** annual report, then the pivot from
blogosphere-mapping optimism to the media-ecosystem critique of *Network Propaganda*
and its 2020 election-disinformation studies.
