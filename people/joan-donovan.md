# Joan Donovan

Disinformation researcher and one of the archive's recurring names on **media
manipulation and online extremism** (mentioned corpus-wide). In this batch she
appears via a hybrid BKC book talk for *Meme Wars: The Untold Story of the Online
Battles Upending Democracy in America*, co-authored with Brian Friedberg, paired with
Brandi Collins-Dexter discussing her own book *Black Skinhead: Reflections on
Blackness and Our Political Future* — a joint session pairing two accounts of how
online subcultures and platform dynamics shape real-world political and racial
narratives.
