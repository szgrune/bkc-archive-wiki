# Cory Doctorow

Author, blogger, and digital-rights activist, and one of the archive's most-mentioned
outside voices (corpus-wide) on **corporate concentration in creative markets**. In
this batch he appears via a BKC talk with Rebecca Giblin on their co-authored book
*Chokepoint Capitalism*, which argues that Big Tech and Big Content have engineered
"chokepoints" — monopoly and monopsony power positioned between creators and
audiences — across book publishing, Hollywood, games, news, and music, and proposes
structural fixes to let creators and audiences capture more of the value they
generate.
