# Bruce Etling

A founding researcher of BKC's [[internet-democracy-and-networked-protest|Internet &
Democracy Project]] (14 publications, 2008–2020), first author on its signature
blogosphere-mapping studies — Iran's Persian blogosphere, the Arabic blogosphere, the
Russian ("RuNet") blogosphere — before following [[robert-faris|Robert Faris]] and
[[yochai-benkler|Yochai Benkler]] into the later media-ecosystem critique
(*Partisanship, Propaganda, and Disinformation*, the 2020 election-disinformation
studies). "Russia, Ukraine, and the West" (2014) applies the same network-mapping
method to Euromaidan and its aftermath.
