# Leah Plunkett

Meyer Research Lecturer on Law at Harvard Law School and a BKC Faculty Associate
working with the Center's Youth and Media team — the archive's recurring voice on
**children, teenagers, and digital regulation** (mentioned corpus-wide alongside this
batch's two appearances). She testifies on kid-influencer labor-law reform (compensation
and privacy rights for the "TikTok kids" who generate platform revenue but have no
seat at the legislating table) and, separately, on how youth data-governance and
"altruistic data sharing" regimes should account for young people as both data
subjects and future data-rights advocates.
