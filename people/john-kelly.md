# John Kelly

The network-mapping methodologist behind BKC's blogosphere studies (12 publications,
2008–2018) — founder of Morningside Analytics, whose social-network-mapping
techniques underpin the [[internet-democracy-and-networked-protest|Internet &
Democracy Project's]] signature visualizations of the Iranian, Arabic, and Russian
blogospheres alongside [[bruce-etling|Bruce Etling]] and [[robert-faris|Robert
Faris]]. Later applies the same hashtag- and network-analysis method to Saudi Twitter
("Openness and Restraint"), Iran's 2016 elections ("#IranVotes"), and — with Helmi
Noman — internet censorship during the Yemen war.
