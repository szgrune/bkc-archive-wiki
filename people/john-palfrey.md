# John Palfrey

A BKC co-founder and its former executive director, later Head of School at Phillips
Academy Andover and, since 2019, president of the **MacArthur Foundation** — and, by
publication count, BKC's second-most-prolific author (53 items, 2004–2019). In the Buzz
years he is the archive's leading voice on **libraries and access to knowledge** — the
recurring champion of the **Digital Public Library of America (DPLA)**, whose launch
and anniversaries he narrates. BKC's own publications archive reveals the fuller
research program underneath: co-author with [[jonathan-zittrain|Jonathan Zittrain]],
Ron Deibert, and Rafal Rohozinski of the
[[internet-filtering-and-openness|Access Denied/Controlled/Contested]] internet-filtering
trilogy (2008–2011); co-author with Urs Gasser of *Born Digital* (2008), the book that
launched [[digital-natives-and-youth-privacy|BKC's Digital Natives research program]],
and of *Interop* (2012), on the promise and perils of interconnected systems. His move
to MacArthur (a major tech-policy funder) is itself a node connecting BKC's alumni
network to the field's philanthropy. Foundational to [[ai-copyright-and-knowledge|
knowledge institutions]], [[internet-filtering-and-openness]],
[[digital-natives-and-youth-privacy]], and [[berkman-klein-center]].
