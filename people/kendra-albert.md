# Kendra Albert

A [[cyberlaw-clinic|Cyberlaw Clinic]] instructor at Harvard Law School whose work
spans reproductive-justice law, trans rights, and internet law — a recurring name
across the archive (dozens of mentions corpus-wide), reflecting the Clinic's steady
public programming on the legal fallout of *Dobbs* and the Comstock Act's revival.
In this batch, Albert is a featured speaker on "The Afterlife of Anthony Comstock,"
connecting the 1873 obscenity law's restrictions on contraception, sex work, and
queer/trans expression to its contemporary resurgence as a tool against abortion
access and bodily autonomy.
