# The Future of the Internet Summit — Applied Social Media Lab Launch

A daylong October 2023 summit at BKC, convened to launch the Center's new **Applied
Social Media Lab (ASML)**, spun up as the Institute for Rebooting Social Media's
three-year "pop-up" mandate wound down. Former President **Barack Obama** — "the
first 'digital' president" — joined academics and technologists for a wide-ranging
look at how social media, generative AI, and other disruptive technologies both erode
and support healthy democratic societies. Five YouTube recordings, uploaded across a
two-week window (Oct 19 – Oct 31, 2023), document distinct sessions from the same
summit:

- The Summit's own framing video introduces the day and ASML's launch.
- **[[jonathan-zittrain|Jonathan Zittrain]]** leads a "Future of the Internet" panel
  with Obama senior advisor Jason Goldman, former Twitter Head of Trust & Safety
  Yoel Roth, Block Party CEO Tracy Chou, and Data Nutrition Project co-founder Kasia
  Chmielinski — industry veterans reflecting on platform misinformation, the social
  media upheaval, and generative AI's "meteoric rise."
- DJ Patil (former White House Chief Data Scientist) leads a discussion with ASML's
  founding faculty — James Mickens, Latanya Sweeney, and **[[lawrence-lessig|Lawrence
  Lessig]]** — on the Lab's inaugural sprints: generative AI and political
  polarization among them.
- Nabiha Syed (CEO, The Markup) discusses the governance and media landscape ahead of
  2024 with Martha Minow and Nancy Gibbs (Shorenstein Center).
- Zittrain closes the week in conversation with journalist Kashmir Hill on her book
  *Your Face Belongs to Us*, about Clearview AI's facial-recognition tool.
