# Enabling Accountable Technical Oversight of Generative AI — RSM Fireside Series

A three-part 2023 fireside series, convened by the Institute for Rebooting Social
Media (RSM), on how to hold generative AI systems accountable without waiting on
regulation alone. The opening speaker of the third and closing conversation frames
the arc explicitly: the first fireside asked whether generative AI is introducing
genuinely **new harms** or "old harms in new outfits" (with Reva Schwartz of NIST,
whose AI Risk Management Framework work is the throughline of that session's own
description); the second, with **Bruce Schneier**, weighed **balancing transparency
and security** in open research on generative AI; the third brought investigative
journalist **Julia Angwin** and CrowdTangle founder **Brandon Silverman** together,
moderated by **Dr. Rumman Chowdhury** (BKC Responsible AI Fellow), to ask how
journalism, civil-society audits, and data access — not just legislation — can supply
accountable oversight of generative AI "across a spectrum."

Note on sources: only the third session's own YouTube description names its
participants and moderator; the identification of the first two sessions' speakers
(Schwartz, Schneier) comes from the third video's own spoken recap of the series, not
from those two videos' descriptions. The three videos' `date_published` values are
also out of chronological order with the internal "first/second/third" narration
(the third-and-final session is dated earliest, 2023-07-14, while the first and
second are both dated 2023-11-11) — likely a YouTube upload-date artifact rather than
the true event order.
