# Cyberlaw Clinic

BKC and Harvard Law School's in-house legal clinic (clinic.cyber.harvard.edu),
representing clients and hosting public programming on technology-law issues,
frequently at the leading edge of digital-rights litigation. Recurs across the
archive both as a **litigant/representative** (see [[berkman-klein-center]]'s 2025
items on EPIC and election-deepfake cases) and, in this batch's 2022–2023 YouTube
videos, as a **convener** of public panels on reproductive-justice and data-privacy
law, staffed by its instructors: [[kendra-albert|Kendra Albert]], Jessica Fjeld, and
Alejandra Caraballo. Its **Lumen** project (lumen.database.org, formerly Chilling
Effects) — a database of platform notice-and-takedown requests — is a recurring
reference point in [[content-moderation-and-speech]] and inspired the EU Digital
Services Act's own transparency database.
