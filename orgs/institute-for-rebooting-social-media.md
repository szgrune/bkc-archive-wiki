# Institute for Rebooting Social Media (RSM)

A three-year "pop-up" research initiative housed at the [[berkman-klein-center]]
(2021–2023), convened to accelerate progress on social media's most urgent problems —
misinformation, privacy breaches, harassment, and content governance — by bringing
together industry, government, civil society, and academic participants for
time-bound, focused collaboration. RSM's own recurring **Speaker Series** supplies
several of the archive's 2023 YouTube items, each closing with the same
self-description and a pointer to its successor project, the
[[applied-social-media-lab|Applied Social Media Lab]]. An **RSM Assembly Fellowship**
funded early-career technologists working inside the initiative.
