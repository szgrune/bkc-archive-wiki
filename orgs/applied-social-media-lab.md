# Applied Social Media Lab (ASML)

BKC's successor initiative to the [[institute-for-rebooting-social-media|Institute
for Rebooting Social Media]], launched October 2023 at
[[2023-future-of-the-internet-summit|The Future of the Internet Summit]]. ASML gives
"industry-trained technologists the freedom to build social media solutions that
center the public interest" — a builder-oriented complement to RSM's convening model.
Its founding faculty (James Mickens, Latanya Sweeney, [[lawrence-lessig|Lawrence
Lessig]]) and its first projects (spanning generative AI and political polarization)
were introduced at the launch summit.
