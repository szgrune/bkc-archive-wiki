# 2003

**The OpenNet Initiative's origin point.** [[jonathan-zittrain|Jonathan Zittrain]]'s
"Empirical Analysis of Internet Filtering in China" and "Internet Points of Control"
are the earliest work in what becomes, five years later, the
[[internet-filtering-and-openness|Access Denied/Controlled/Contested]] trilogy — the
first attempt to actually measure state internet censorship rather than just theorize
about it. The same year, [[david-weinberger|David Weinberger]] publishes *Small Pieces
Loosely Joined* and [[ethan-zuckerman|Ethan Zuckerman]] begins his "Global Attention
Profiles" work, while "Andean Readiness for the Networked World" continues BKC's early
global-development research strand.
