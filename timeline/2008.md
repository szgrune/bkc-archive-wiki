# 2008

A US **election year**, read through the lens of a young field asking what networked
technology does to democracy. The Center's project portfolio is at its most expansive.

## The year's threads
- **The internet and democracy.** The
  [[internet-democracy-and-networked-protest|Internet & Democracy Project]] and
  **Publius** frame the election-year question of how the network reshapes political
  participation, publishing
  [[pub_2008-mapping-irans-online-public-mapping-irans-online-public-politics-and-culture-in-the-pers|Mapping
  Iran's Online Public]] — the deep root of [[ai-and-democracy|today's democracy thread]].
- **Youth comes onto the agenda.** [[john-palfrey|John Palfrey]] and Urs Gasser's
  [[pub_2008-born-digital-born-digital-understanding-the-first-generation-of-digital-n|*Born
  Digital*]] launches the **Digital Natives** program, opening
  [[digital-natives-and-youth-privacy|BKC's long line of youth-and-media work]].
- **Global expression.** [[global-voices|Global Voices]], the
  [[internet-filtering-and-openness|OpenNet Initiative]] (whose
  [[pub_2008-access-denied-access-denied-the-practice-and-policy-of-global-internet-fil|*Access
  Denied*]] appears this year), and **Iran** keep
  [[digital-colonialism-and-global-south|global internet freedom]] central.
- **Generativity arrives.** [[jonathan-zittrain]] emerges as a defining voice with
  *The Future of the Internet — And How to Stop It*.

## Notable voices
Doc Searls, [[david-weinberger]], [[john-palfrey]], Wendy Seltzer, Charles Nesson, and
[[jonathan-zittrain]]. See [[berkman-klein-center]].
