# 1998

[[lawrence-lessig|Lawrence Lessig]]'s "What Things Regulate Speech: CDA 2.0 vs.
Filtering" — an early draft of the argument, refined over the next decade into *Code
and Other Laws of Cyberspace*, that content filtering could regulate online speech as
effectively as the Communications Decency Act itself, just through different means
("architecture" as a regulator alongside law).
