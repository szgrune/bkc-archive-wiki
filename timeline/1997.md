# 1997

**BKC's own founding moment, on the page.** [[jonathan-zittrain|Jonathan Zittrain]]'s
"The Rise and Fall of Sysopdom" (an early meditation on who governs online forums,
years before "platform governance" was a phrase) and Charles Nesson's "Borders in
Cyberspace" appear the same year as "The Harvard Conference on the Internet and
Society" — very likely the founding conference from which the Berkman Center itself
grew (Nesson and [[jonathan-zittrain|Zittrain]] co-founded BKC in 1996–97).
