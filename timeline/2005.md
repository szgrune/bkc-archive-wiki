# 2005

The **OpenNet Initiative's first country studies** — China, Iran, Bahrain, Singapore,
Burma, Tunisia — establish the empirical filtering-measurement method that becomes the
[[internet-filtering-and-openness|Access Denied/Controlled/Contested]] trilogy from
2008. Copyright litigation continues with the *MGM v. Grokster* amicus brief
([[jonathan-zittrain|Zittrain]], [[william-fisher|Fisher]], [[john-palfrey|Palfrey]]) —
one of the foundational P2P-liability Supreme Court cases — alongside Wendy Seltzer's
"The Broadcast Flag" and Lewis Hyde's "Frames from the Framers," on the American
revolutionaries' own conception of intellectual property. [[david-weinberger|David
Weinberger]]'s "Tagging and Why It Matters" continues his epistemology-of-the-web
trilogy between *Small Pieces Loosely Joined* (2003) and *Everything is Miscellaneous*
(2007).
