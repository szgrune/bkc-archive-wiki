# 2001

Two founding texts of the "web as public conversation" strand: [[david-weinberger|David
Weinberger]] co-authors *The Cluetrain Manifesto* ("markets are conversations"), and
[[lawrence-lessig|Lawrence Lessig]] publishes *The Future of Ideas*, warning that
excessive control over the internet's core protocols and copyright regime would choke
off the innovation *Code and Other Laws of Cyberspace* (1999) had just celebrated.
