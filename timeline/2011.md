# 2011

The year of **networked protest and the copyright wars**. The Buzz tracks the Arab
Spring and Occupy in real time while the **SOPA/PIPA** fight mobilizes the open-internet
movement at home.

## The year's threads
- **Revolutions, online.** The Arab Spring (Tunisia, **#Amina**, Egypt) and **Occupy /
  #OWS** run through the year — [[content-moderation-and-speech|networked speech]] and
  [[digital-colonialism-and-global-south|global internet freedom]], with "internet kill
  switches" and the Jasmine Revolution's filtering, tracked in BKC's own
  [[pub_2011-access-contested-access-contested-security-identity-and-resistance-in-asian-c|*Access
  Contested*]] (closing the OpenNet Initiative trilogy) and
  [[pub_2011-international-bloggers-internet-control-full-survey-results-international-bloggers-and-internet-control-full-survey-resu|International
  Bloggers and Internet Control]].
- **The copyright uprising.** **SOPA/PIPA** and ACTA galvanize
  [[copyright-and-open-access|copyright and open-access]] activism (the end of
  Righthaven, too), the same year Yochai Benkler's
  [[pub_2011-penguin-and-the-leviathan-the-penguin-and-the-leviathan-how-cooperation-triumphs-over|*The
  Penguin and the Leviathan*]] makes the cooperation-over-self-interest case.
- **Libraries and knowledge.** The **Digital Public Library of America** push begins (see
  [[john-palfrey]]); **Media Cloud** and **Herdict** mature as research tools.
- **End of an era.** Steve Jobs's death prompts reflection on the PC's future
  ([[jonathan-zittrain]]'s generativity question).
