# 2013

**Snowden and the surveillance turn** — the year the
[[privacy-and-surveillance|NSA disclosures]] reframed the whole field — bookended by the
death of Aaron Swartz, the open-access movement's martyr.

## The year's threads
- **After Snowden.** "Online security in the face of NSA surveillance" and urging tech
  companies to "push back against the NSA" — the start of [[cybersecurity-and-encryption]]
  as a mass-political concern.
- **Open access, won and mourned.** A new **White House open-access mandate** and
  [[william-fisher|William Fisher]]'s **CopyrightX** launch; the community mourns
  **Aaron Swartz**. See [[copyright-and-open-access]].
- **Knowledge institutions.** The **Digital Public Library of America** launches
  ([[john-palfrey]]).
- **Teens, privacy, and civics.** New survey work on
  [[pub_2013-teens-and-technology-teens-and-technology-2013|teens and privacy]]
  ([[danah-boyd]], [[sandra-cortesi|Sandra Cortesi]]); "generativity and originality"
  ([[jonathan-zittrain]]); the
  [[internet-democracy-and-networked-protest|Internet & Democracy Project]] maps the
  [[pub_2013-social-mobilization-and-the-networked-public-sphere-social-mobilization-and-the-networked-public-sphere-mapping|SOPA-PIPA
  debate]].
