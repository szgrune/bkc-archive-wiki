# 2016

**Not actually quiet — just quiet in the curated feed.** The Berkman Buzz newsletter
had ended and the TagTeam feed hadn't yet ramped up, leaving only 4 TagTeam/Buzz items
— but BKC's own publications backfill reveals 2016 as one of the Center's busiest
research years, with 25 items spanning encryption policy, the OpenNet Initiative, youth
privacy, and early hate-speech research.

## The year's threads
- **The "Going Dark" debate.** [[jonathan-zittrain|Jonathan Zittrain]], Urs Gasser, and
  [[bruce-schneier|Bruce Schneier]] co-author
  [[pub_2016-cybersecurity-dont-panic-dont-panic-making-progress-on-the-going-dark-debate|"Don't
  Panic"]] on encryption and law-enforcement access, the same month Schneier surveys
  [[pub_2016-encryption-survey-a-worldwide-survey-of-encryption-products|865 worldwide
  encryption products]] — [[cybersecurity-and-encryption]].
- **Internet control, mapped.** The
  [[internet-democracy-and-networked-protest|Internet & Democracy Project]]'s
  [[pub_2016-arab-nps-mobilization-for-change|*Mobilization for Change*]] and John
  Kelly's [[pub_2016-iranvotes-iranvotes|*#IranVotes*]] continue the
  [[internet-filtering-and-openness|OpenNet]] research line.
- **Youth privacy, institutionalized.** A run of research briefings on
  [[digital-natives-and-youth-privacy|student privacy, ed-tech, and big-data ethics]]
  (Urs Gasser, David O'Brien, Alexandra Wood, Leah Plunkett), plus a revised, expanded
  edition of *Born Digital*.
- **Early hate-speech research.** "Defining Hate Speech" and country studies from
  Brazil, Colombia, and India anticipate a decade of
  [[content-moderation-and-speech|content-moderation]] scholarship.
- **Municipal broadband.** David Talbot and [[susan-crawford|Susan Crawford]]'s
  [[pub_2016-wiredwest-case-study-wiredwest-a-cooperative-of-municipalities-forms-to-build-a-f|WiredWest]]
  case study — [[net-neutrality-and-internet-access]].
