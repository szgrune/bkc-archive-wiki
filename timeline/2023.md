# 2023

**The ChatGPT year.** Generative AI explodes and reorganizes every existing thread —
copyright, safety, journalism, labor — around a single technology.

  [[jonathan-zittrain]] declined to sign) — [[ai-safety-and-agents]],
  [[ai-governance-and-regulation]].
- **The copyright wars open.** Fair use "in the age of AI," the OpenAI suits, Hard Fork —
  [[copyright-and-open-access]].
- **Journalism's new threat.** "Can journalism resist a chatbot-fueled race to the

- **Section 230 at the Court.** *Gonzalez v. Google* and The Markup's "load-bearing wall"

- **BKC's own year, on video.** A 26-video YouTube layer newly synthesized this year:
  the RSM Speaker Series and its [[2023-rsm-genai-oversight-fireside-series|three-part

  Applied Social Media Lab, with Barack Obama; and panels on post-*Dobbs* digital
  privacy, the Comstock Act's revival, and kid-influencer labor law
  ([[privacy-and-surveillance]], [[ai-labor-and-economy]]).
