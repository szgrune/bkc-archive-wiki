# 1993

**Before the Center existed, its founding scholarship already had.** The archive's
single earliest item — [[william-fisher|William Fisher]] and Morton Horwitz's
"American Legal Realism" — predates BKC's own 1996 founding by three years, part of
Fisher's long-running work on the intellectual history of American law that would
later inform his IP scholarship and BKC's CopyrightX course.
