# 2002

BKC's earliest **global-development** work — Jamaica and a world "Readiness for the
Networked World" report — predates by two decades the archive's later
[[digital-colonialism-and-global-south]] emphasis, showing the Center's global lens was
present from early on, not a recent addition. Alongside it, early economic analysis of
copyright term length ("Incentives to Create Under a 'Lifetime-Plus-Years' Copyright
Duration") continues the [[copyright-and-open-access]] thread.
