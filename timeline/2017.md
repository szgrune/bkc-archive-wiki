# 2017

The **TagTeam feed comes online** and the **techlash begins**. After 2016, the dominant
question is what the platforms did to democracy — and the curated feed starts capturing
it in earnest.

## The year's threads
- **The 2016 reckoning.** Russian propaganda's reach, "fake news," and BKC's own
  [[pub_2017-08-mediacloud-partisanship-propaganda-and-disinformation-online-media-and|*Partisanship,
  Propaganda, and Disinformation*]] (Faris, Roberts, Etling, Zuckerman, Benkler) finding
  the real driver was an asymmetric right-wing media ecosystem, not just Russian
  bots — see [[misinformation-and-elections]] and
  [[internet-democracy-and-networked-protest]].
- **Algorithms enter the dock.** "How Facebook's oracular algorithm decides," algorithms
  in the justice system at Harvard Law — [[algorithmic-accountability]]; Urs Gasser's
  [[pub_node-100108-a-layered-model-for-ai-governance|"A Layered Model for AI
  Governance"]] anticipates the archive's 2025 [[ai-governance-and-regulation]] thread
  by eight years.
- **Net neutrality's last stand.** The FCC repeal fight, with the Cyberlaw Clinic
  litigating — [[net-neutrality-and-internet-access]].
- **Surveillance, local and global.** A Cambridge surveillance ordinance
  ([[privacy-and-surveillance]]); Citizen Lab's first spyware reports
  ([[cybersecurity-and-encryption]]).
